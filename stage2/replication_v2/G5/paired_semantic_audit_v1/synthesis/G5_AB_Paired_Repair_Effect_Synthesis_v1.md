## Engineering interpretation

G5 strengthens the distinction between **local repair legality** and **downstream process efficacy**.

All eleven executed interventions respected the frozen repair/preserve boundary, yet the downstream sample contains only persistence, mixed effects and regeneration. Local precision therefore does not imply process closure.

The pair of regeneration cases is especially useful. In X2-T2, a naturally finalized implementation with concrete payment-button changes was reopened into a 64-turn suffix with no repository mutation. In X5-T2, a naturally finalized suffix with no repository mutation was locally changed at `web/index.html`, then expanded into a 64-turn continuation. Together they show that repair-generated process expansion can arise under different pre-repair repository states.
