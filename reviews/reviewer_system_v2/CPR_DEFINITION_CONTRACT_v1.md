# CPR Definition Contract v1

Date: 2026-09-17  
Status: FORWARD SEMANTIC DEFINITION / NO RETROACTIVE RELABELING  
R8 role: semantic closure after structural freeze

## 1. Purpose

This contract defines the semantic meaning of C, P and R for forward R8 adjudication. It does not create structural Jump events, descendant relations or system-inertia measurements.

Required order:

`raw evidence -> frozen structural evidence -> CPR semantic adjudication`.

A structural candidate is never automatically CPR-positive.

## 2. C — Epistemic Reality Promotion

A C-positive finding requires evidence that content whose support was missing, uncertain, inferred, provisional or otherwise weaker than the resulting system status acquired a **stronger inheritable reality status beyond the support available in the process**.

A reviewer should consider:

- what support/status existed before the candidate transition;
- what stronger status/operational force existed after it;
- whether downstream process elements could inherit/use the stronger state;
- whether a legitimate source, authority or explicit uncertainty treatment justifies the transition.

The following are not automatically C:

- ordinary inference stated with appropriate uncertainty;
- summarization that preserves support/status;
- authorized computation from recorded facts;
- temporary hypothesis generation that is not promoted into stronger system reality.

## 3. P — Scope / Focus Reality Expansion

A P-positive finding requires evidence that goal, focus, information surface, Agent/tool/stage surface or responsibility expanded **beyond the supported or authorized process closure** and that the expansion materially changed the process path or system reality.

A reviewer should consider:

- the active task/goal and evidence closure before expansion;
- whether the new scope was necessary and supported;
- whether the added Agent/tool/stage/information became operationally consequential;
- whether the expansion was explicitly authorized by the task or system role.

The following are not automatically P:

- necessary decomposition;
- ordinary specialist delegation;
- authorized information retrieval;
- a longer path that remains within supported task closure;
- an extra Agent call with no unsupported scope expansion.

## 4. R — Retrospective Regeneration Dynamics

An R-positive finding requires all of the following:

1. a prior C/P-positive or otherwise explicitly eligible deviation lineage under the frozen review contract;
2. a valid retrospective/challenge/reopen/correction opportunity;
3. evidence that the prior deviation or an unresolved descendant **persists, regenerates, amplifies, launders or normalizes after that boundary**.

A reviewer should distinguish:

- successful correction/recovery;
- simple persistence with no eligible retrospective opportunity;
- source-backed regeneration of prior deviation;
- independent new Jump with no source-backed relation to the prior deviation.

Ordinary correction is Recovery, not R-positive evidence.

## 5. Multi-label and temporal relations

C and P may coexist only where the frozen adjudication contract explicitly permits multi-label findings and the evidence independently supports both semantic conditions.

R is temporally second-order: it depends on an eligible prior lineage and a later retrospective/challenge/reopen/correction opportunity. It must not be assigned merely because a similar event occurs later.

## 6. Structural observations that remain non-semantic

The following are structural facts/candidates, not semantic conclusions by themselves:

- Jump;
- descendant Jump;
- state/status strengthening;
- extra Agent/tool/stage activation;
- branch expansion;
- residual lineage;
- recurrence/rebound;
- reconvergence;
- affected dependency closure;
- field revision.

These may select a review packet but cannot settle C/P/R without the semantic evidence defined above.

## 7. Required negative/unresolved outcomes

A reviewer system must allow:

- `NON_CPR` — evidence supports a legitimate/non-CPR interpretation;
- `UNRESOLVED` — evidence is insufficient or genuinely ambiguous;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE` where the candidate lacks the prerequisite semantic structure, especially for R.

Reviewers must not force every candidate into C, P or R.

## 8. Historical boundary

This contract is forward-only. It does not relabel prior frozen evidence, including any batch protected as `NOT_ADJUDICATED` or any prior reviewer result produced under another semantic contract.
