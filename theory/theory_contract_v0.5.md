## 0. Scope

The first paper studies how reality-relevant state changes arise and propagate through naturally emergent multi-Agent processes. It does not define reliability primarily by terminal task accuracy and does not test a full engineering-governance framework as its main contribution.

## 1. Two non-equivalent validity axes

### Outcome Validity

Whether the terminal result satisfies an external task criterion.

### Process Reality Fidelity

Whether the process constructing that result keeps evidence, uncertainty, provenance, goal/scope, authority/responsibility and state transitions traceably supported.

Outcome correctness does not prove process fidelity. Outcome failure does not by itself prove process unreality.

## 2. Primary experimental object

A **System Trajectory** is the main experimental object. Behavior Events and State Transitions are measurement units inside it. Final answer/task success is one terminal property of the trajectory.

## 3. Phenomenon-first theory construction

The theory is generated and tested in two directions:

1. **Induction/abduction:** observe natural trajectories, localize recurrent phenomena, infer Jump/inertia mechanisms and summarize recurrent dynamic patterns as CPR;
2. **Prospective deduction/intervention:** freeze definitions, observe new trajectories, derive predictions, perturb one natural node once and test downstream structural consequences.

Post-hoc discovery evidence must not be relabeled as independent prospective confirmation.

## 4. Jump

A Jump is an observable local displacement in a recorded behavior/state transition that changes task-relevant epistemic state, goal/scope/focus, executable path, settled state, authority/responsibility state, or another preregistered reality-bearing dimension.

A Jump is an event, not automatically a semantic C/P/R truth and not automatically an error.

## 5. Inherited Inertia

Inherited Inertia is path dependence after Jump-derived content becomes part of downstream context, shared state, messages, invocation history, goals, evidence or operational history.

A downstream Agent can be locally coherent while operating on an already displaced inherited reality.

The mechanism under study is therefore not only `wrong content propagates`; it includes structural inheritance that changes which Agents activate, which paths remain reachable, where branches cross and whether the system re-enters prior roles.

## 6. CPR as process-dynamics taxonomy

CPR is derived from repeated trajectory phenomena and later frozen for prospective use.

### C

Epistemic reality promotion: missing, uncertain, inferred or weakly supported content acquires stronger and inheritable status beyond the support available in the process.

### P

Scope/focus reality expansion: the system introduces unnecessary or unauthorized goal, focus, information, Agent/tool/stage or responsibility expansion that materially alters process structure.

### R

Retrospective regeneration dynamics: prior C/P-derived state, or unresolved descendants, persists, regenerates, amplifies, launders or normalizes after a retrospective/challenge/correction opportunity.

Ordinary successful correction is Recovery, not R-positive evidence.

## 7. R2–R6 unified trajectory principle

R2, R3, R4 and R6 are simultaneous or asynchronously audited views over the same trajectory. R5 is a causal branch operator applied to a frozen natural node.

- R2 measures emergence;
- R3 measures inheritance/propagation/inertia;
- R4 measures retrospective CPR dynamics;
- R5 compares matched continuations after one local perturbation;
- R6 measures recovery/residual/recurrence.

R numbers do not imply that five separate subject experiments must be run.

## 8. One-shot local perturbation

For the forward R5-MID mechanism test:

- A and B restore the same frozen deterministic Arena state;
- only B receives a one-shot epistemic overlay at the selected natural Jump boundary;
- the experimental overlay is visible exactly once;
- the experiment does not persistently mutate shared-state metadata;
- after the exposure is consumed, the experiment does not reinject the field;
- downstream uncertainty, provisional status, Jump or CPR-like behavior generated by Agents remains natural system behavior and must be preserved.

This supports the causal form:

`one local perturbation -> free evolution -> global/topological response`.

## 9. Three nested mechanism scales

### Jump recurrence

Does a descendant Jump naturally reappear downstream after direct experimental exposure has ended?

### Inertia

Does the original path remain, deflect, break or later reconverge?

### Path topology

Do observed path families, crossings, loops, re-entry, merge/reroute and role/responsibility activation reorganize?

These are not required to move in the same numerical direction.

## 10. Structural result is not success/failure

Possible post-perturbation phenomena include disappearance, persistence, rerouting, delayed regeneration, new loops, alternative verification paths and reconvergence. None is designated in advance as the only successful result.

A null structural contrast under adequate repeated conditions is valid evidence.

## 11. Process and semantic evidence

Machine evidence establishes recorded behavior, state transition, direct exposure, lineage and structural topology. Semantic review may later judge whether a structural transition represents C/P/R, adoption, authority/responsibility crossing, legitimation or recovery.

Visibility is not adoption. Mechanical reach is not semantic Authority Penetration. RUN_COMPLETE is not task success.

## 12. Falsifiability

The theory is weakened if prospective evidence shows, under adequate tests, that:

- proposed Jump definitions cannot be reproducibly localized;
- downstream structure is indistinguishable from matched natural variability after the claimed mechanism perturbation;
- supposed recurrence cannot be separated from independent new Jumps;
- inherited inertia cannot be distinguished from generic activity volume;
- CPR categories cannot be reliably separated from ordinary valid inference/decomposition/correction;
- process-reality measurements add no explanatory information beyond terminal outcome or simple failure attribution.

## 13. Engineering boundary

Mechanism findings may later motivate a governance framework, but the first paper does not define theory success as engineering containment success.

A later engineering study may compare free systems with mechanism-informed governance and ask which identified mechanisms are altered.

## 14. Historical compatibility

Persistent status-downgrade experiments remain historical pilot evidence. They are not silently reclassified as one-shot evidence. New one-shot contracts require new code/config/binding identities and new evidence.
