# Reality Bias Research Program

## 1. Process Reality object

The program studies process reality fidelity in multi-Agent systems: how information acquires authority, enters carriers, becomes path-dependent, responds to bounded perturbation, and may later be localized or repaired.

Terminal correctness remains a separate outcome axis.

## 2. Normal inheritance

Normal inheritance is expected coordination behavior in which prior information is delivered, read and reused downstream.

Normal inheritance alone is not System Inertia.

## 3. Inherited System Inertia

**Inherited System Inertia** is source-backed path dependence in which a prior epistemic status, commitment, constraint or action tendency continues to shape later process options, transition propensity or downstream state through observable carriers after the originating event has passed.

Evidence may strengthen across:

`reachable -> delivered/read -> adopted -> inherited -> propagated`

A stronger persistence claim is supported when the inherited constraint survives a relevant challenge, downgrade or authority-withdrawal opportunity.

Inertia is not automatically a semantic violation or risk. Risk status remains downstream.

## 4. Carrier evidence and semantic use

A lineage edge establishes a recorded relationship. A hash establishes identity/version. Neither alone proves semantic adoption.

Where evidence permits, preserve relation-level evidence for:

- delivery;
- read;
- explicit reference;
- decision-premise adoption;
- abandonment/rejection;
- inherited state/action;
- propagation;
- source replacement.

## 5. R6 identification

R6 answers four questions:

1. natural variability;
2. carrier and inherited dependence;
3. post-consumption intervention-related inertia transition;
4. target-specific response relative to ordinary factual uncertainty.

Local response remains separate from post-consumption inertia.

## 6. Three-domain comparison geometry

R6 comparison uses separate domains:

- **STRUCTURAL** — topology/path/actor/state-transition geometry;
- **INHERITANCE** — read/use/adopt/abandon/inherit/propagate/source-replacement behavior;
- **EPISTEMIC_AUTHORITY** — downstream use of authority/status, downgrade response and re-confirmation evidence.

These domains must not be silently reduced to one post-hoc scalar.

## 7. Specificity boundary

S1 and S2 use the same frozen operator, but target equivalence is not assumed.

`S2 - S1` first identifies the difference between two frozen target responses under common mechanics.

An Escape-derived/J0-specific causal interpretation requires a supported matching argument or append-only robustness controls across additional ordinary factual targets.

## 8. Conditionality

Same-parent continuations from the exact frozen prefix identify conditional behavior under that prefix. They do not by themselves establish population-level or architecture-general causality.

## 9. R7 closure semantics

R7 distinguishes:

- structurally reachable potential descendants;
- descendants with evidence-supported adoption/inheritance;
- the minimal authorized repair closure.

Repair should target the smallest justified closure, not all structurally reachable descendants by default.

## 10. Experiment-origin and endogenous state

`persistent_state_mutation=false` in the frozen R5/R6 operator means no persistent mutation is directly written by the experiment.

Endogenous Agent state produced after exposure remains allowed, observable and scientifically relevant.

## 11. CPR boundary

C/P/R remain semantic adjudication objects. Structural inheritance, fact labels, extra Agent calls or recurrence do not automatically establish CPR.

## 12. Engineering implication

A Process Integrity Protocol may use content addressing to identify and trace process objects, but active repair scope must be determined using source-backed relation/adoption evidence plus frozen replay dependencies.

## 13. Falsifiability

The program is weakened if:

- claimed inertia cannot be distinguished from ordinary inheritance;
- target-specific claims depend on treating non-comparable targets as exchangeable without justification;
- semantic adoption is inferred from reachability/hash identity alone;
- repair requires broad suppression because affected closure cannot be localized;
- same-parent conditional results are presented as population-general effects without replication.

## 14. Authorization

This contract authorizes no scientific provider call, evaluator call, semantic adjudication or active recovery.
