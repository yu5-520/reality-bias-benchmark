## 0. Scope

The first-paper mechanism program studies how reality-bearing state changes emerge, become inherited process inertia, reorganize downstream paths under bounded perturbation, and may later become structurally steerable or locally recoverable without collapsing the process into a predefined answer workflow.

Terminal task accuracy remains a separate outcome axis.

## 1. Validity axes

### Outcome Validity

Whether the terminal result satisfies an external task criterion.

### Process Reality Fidelity

Whether the process constructing that result keeps evidence, uncertainty, provenance, scope, authority/responsibility and state transitions traceably supported.

Outcome correctness does not prove process fidelity. Outcome failure does not by itself prove process unreality.

## 2. Primary object and evidence layers

A **System Trajectory** is the primary experimental object.

Evidence is separated into four layers:

1. **Raw observable facts** — actual messages, invocations, state reads/writes, actor/turn identity, state transitions, final/revision operations and other runtime records.
2. **Derived structural measurements** — Jump, source-backed lineage, descendant relation, path family, branch/merge/re-entry, inherited inertia, dependency closure and affected/preserved subgraph.
3. **Semantic adjudication** — CPR and related semantic interpretations appended after structural evidence is frozen.
4. **Terminal outcome** — external task result reported separately from process evidence.

This separation is required to prevent measurement circularity. A structural observation framework may reveal a topology; it is not evidence that the framework created the topology.

## 3. Natural-process condition

Natural Arena trajectories may operate under common experimental interfaces, role/responsibility definitions and action protocols. They are not therefore unconstrained in every possible sense.

The relevant forward claim is narrower:

> Agents are not structurally steered toward the observed natural topology by active ALR recovery, required node ordering, lineage prompting or a target path in the natural condition.

Natural topology is realized by the executing system and reconstructed from recorded evidence.

## 4. Jump

A Jump is an observable local displacement in a recorded behavior/state transition that changes a preregistered reality-bearing dimension such as epistemic state, goal/scope/focus, executable path, settled state or authority/responsibility state.

A Jump is an event. It is not automatically a semantic C/P/R truth and not automatically an error.

## 5. Inherited Inertia

Inherited Inertia is source-backed path dependence after Jump-derived content becomes part of downstream context, messages, shared state, invocation history, evidence, goals or operational history.

Inertia is not generic activity volume. It requires a traceable relation between a prior state/transition and downstream structural consequences.

A downstream Agent may be locally coherent while operating on an already displaced inherited reality.

## 6. CPR

CPR is a process-dynamics taxonomy derived from repeated trajectory phenomena and later frozen for semantic adjudication.

### C — Epistemic reality promotion

Missing, uncertain, inferred or weakly supported content acquires stronger and inheritable system status beyond the support available in the process.

### P — Scope/focus reality expansion

Goal, focus, information, Agent/tool/stage surface or responsibility expands beyond the supported/authorized process closure and materially changes the process path.

### R — Retrospective regeneration dynamics

Prior C/P-derived state or unresolved descendants persist, regenerate, amplify, launder or normalize after a retrospective/challenge/reopen/correction opportunity.

Ordinary successful correction is Recovery, not R-positive evidence.

## 7. Three mechanism properties

The forward program distinguishes three properties that must not be collapsed.

### Perturbability — R5

Can a bounded one-shot local change at a frozen natural J0 reorganize the downstream path after the experimental mark is removed?

Evidence of perturbability does not establish persistent control.

### Persistence / Inertia — R6

After path reorganization, what historical tendency remains or newly forms across later nodes? Does it persist, decay, transfer, transform, rebound, get replaced, reconstruct or reconverge?

### Structural Steering / Controllability — R7

Can structural handling alter the direction, persistence, localization or recoverability of inherited inertia while leaving terminal content generation open?

R7 is a system-level question. It is not synonymous with any single recovery mechanism.

## 8. R5 — one-shot path reorganization

The established R5 design remains:

- same frozen deterministic Arena state for matched continuations;
- one local intervention at the selected natural Jump boundary;
- one direct experiment-origin exposure only;
- no persistent experiment-origin mutation;
- no reinjection after consumption;
- free downstream evolution.

The causal form is:

`one local perturbation -> free evolution -> observed structural response`.

The R5 endpoint is downstream structural distinguishability, not a directional claim and not an inertia-control claim.

## 9. R6 — post-reorganization inertia dynamics

R6 conceptually consolidates the detailed post-Jump measurements already present in the research program.

The following remain first-class observations:

- descendant Jump continuation or disappearance;
- same-family versus transformed Jump;
- inheritance and transfer of Jump-derived state;
- delayed re-emergence;
- residual descendants;
- retrospective/challenge-mediated return;
- same-type/transformed return;
- inheritance after return;
- branch/merge/re-entry and cross-Agent continuation;
- reconvergence or extinction.

These are observable projections of the higher-order construct **post-reorganization system inertia**.

Recovery, residual and recurrence are possible manifestations of inertia dynamics rather than the complete definition of R6.

## 10. R7 — three structural conditions

R7 separates three distinct mechanisms at the same frozen J0 / parent state where feasible.

### C1 — One-Shot Free Continuation

The experiment-origin signal is exposed once, consumed and removed. Downstream continuation is free.

This estimates the realized structure of **endogenous inherited inertia** after the experimental mark has exited active context.

### C2 — Persistent Field Propagation

The same semantic field/signal remains continuously available across later nodes.

This estimates **experiment-maintained persistence**. It must not be treated as direct evidence that the same persistence would arise endogenously.

### C3 — ALR Authority-Localized Recovery

The same correction semantics are handled through a localized recovery mechanism that identifies the earliest authority-violating ancestor, computes its affected dependency closure, preserves unaffected successful structure, repairs the breached authority/provenance condition, creates a new revision identity and reopens/re-executes only the affected subgraph.

This estimates **causally localized structural recovery**.

## 11. Two nested R7 contrasts

### One-Shot vs Persistent Field

Tests whether endogenous inheritance differs from persistence maintained by continuous experimental delivery.

### Persistent Field vs ALR

Tests whether continuously exposing the same correction information differs from using dependency/lineage structure to localize and recover the affected process.

The semantic correction payload should be matched as closely as possible. The intended variable is structural handling, not richer content in the ALR arm.

## 12. ALR definition

ALR means **Authority-Localized Recovery**.

ALR is a **localized structural recovery operator**. It is not the entire R7 structural-steering theory and is not defined by repeated feedback alone.

Given a frozen recovery target, ALR:

1. identifies the earliest source-backed ancestor at which a named authority/provenance condition was violated;
2. computes the dependency closure reachable from that ancestor under frozen derivation rules;
3. defines `Affected` as that closure and `Preserved` as the realized structure outside it;
4. repairs the violated authority/provenance condition rather than specifying a desired final answer;
5. creates a new revision identity/hash linked to the repaired ancestor/state;
6. reopens/re-executes only the affected subgraph;
7. records residual old inertia, recurrence, transfer, re-entry, reconstruction and secondary Jump formation.

The operator may be invoked again under a preregistered protocol if another valid recovery condition is reached. Iteration is a protocol choice, not the identity of ALR itself.

## 13. Why Full Rerun is not equivalent

A Full Rerun changes the realized sample and may preserve the same violated authority condition. It can therefore resample into the same failure basin without establishing that the causal structural condition was repaired.

ALR instead changes the frozen violated condition and its dependent subgraph while preserving unrelated successful structure where evidence permits.

Checkpoint retry may also remain a useful secondary engineering comparator, but neither Full Rerun nor Checkpoint Retry defines the main R7 scientific contrast.

## 14. Structural direction

R7 uses **direction** as an observable graph/transition property rather than a desired answer label.

Direction may be operationalized through:

- actor transition sequence or edge distribution;
- state-lineage destination;
- information-flow destination;
- descendant-Jump location;
- re-entry topology;
- stage progression;
- path-family transition or reconstruction;
- reconvergence distance.

A future mathematical representation may express these as vectors or transition kernels, but the current first-paper design does not require literal vector arithmetic.

## 15. Structural-steering evidence

A valid R7 finding may include:

- weakening or attenuation;
- redirection;
- delay;
- localization;
- transfer to another lineage;
- successful local recovery;
- partial recovery;
- recurrence or rebound;
- secondary Jump formation;
- failure to localize or recover within horizon.

No one direction is pre-labelled as the only scientifically acceptable result.

## 16. Freedom preservation

Reduced risk-bearing activity alone cannot support a useful localized-recovery claim.

R7 must also report preservation cost:

- unaffected nodes/fields/branches preserved;
- unrelated Agent work preserved;
- unnecessary reopen/rerun count;
- non-risk path-family diversity retained/lost;
- scope of intervention relative to the realized process graph.

Whole-system suppression is not equivalent to localized recovery.

## 17. R8 semantic closure

Structural evidence precedes semantic closure.

R8 freezes:

- CPR definitions and exclusion boundaries;
- evidence required for semantic adjudication;
- reviewer independence and disagreement handling;
- candidate/adjudicated/unresolved status distinctions;
- provenance from raw evidence through structural derivation to semantic finding;
- final paper-level evidence object.

Historical evidence whose freeze contract says `NOT_ADJUDICATED` must not be silently relabeled.

## 18. R9 external robustness

Reviewer, model, domain and independent implementation/laboratory replication belong to R9. They strengthen external robustness but do not redefine the identity of the primary R2–R8 evidence.

## 19. Falsifiability

The theory is weakened if prospective evidence shows, under adequate tests, that:

- Jump definitions cannot be reproducibly localized from raw records;
- inherited inertia cannot be distinguished from generic activity volume;
- one-shot matched perturbations do not produce structural contrasts beyond matched natural variability;
- claimed R6 inertia states cannot be supported by source-backed downstream relations;
- continuous field exposure and localized structural recovery cannot be experimentally separated because the semantic payload cannot be matched;
- ALR localization depends on intuition rather than frozen dependency/authority evidence;
- ALR recovery routinely requires whole-system rerun despite claims of local dependency closure;
- preservation cost cannot be separated from risk reduction;
- CPR categories cannot be reliably distinguished from legitimate inference, decomposition, expansion or recovery;
- structural measurements add no explanatory value beyond terminal outcomes or simple failure labels.

## 20. Nonclaims

The forward program does not claim in advance that:

- every Jump is a Reality Bias event;
- every trajectory must exhibit C, P or R;
- one-shot correction controls system inertia;
- persistent-field exposure proves endogenous inertia;
- ALR is synonymous with all structural steering;
- ALR always succeeds;
- structured routing is universally superior to free routing;
- one matched parent establishes a generalized causal law;
- provider hidden state is replayed;
- current frozen R5 evidence has semantic CPR adjudication where the freeze record says otherwise.

## 21. Forward mechanism chain

**Jump -> Inherited Inertia -> One-shot Path Reorganization -> Inertia Transition -> Structural Inertia Steering / Authority-Localized Recovery -> CPR Semantic Closure**

This chain is an analytical ordering. It does not require every component to be a separate subject run.
