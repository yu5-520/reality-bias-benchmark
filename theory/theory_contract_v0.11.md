# Reality Bias Research Program
## R0 — Theory Contract v0.11

**Status:** FORWARD / PROCESS-REALITY v5 / STRUCTURAL-SUPPORT AND SHARED-POOL REBASE  
**Date:** 2026-09-18  
**Supersedes for forward interpretation:** \`theory/theory_contract_v0.10.md\`

## 1. Primary object

The program studies **Process Reality Fidelity** in multi-Agent systems.

The primary question is not whether one Agent generated a wrong sentence. It is whether the epistemic state, semantic boundary and decision implications of real-world information remain faithful as that information is transformed, structurally supported, shared and directly consumed across a multi-Agent process.

Terminal correctness remains a separate outcome axis.

## 2. Multi-source instability

Forward theory recognizes four interacting source families:

- multi-source information;
- multi-source pressure;
- multi-source goals;
- multi-source boundaries.

Their interaction may produce **Unstable Information**: information whose value, scope, authority, interpretation or action meaning is not yet stably resolved across the system.

Unstable does not mean false.

## 3. Escape Structure

**Escape Structure** is an emergent transmission structure in which information is repeatedly transformed, adopted, reframed, materialized or converted into downstream constraints across messages, state, Agents or actions.

Escape is therefore a property of the realized transmission structure, not an intrinsic property of one information item and not a hidden mental property attributed to one Agent.

## 4. Semantic transformation

A single source proposition may become multiple downstream semantic forms.

Example form:

\`source proposition -> derived proposition -> judgment -> shared state -> action constraint\`

Literal persistence is neither required nor sufficient for semantic persistence.

## 5. Structural Support

A **Structural Support Point/Region** is a candidate location at which a proposition or descendant meaning obtains enough system-level structural support to continue through addressable state, dependency, relation or action structure without requiring the original Agent-to-Agent relay to be repeated.

Structural support is not automatically correctness, authority legitimacy or CPR-positive evidence.

## 6. Stable Shared Information Pool

A **Stable Shared Information Pool** is a system-accessible information state from which downstream Agents may directly consume information or descendants without requiring a new Agent-to-Agent transmission from the original carrier.

"Stable" means structurally addressable and reusable. It does not mean true, confirmed or unbiased.

Forward audit should preserve, where observable:

- pool-entry ref;
- source ref;
- source epistemic status;
- pool epistemic status;
- direct consumer refs;
- transformation lineage;
- relation evidence.

## 7. Structural Exposure

A **Structural Exposure State/Region** is a later state in which accumulated support, reuse, dependency or constraint becomes sufficiently observable for a low-cost structural scout to nominate a bounded semantic-audit region.

Semantic genesis may precede structural support. Structural support may precede structural exposure.

\`semantic genesis != structural support != structural exposure\`

## 8. Historical Jump/J0 boundary

Historical \`Jump\`, \`J0\` and \`*_JUMP_CANDIDATE\` identifiers remain valid frozen experimental identifiers.

Forward theory does not assume:

- J0 is semantic origin;
- J0 is the first structural-support point;
- J0 is the first stable-pool entry;
- J0 is the only valid exposure anchor.

Any new mapping from a historical J0 to Structural Support or Structural Exposure requires evidence-bound re-derivation.

## 9. System Inertia

System Inertia remains a valid downstream property, but it is no longer the entire theory object.

A stronger inertia claim requires evidence that semantic relations, descendants, constraints or action tendencies continue to shape later process behavior after a relevant challenge, downgrade or withdrawal opportunity.

Structural counts, hash ancestry, literal recurrence and visibility alone remain insufficient.

## 10. R5 forward role

R5 has two forward roles:

1. **Structural Scouting** — reduce the semantic-audit surface by using system-native structural evidence to nominate bounded candidate regions.
2. **Driver Discrimination** — use minimal probes to test whether observed persistence remains source-information-driven or is supported by already materialized structure.

The historical \`fact -> unconfirmed\` one-shot operator is one implemented driver-probe family. It is not the definition of R5.

## 11. R6 forward role

R6 identifies the structure needed to explain and control persistence.

R6 should distinguish:

- source/root;
- semantic transformation chain;
- Structural Support;
- stable-pool entry;
- Structural Exposure;
- upstream trace;
- downstream affected closure;
- candidate intervention surface.

Forward principle:

\`Detection Surface != Trace Root != Intervention Surface\`

R6 may still report natural variability, carrier evidence, post-challenge persistence and historical specificity modules, but those are analysis modules rather than the universal shape of every future run.

## 12. R7 forward role

R7 tests localized recovery over an evidence-supported affected structure.

Repair may occur at the source, a structural-support point, a pool entry, a descendant constraint or a bounded closure. The selected repair surface must be named explicitly.

Repair should preserve unrelated process freedom and must not infer semantic affectedness from reachability/hash identity alone.

## 13. Observation hierarchy

Forward evidence hierarchy:

\`raw evidence -> structural indexing -> support/pool/exposure candidates -> localized semantic audit -> claim/boundary audit -> optional control\`

The structural layer is a measuring/scouting instrument, not a semantic judge.

## 14. Falsifiability

The v5 theory is weakened if:

- unstable-information candidates cannot be distinguished from ordinary resolved information under an operational audit;
- support/pool/exposure objects cannot be bound to raw evidence;
- structural scouting fails to reduce the semantic-audit surface;
- alleged pool entry still requires hidden Agent-to-Agent relay to reproduce the information;
- support/persistence claims depend only on reachability or literal recurrence;
- source tracing cannot be separated from post-hoc narrative reconstruction;
- localized intervention surfaces cannot be distinguished from whole-process suppression.

## 15. Historical compatibility

v4.5.1 remains the frozen legacy methodological layer for evidence already bound to it.

v5 is forward-only unless an append-only re-derivation explicitly binds old frozen evidence to new interpretive objects.
