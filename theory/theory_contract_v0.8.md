## 0. Scope

The first-paper program studies **Process Reality Fidelity**: how a naturally realized system state acquires process authority, becomes inherited system inertia, changes downstream trajectory under a bounded local perturbation, and can later be distinguished from generic uncertainty response and subjected to localized risk control.

Terminal task correctness remains a separate outcome axis.

## 1. Evidence layers

A System Trajectory is the primary experimental object. Evidence remains separated into:

1. raw observable facts;
2. source-backed structural derivation;
3. semantic adjudication after structural freeze;
4. terminal outcome reported separately.

Structural observability reconstructs realized relations; it does not prescribe the natural topology.

## 2. Jump

A Jump is an observable local displacement in a recorded behavior/state transition that changes a preregistered reality-bearing dimension such as epistemic state, scope/focus, executable path, settled state or authority/responsibility state.

A Jump is an event. It is not automatically semantic C/P/R and not automatically an error.

## 3. Reality Authority

**Reality Authority** is the process-level status by which information is treated as sufficiently factual, settled, authorized or provenance-supported to be inherited into later reasoning, state, messages, invocations or historical process structure.

Reality Authority is not merely linguistic confidence. It is operational when the recorded system permits the information to influence downstream process state as if that status were available.

A Jump may involve information acquiring stronger Reality Authority than the available support warrants. Whether that event is semantic C/P/R is deferred to R8.

## 4. Inherited Inertia

Inherited Inertia is source-backed path dependence after prior process state becomes a downstream structural carrier through context, messages, shared state, invocation history, goals, evidence, responsibility or operational history.

Activity volume is not sufficient. Inertia requires a traceable relation between prior state/transition and later structural consequences.

Jump phenotype may disappear while structural inertia remains.

## 5. CPR semantic taxonomy

CPR remains downstream semantic adjudication:

- **C** — unsupported or weak epistemic state acquires stronger inheritable reality status;
- **P** — scope/focus/information/Agent/tool/stage surface expands beyond supported process closure and changes the path;
- **R** — prior C/P-derived state or unresolved descendants persist, regenerate, amplify, launder or normalize after a retrospective/challenge/reopen/correction opportunity.

Ordinary correction is Recovery, not R-positive evidence.

## 6. R2-R6 analytical geometry

R2, R3, R4 and R6 are observational views over a trajectory. R5 is the bounded intervention operator.

- R2 asks where a natural Jump appears.
- R3 asks how its state is inherited or propagated.
- R4 asks what happens at retrospective/challenge/reopen boundaries.
- R6 asks what source-backed system inertia exists and how it changes.
- R5 supplies one local perturbation at a selected natural J0 and then releases the process to free continuation.

Therefore:

> **R5 supplies the perturbation; R6 supplies the principal inertia-level readout.**

## 7. R5 — authority withdrawal, not semantic counter-instruction

The canonical R5 mechanism probe is a one-shot **withdrawal of acquired factual authority** at J0.

For the established status family this can be represented as:

`fact -> unconfirmed`

The scientific identity is defined by what the intervention does not do:

- no opposite factual claim;
- no replacement conclusion;
- no desired action;
- no desired terminal answer;
- no Agent identity/prompt modification;
- no topology prescription;
- no persistent experiment-origin state mutation;
- no reinjection after the first exposure.

After one exposure the experiment-origin mark is consumed and downstream execution is generative/free under the normal Arena constraints.

R5 primary property: **Perturbability / Path Reorganization**.

## 8. R6 — post-consumption inertia

R6 is not defined by recovery alone. It consolidates continuation, disappearance, inheritance, transfer, transformation, recurrence, return, reconstruction and reconvergence into the higher-order construct of **post-consumption / post-reorganization system inertia**.

The central separation is:

> **Immediate perturbation response is not equivalent to an inertia transition.**

R6 requires source-backed downstream evidence after the experiment-origin signal has exited active context.

Candidate dimensions include:

- descendant Jump continuation/extinction;
- family continuity/transformation;
- direct/indirect inheritance;
- cross-field or cross-Agent transfer;
- delayed re-emergence;
- retrospective return/rebound;
- post-return inheritance;
- branch/merge/re-entry/cross-Agent continuation;
- replacement/reconstruction/hybridization;
- reconvergence;
- censoring.

R6 primary property: **Persistence / Inertia Transition**.

## 9. Local counterfactual falsification interpretation

The R5-R6 combination is best interpreted as a **falsification-oriented local counterfactual perturbation**, not mathematical proof by contradiction.

Candidate null interpretation:

> J0 is only a local information event and its acquired factual authority is not structurally embedded in downstream system inertia.

Procedure:

1. locate natural J0 from frozen evidence;
2. withdraw only its factual authority once;
3. consume the experimental signal;
4. permit free continuation;
5. measure downstream inertia through R6.

If downstream inertia reorganizes, the null interpretation is challenged under that matched local intervention.

This is evidence of **causal implication under the frozen intervention**, not a universal causal law.

## 10. Generic uncertainty alternative

A successful R5-R6 contrast still admits a weaker explanation:

> changing any factual field from `fact` to `unconfirmed` may create additional uncertainty and therefore reorganize downstream structure.

This alternative must be tested prospectively rather than dismissed rhetorically.

The forward program therefore adds **target specificity** as a separate mechanism property.

## 11. R5-R6 target specificity

Target specificity asks:

> Does the same one-shot status downgrade produce a distinguishable inertia-level response when applied to J0 compared with a preregistered matched non-J0 factual field?

Use the separate namespace:

- `S0` Natural Reference;
- `S1` Matched Factual-Field Status Downgrade;
- `S2` J0-Targeted Authority Withdrawal.

Interpretations:

- `S1 - S0` = generic epistemic uncertainty response;
- `S2 - S0` = total J0-targeted response;
- `S2 - S1` = target-specificity contrast.

The primary specificity contrast is `S2 vs S1`.

S1 is not required to be inert. Its purpose is to estimate the generic effect of the same mechanical downgrade.

## 12. Matched factual-field principle

A valid S1 target must be selected outcome-blind from a frozen eligible pool.

Matching should prioritize structural comparability such as:

- factual status before intervention;
- parent/trace availability;
- visibility/timing;
- state/interface family;
- actor/role accessibility;
- comparable opportunity to influence downstream execution.

The control field must not be selected because it is expected to have little effect.

Do not call it a `non-Jump field` unless independently supported. `Matched factual field` or `matched non-J0 factual field` is the preferred neutral term.

## 13. Mechanism properties must remain distinct

The forward theory separates four properties:

1. **Perturbability — R5**: does one local authority withdrawal reorganize the path?
2. **Inertia implication — R6**: after intervention exits, does the downstream inherited tendency differ?
3. **Target specificity — R5-R6 S triad**: is the inertia response distinguishable from a matched generic uncertainty response?
4. **Risk-bounded structural control — R7**: can risk-bearing inherited inertia be localized/recovered while preserving unrelated process freedom?

Evidence for one property does not automatically prove the next.

## 14. R7 namespace separation

Existing R7 conditions remain `C1/C2/C3`:

- C1 one-shot free continuation;
- C2 persistent field propagation;
- C3 Authority-Localized Recovery.

The S triad and C triad are orthogonal:

- `S` varies **target identity/specificity**;
- `C` varies **structural handling mode**.

A shared runtime primitive may be reused, but experimental identity must be explicit in plans and evidence.

## 15. Mechanical primitive versus scientific role

The primitive `ONE_SHOT_EPISTEMIC_STATUS_DOWNGRADE` may support multiple scientific roles:

- R5 J0 probe;
- R5-R6 S1 matched factual-field control;
- R5-R6 S2 J0 target;
- R7 C1 one-shot free continuation.

The operator is not itself the experiment. Research identity is determined by frozen target, contrast, hypothesis and evidence contract.

## 16. R7 — Structural Inertia Risk Control

R7 asks whether risk-bearing inherited inertia can be localized, bounded or recovered without forcing the whole system into a predefined workflow or target answer.

ALR remains **Authority-Localized Recovery**: a localized recovery operator anchored to a source-backed authority/provenance breach and dependency closure.

R7 must report both risk reduction and freedom preservation. Whole-system suppression or canonical-path forcing is not equivalent to localized recovery.

## 17. R8 semantic closure

Structural mechanism evidence precedes semantic CPR adjudication.

The S specificity test does not determine whether J0 is C/P/R. Historical evidence frozen as `NOT_ADJUDICATED` remains historically unchanged.

## 18. Falsifiability

The theory is weakened if prospective evidence shows, under adequate tests, that:

- J0 cannot be reproducibly localized from raw evidence;
- the one-shot intervention cannot be separated from prompt steering or persistent mutation;
- post-consumption inertia cannot be distinguished from immediate local activity;
- R6 claimed lineage/inertia lacks source-backed relations;
- matched factual-field selection cannot be made outcome-blind and structurally comparable;
- S2 is not distinguishable from S1 beyond matched natural variability under adequate replication;
- claimed specificity depends on cherry-picked control fields;
- ALR localization requires whole-system suppression despite claims of local recovery;
- structural measurements add no explanatory value beyond terminal correctness or simple failure labels.

## 19. Nonclaims

The forward program does not claim in advance that:

- every Jump is a Reality Bias event;
- every factual-status downgrade should have no effect on a normal field;
- R5 alone proves Jump-specific causal status;
- R6 alone proves target specificity;
- S2 must always produce a larger or more complex response than S1;
- direction must be monotonic across matched runs;
- one matched parent establishes a generalized causal law;
- provider hidden state is replayed;
- existing R7 readiness is itself specificity evidence;
- structural specificity automatically establishes semantic CPR truth.

## 20. Forward mechanism chain

**Natural Jump -> Inherited Inertia -> One-Shot Authority Withdrawal -> Path Reorganization -> Post-Consumption Inertia Transition -> Target Specificity -> Structural Inertia Risk Control / Authority-Localized Recovery -> CPR Semantic Closure**
