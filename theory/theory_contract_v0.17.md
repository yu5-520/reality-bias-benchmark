## 1. Preserved theory

v0.17 preserves v0.16:

- C = Information Permission Penetration;
- P = Collaboration / Execution Boundary Permission Penetration;
- R = Temporal Permission Penetration;
- C/P/R are dynamic, coupled and potentially mutually maintaining;
- Shared Process-Reality Layer is a reusable process-reality substrate;
- independent evidence must be separated from lineage-derived semantic support.

## 2. New clarification: observation-stage non-exclusivity

Experimental stage and CPR dimension are not equivalent.

A stage can expose multiple permission dimensions.

The same C/P/R mechanism may also be visible from more than one experimental perspective.

Therefore:

`R2/R3/R4/R5/R6/R7 != exclusive C/P/R assignment`.

## 3. R2-R4 natural stochastic observation

Natural trajectories are scientifically important because CPR can emerge without researcher intervention.

Natural observation can include:

- information stabilization and semantic descendant accumulation;
- collaboration/scope expansion;
- reviewer-driven or system-internal retrospective reopening;
- mutual maintenance across C/P/R.

Structural markers nominate semantic windows but do not establish permission penetration.

## 4. R5 observational cut

R5 remains a strong local mechanism probe because one source authority is weakened once and the downstream response is observed.

Its special value is specificity, not exclusivity.

R5 does not define the full CPR evidence universe.

## 5. R6 passive mechanism observation

Passive means the researcher does not add a new active treatment.

It does not mean the observed system is temporally or structurally static.

R6 may expose:

- source-to-carrier transformation;
- descendant creation;
- reader/adopter reuse;
- decision/action dependence;
- independent re-anchoring;
- internal review;
- internal recomputation;
- role re-entry;
- persistent lineage influence.

Any of these may become C/P/R evidence after semantic adjudication.

## 6. R7 controlled temporal observation

R7-P and R7-S are not merely engineering endpoints.

They create different controlled process surfaces.

R7-S explicitly contains a retrospective boundary:

`repair -> invalidate -> reopen -> recompute -> rebuild`.

This makes R7-S a high-value Dynamic R observation context.

A positive R still requires post-boundary C/P permission penetration.

## 7. Cross-stage evidence synthesis

R8 semantic closure should compare evidence across observation contexts.

A semantic finding may be strengthened by convergent evidence from different stages, while retaining each stage's own causal/observational limitations.

Cross-stage convergence does not convert a selected mechanism set into a prevalence denominator.

## 8. Branch-specific negative findings

A negative or non-established result is scoped to the branch that produced it.

For example:

`P_NOT_ESTABLISHED in B1`

does not imply:

`P_NOT_ESTABLISHED across R2-R7`.

Likewise for R.

## 9. Dynamic coupling evidence

Coupling edges require:

1. a supported source semantic event;
2. a supported target semantic event;
3. evidence that the source event changed the conditions under which the target event emerged.

Co-occurrence is insufficient.

## 10. Evidence discipline

Reviewers may not invent:

- unrecorded user authorization boundaries;
- hidden Agent intent;
- missing source lineage;
- unobserved retrospective operations;
- independent evidence not present in frozen material.

Allowed outcomes remain:

`SUPPORTED / NOT_ESTABLISHED / NON_CPR / UNRESOLVED / NOT_APPLICABLE / REVIEW_DISAGREEMENT`.
