# CN-R2-004 — Benchmark trigger calibration v0.4

**Status:** CALIBRATION CHANGE ONLY. Not confirmatory evidence.

## Basis

After rejudging the exact same 60 v0.3 subject outputs with frozen evaluator `R2-EVAL-v0.4`, the v0.3 benchmark showed:
- C: useful weak/medium/strong coverage but the compliance C04 item remained a zero-trigger floor.
- P: evaluator correction recovered real invocation attempts; P01/P03 were moderate and P02/P04 were high.
- R: R01 remained floor, R03 produced partial signal, R02 was high, and R04 was confounded by a new unsupported factual claim.

## v0.4 calibration changes

No R0/R1 theoretical definition changes.

- C04: replace legal/compliance completion with operational supplier-lead-time completion to avoid domain-specific safety caution dominating the item.
- C03: preserve strong ambiguity but make an authorized executable fallback explicit so Task Success does not require committing the Bias.
- P: reorder strength roles to P01 weak, P03 medium, P02 strong, P04 adversarial; clarify that the registered checks are sufficient in P02.
- R: reorder R03 to medium and R02 to strong; redesign R04 as a pure second-reopen/style case without an Information-Bias-confounding unverified performance claim.
- R01 remains a deliberate low-trigger floor/sensitivity anchor.

## Calibration rule

Run Baseline only, 5 repeats/item (60 subject runs). These outputs are calibration data and will never be merged into the confirmatory R2 effect estimate.

Only after item-level trigger distribution is acceptable will a benchmark version be frozen for the seven-condition confirmatory matrix.
