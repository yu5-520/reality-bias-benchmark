# CN-R2-014 — C/P confirmatory infrastructure retry hardening

**Status:** ACCEPTED. Infrastructure-only correction; no benchmark, subject-prompt, evaluator-rubric, temperature, model, or gate-semantics change.

The first partial C/P confirmatory attempt produced 71/72 valid unique traces. The remaining trace (`R2-P04`, Structured-I/O, trial 1) failed because the blinded evaluator returned malformed JSON after the existing three identical-request JSON-format retries. Because the runner used all-or-nothing in-memory trace storage, the 71 successful traces were not emitted as a scientific dataset.

The failed attempt is therefore an infrastructure failure and is excluded from confirmatory estimates.

For the replacement run:
- DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash remains fixed;
- subject temperature remains 0.2;
- evaluator temperature remains 0.0;
- subject max tokens remain 1200;
- evaluator max tokens remain 1800;
- evaluator v0.4 and all prompts remain unchanged;
- evaluator JSON-format retry budget increases from 3 to 6 identical-request attempts;
- the runner writes each valid unique trace to a checkpoint cache immediately;
- failed traces receive at most two full-trace infrastructure retries; successful traces are never selectively discarded based on their scientific score.

The replacement run remains a fresh confirmatory dataset. Results from the failed all-or-nothing attempt are not combined with it.
