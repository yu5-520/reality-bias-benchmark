# CN-R-086 — P4 Inter-System Extrapolation Boundary Attack

Date: 2026-09-21  
Status: **ACCEPTED / GENERALIZATION-BOUNDARY HARDENING**

## Attack

> The experiments are Multi-Agent. Does Inter-System Process Reality overreach beyond the evidence?

## Decision

Yes, if presented as an empirical generalization.

No, if presented as a clearly bounded and testable outlook.

The 90 held-out natural trajectories vary domain across Finance, Supply Chain and Software Engineering but retain the same broad interacting-agent runtime.

Canonical guard:

> **cross-domain != cross-system validation**

## Evidence ladder

- empirical: Process Reality / C/P/R / lineage / perturbation / repair in studied multi-agent trajectories;
- mechanism abstraction: separability of content, authority, scope and temporal permission;
- outlook: arbitrary Human-AI, database/workflow, service/service, ERP/CRM, Character/World or model-native boundaries.

Canonical paper boundary:

> **Multi-Agent is the empirical setting. Inter-System Process Reality is the next-level hypothesis.**

## Future test requirement

Inter-system validation requires independently governed source/target state and authority, an explicit transfer object, observable commit rules, and a comparison between value/schema preservation and semantic-authority preservation.
