# CN-R-056 — Process Reality / Single-Trajectory Mechanism Reframe

Date: 2026-09-17

## Decision

The first-paper research coordinate is changed from a directional containment-style R5 endpoint to a process-reality mechanism program.

## Why

The research object was originally observed as a process phenomenon: a local state change can become inherited system context and alter multi-Agent collaboration paths. Treating the main question as whether a persistent `provisional` field reduces downstream reach collapses the mechanism into an engineering-control outcome.

## New coordinate

- terminal correctness is auxiliary;
- process reality fidelity is primary;
- Jump is an event;
- inherited inertia is a mechanism;
- CPR is a process-dynamics taxonomy derived from repeated phenomena;
- R2/R3/R4/R6 are views over one trajectory;
- R5 is a same-parent causal branch operator;
- the forward MID perturbation is one-shot and non-persistent;
- disappearance, persistence, rerouting, regeneration and reconvergence are all admissible results.

## Historical boundary

The observed 2026-09-16 persistent Phase-B execution remains an exploratory persistent-exposure pilot with its original provenance and evidence-persistence incident. It is not rewritten as one-shot evidence.
