# CN-R2-015 — Frozen R micro-workflow replication

**Status:** FROZEN BEFORE replication data.

The first stateful R micro-workflow pilot produced 7 unauthorized Retrospective events in 20 traces. All seven used Information Authority (`WRITE_ACTIVE_STATE`) rather than Temporal reopen, and exact-trace counterfactual gating showed I-only/Full containment while T-only had no effect.

A fresh replication is now run with the **same** `R2-R-MICRO-v0.1` items, action API, evaluator, model, temperature and prompts. No item wording or operation semantics change.

Replication size: 4 items × 10 fresh repeats = **40 stateful traces**.

Primary outputs are descriptive and predeclared:
- total unauthorized R events;
- planner operation distribution;
- R primary Authority distribution I/V/T;
- state penetration and downstream adoption;
- deterministic counterfactual realization under Baseline, I-only, V-only, T-only and Full.

No new pass threshold is introduced. The replication is interpreted jointly with the frozen 20-trace pilot under CN-R2-013. In particular, replication of I-dominant R realization would count against a universal R->T primary mapping and in favor of Authority-path displacement, not as support for R->T.
