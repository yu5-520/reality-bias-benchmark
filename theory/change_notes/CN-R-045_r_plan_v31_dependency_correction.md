# CN-R-045 — R Plan v3.1 dependency correction

Status: **ACCEPTED planning correction**  
Date: 2026-09-16  
Canonical forward plan: `docs/R_Plan_v3.1.md`

## 1. Decision

This Change Note corrects the forward research dependency graph without changing any frozen subject evidence, historical reviewer output, historical launch record or prior planning artifact.

The correction is:

> **R2-R8 are the structural/mechanistic subject-research program. R9 is supplementary robustness and external replication. R9 does not gate permission to continue R2-R8.**

The former `Paper Claim Mapping` function is moved outside the numbered R program into a separate Manuscript Claim–Evidence Matrix.

## 2. Why this correction is necessary

Earlier accepted governance already established two principles:

1. `CN-R2-023` made semantic adjudication a separate later operation over frozen evidence, with review failure forbidden from triggering a subject rerun.
2. `CN-R-024` stated that gates regulate claim strength rather than permission to observe a phenomenon.

Later planning and Reviewer-v2 work gradually placed independent cross-model semantic replication on the critical path before later structural experiments. `docs/R234_v2_current_gate.md` eventually described the state as requiring a different reviewer family while K=2 remained blocked.

That dependency was not required to preserve evidence integrity and had the side effect of shifting research effort from the structural Reality Bias program toward increasingly resource-intensive reviewer benchmarking and API engineering.

This note does **not** retroactively label that planning history as experimental Perfection Bias evidence. The history may be used only as a provenance-backed reflexive methodological illustration in supplementary material.

## 4. Corrected layer model

The program now separates three objects:

- **Structural emergence:** what happened in frozen subject trajectories.
- **Semantic identification:** how a reviewer classifies fixed structures as C/P/R, authorization, adoption, laundering, etc.
- **Measurement sensitivity:** how those semantic classifications change under different contracts, packets, reviewers or model families.

Reviewer disagreement changes the second/third layers. It does not erase the first layer.

An R9 result may still reveal a genuine upstream integrity defect, such as a hash mismatch, corrupt source record, wrong evidence binding or deterministic extraction bug. Such a defect is handled through the normal evidence audit process. Semantic disagreement alone is not an integrity defect.

## 5. Corrected R0-R9 responsibilities

- R0: theory contract and boundary definitions.
- R1: theory/measurement stress tests.
- R2: structural emergence and event-layer candidates.
- R3: propagation, lineage and penetration structure.
- R4: feedback, loop, laundering and black-hole dynamics.
- R5: causal interruption/control.
- R6: recovery/recurrence.
- R7: scoped boundary conditions and limited generalization.
- R8: reproducibility freeze.
- R9: supplementary robustness and external replication.

R9 includes multi-reviewer and multi-model semantic review, future human IRR, larger cross-model subject replication, heterogeneous agents, MCP/A2A, tools, memory/RAG, topology scaling and external laboratory reproduction.

## 6. K=2 correction

A different-family semantic Reviewer is no longer a prerequisite for launching R4 K=2.

K=2 still requires its own engineering/evidence-integrity readiness and, for a real paid subject run, explicit provider authorization and a spending ceiling. K=4 may remain gated by a preregistered R4-internal persistence/expansion/amplification criterion and cost policy, but not by cross-model Reviewer consensus.

## 7. Human–AI extension boundary

The current paper remains a Multi-Agent structural/mechanistic study. A separate observation is retained only as a future-work hypothesis: Perfection-like goal-scope or goal-focus drift may also occur in iterative Human–AI collaboration when locally reasonable, locally authorized expansions accumulate into global goal drift.

This is not part of R2-R8 evidence and must not be presented as cross-setting validation. It may appear briefly in Discussion/Future Work and in a supplementary reflexive note as hypothesis-generating material.
