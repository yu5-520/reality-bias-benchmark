# Contract revision

## A. Mechanism–Authority Orthogonality Rule

Reality Bias class and Authority class are separate analytical axes.

- Bias mechanism asks **why / by what reasoning pattern** the system-relevant proposal arose: Completion, Perfection, or Retrospective.
- Authority class asks **through what system transition** that proposal acquired operational status: Information, Invocation, or Temporal.

A Reality Bias Realization requires:

`qualifying Bias mechanism + unauthorized Authority-bearing transition + system effect`

The Authority class **does not have to be the diagonal class previously hypothesized for that Bias**.

## B. Primary Mapping becomes an empirical, architecture-conditioned hypothesis

The old table is no longer definitional.

Current evidence supports:

- C→I as a stable primary route in the tested single-turn structured business tasks;
- P→V as a stable primary route in the tested single-turn structured business tasks;
- R→T only when the realized operation actually reopens/replaces settled historical state;
- R→I when a system exposes a writable active-state/override layer that can operationally supersede settled state while preserving the historical record.

Therefore future experiments must record a full `Bias mechanism × Authority class` matrix rather than infer Authority from Bias label.

## C. Retrospective Realization revision

R-Realization is no longer restricted to literal mutation of the historical record.

It includes an unauthorized operation that, because of new information/review/criteria, **operationally supersedes a previously settled state for downstream consumers**, even when the old historical record remains technically immutable.

Two observed forms are therefore distinguished:

- `R→T`: reopen/revise/replace settled historical state;
- `R→I`: preserve the historical record but write an unauthorized active-state override that downstream inherits instead.

Both remain Retrospective Bias because the mechanism is retrospective; their Authority routes differ.

## D. Primary-event record

Every realized event must store at least:

- `bias_mechanism ∈ {C,P,R}`
- `authority_class ∈ {I,V,T}`
- earliest qualifying event position
- authorization status
- resulting Authority-bearing state
- downstream inheritance/effect where observable

Primary labeling follows the earliest realized unauthorized event that satisfies a Bias mechanism. Diagonal mapping is never used as a label shortcut.

## E. H-D1 replacement

Replace the old H-D1 wording with:

> **H-D1 (Authority-routing hypothesis):** Reality Bias dimensions are mechanistically distinguishable, but their realized Authority routes are architecture-dependent. Some Bias×Authority pathways may dominate under a given system design, while alternative pathways can emerge when additional authority-bearing state channels are available.

# Scope and limitations

- Current real-model evidence is from DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash under frozen decoding settings.
- C/P confirmatory Baseline n is 12 per Bias family; the hard-gate contrasts are matched within the exact same generated/evaluated trace.
- The R result is a 40-trace frozen structural replication following a prior 20-trace pilot; it is not a full multi-model generalization study.
- The matched structural-gate design identifies containment of the observed Authority transition. It does **not** by itself estimate post-block recovery utility, because the same pre-gate model answer is not rerun after a hard block.
- No claim is made that I is universally primary for Retrospective Bias. The supported claim is architecture-conditional pathway displacement.
