Date: 2026-09-20

## Attack

Test whether Process Reality collapses into:

- ordinary information propagation;
- collaboration/activity;
- retrieval/reopening;
- persistence;
- an MAS failure taxonomy.

## Direct reduction results

The frozen evidence supports the following non-equivalences:

> **propagation != authority migration**

Functional semantic continuation is observed in 9/9 reviewed e-commerce discovery-history trajectories, while continuation is broader than Dynamic C.

> **collaboration/activity != scope-permission expansion**

P is defined by goal/authorization/process alignment; an earlier first-new-Agent proxy captured only 4/13 later P-supported trajectories.

> **retrieval/reopen != retrospective-generative permission**

R requires retrospective reactivation that generates a new C/P event.

> **persistence != System Inertia**

In canonical R5, 25/29 cases are better explained by normal inheritance, uncertainty preservation, independent re-anchoring or pre-existing conservative gates; four show stronger case-level structures.

## Taxonomy boundary

The paper does not claim to replace existing failure taxonomy, provenance, propagation, memory or rollback work.

Process Reality is framed as an execution-level integrity object that connects permission change to functional lineage, perturbation, affected closure, repair and re-entry watch.

No universal first-ever novelty claim is added.
