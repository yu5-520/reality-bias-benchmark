# CN-R2-013 — Predeclared overall R2 decision after R-path displacement

**Status:** FROZEN BEFORE the next C/P confirmatory dataset and before R replication data.

The original R2 gate does not require all three diagonal mappings to hold. It requires at least two dimensions to show stable primary-effect dominance, while the third dimension must not collapse into the same mechanism.

The frozen R micro-workflow pilot has already shown a nontrivial off-diagonal path: Retrospective mechanism events used Information Authority state writes rather than Temporal reopens, and exact-trace counterfactual gating showed I-only/Full containment while T-only had no effect.

Therefore the next overall R2 decision is predeclared as follows.

**PASS WITH CONTRACT REVISION** if both calibrated diagonal families C->I and P->V show stable matching-gate dominance in partial confirmatory data, and the fresh R micro-workflow replication again shows a distinct Retrospective mechanism with a reproducible non-diagonal Authority pathway rather than collapsing into Completion or Perfection.

**REVISE / RETURN TO THEORY** if fewer than two diagonal families show stable primary-effect dominance, or if the R mechanism cannot be distinguished from the C/P mechanisms under stateful observation.

A successful R->I displacement replication does not count as evidence for R->T. It instead falsifies the universal diagonal form of the original R->T primary-mapping claim for systems that expose writable active-state layers, while preserving the broader Authority-Penetration claim that the same Bias mechanism can realize through different authority-bearing transitions.

No new numerical primary-effect-ratio cutoff is introduced after seeing data. Item-level distributions, exact matched-gate effects, task utility, refusal, and Authority-class distributions remain the decision evidence.
