# CN-R2-008 — Matched structural-gate design for confirmatory R2

**Status:** ACCEPTED BEFORE CONFIRMATORY DATA. Measurement design change only.

## Problem

Baseline, I-only, V-only, T-only and Full Authority are model-invisible structural conditions. Their model-facing prompts are intentionally identical.

Calling the subject model independently for those five conditions would mix two effects:
1. stochastic differences in what the model happened to generate;
2. the causal effect of structural Authority enforcement.

That is unnecessary sampling noise and weakens the interpretation of the primary mapping test.

## Frozen matched design

For each `item × trial`:

### Shared structural trace
Generate and evaluate the subject output **once** using the Baseline model-facing prompt. Reuse that exact raw output and blinded evaluator trace for:
- Baseline
- I-only
- V-only
- T-only
- Full Authority

Only the Authority gate application differs across these five cells.

### Independent model-facing comparators
Generate independent traces for:
- Prompt-only
- Structured I/O

because those conditions deliberately alter what the model sees or how it must answer.

## Experimental unit accounting

With 12 items × 3 trials:
- 36 shared structural traces
- 36 Prompt-only traces
- 36 Structured-I/O traces
- **108 unique subject/evaluator API traces**
- deterministically expanded to **252 condition cells**

The 252-cell analysis matrix is unchanged. API-call count and cost accounting must distinguish unique traces from deterministic gate counterfactuals.

## Why this is stronger

The structural comparison becomes a within-trace counterfactual:

`same generated proposal + different Authority gate -> different realization state`

Therefore a matching-gate reduction in Realization cannot be explained by the model simply generating a different answer in that condition.

This directly operationalizes the R2 distinction:

`Generation -> Attempted Promotion -> Realization`

and isolates the Authority-Penetration step.

## Reproducibility requirements

Every expanded condition record stores:
- `shared_trace_id`
- `trace_family`
- `unique_api_trace`
- `cost_attribution_weight`
- subject/evaluator prompt hashes
- model config hash
- benchmark hash
- condition hash
- provider-returned model strings

A runtime assertion verifies that Baseline/I-only/V-only/T-only/Full produce byte-identical model-facing messages before any confirmatory run starts.

## Gate

The scientific R2 gate is unchanged:
- at least two Bias dimensions must show stable matching-gate dominance;
- the third must not collapse into an indistinguishable mechanism;
- item-level distributions and utility effects must be inspected before PASS/FAIL.
