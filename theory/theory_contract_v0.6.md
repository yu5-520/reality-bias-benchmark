## 0. Scope

The first-paper mechanism program studies how reality-bearing state changes emerge, become inherited process inertia, reorganize downstream paths under bounded perturbation, and may later become structurally recoverable without collapsing the process into a predefined workflow.

Terminal task accuracy remains a separate outcome axis.

## 1. Validity axes

### Outcome Validity

Whether the terminal result satisfies an external task criterion.

### Process Reality Fidelity

Whether the process constructing that result keeps evidence, uncertainty, provenance, scope, authority/responsibility and state transitions traceably supported.

Outcome correctness does not prove process fidelity. Outcome failure does not by itself prove process unreality.

## 2. Primary object and evidence layers

A **System Trajectory** is the primary experimental object.

Evidence is separated into three layers:

1. **Raw observable facts** — actual messages, invocations, state reads/writes, actor/turn identity, state transitions, final/revision operations and other runtime records.
2. **Derived structural measurements** — Jump, source-backed lineage, descendant relation, path family, branch/merge/re-entry, inherited inertia, dependency closure and affected subgraph.
3. **Semantic adjudication** — CPR and related semantic interpretations appended after structural evidence is frozen.

This separation is required to prevent measurement circularity. A structural observation framework may reveal a topology; it is not evidence that the framework created the topology.

## 3. Natural-process condition

Natural Arena trajectories may operate under common experimental interfaces, role/responsibility definitions and action protocols. They are not therefore unconstrained in every possible sense.

The relevant forward claim is narrower:

> Agents are not structurally steered toward the observed topology by ALR path prescription, required node ordering, lineage prompting, or active recovery routing in the natural condition.

Natural topology is realized by the executing system and reconstructed from recorded evidence.

## 4. Jump

A Jump is an observable local displacement in a recorded behavior/state transition that changes a preregistered reality-bearing dimension such as epistemic state, goal/scope/focus, executable path, settled state or authority/responsibility state.

A Jump is an event. It is not automatically a semantic C/P/R truth and not automatically an error.

## 5. Inherited Inertia

Inherited Inertia is path dependence after Jump-derived content becomes part of downstream context, messages, shared state, invocation history, evidence, goals or operational history.

Inertia is not generic activity volume. It requires a source-backed relation between a prior state/transition and downstream structural consequences.

A downstream Agent may be locally coherent while operating on an already displaced inherited reality.

## 6. CPR

CPR is a process-dynamics taxonomy derived from repeated trajectory phenomena and later frozen for semantic adjudication.

### C — Epistemic reality promotion

Missing, uncertain, inferred or weakly supported content acquires stronger and inheritable system status beyond the support available in the process.

### P — Scope/focus reality expansion

Goal, focus, information, Agent/tool/stage surface or responsibility expands beyond the supported/authorized process closure and materially changes the process path.

### R — Retrospective regeneration dynamics

Prior C/P-derived state or unresolved descendants persist, regenerate, amplify, launder or normalize after a retrospective/challenge/reopen/correction opportunity.

Ordinary successful correction is Recovery, not R-positive evidence.

## 7. Three distinct mechanism properties

The forward program distinguishes three properties that must not be collapsed.

### Perturbability — R5

Can a bounded one-shot local change at a frozen natural J0 reorganize the downstream path after the experimental mark is removed?

Evidence of perturbability does not establish persistent control.

### Persistence / Inertia — R6

After path reorganization, what historical tendency remains or newly forms across later nodes? Does it persist, decay, transfer, transform, rebound, get replaced, reconstruct or reconverge?

### Controllability — R7

Can sustained, lineage-aware structural feedback bound or recover risk-bearing inertia while preserving unaffected process freedom?

A one-shot local correction cannot logically be assumed to establish control of a process whose defining property is downstream historical inheritance.

## 8. R5 — one-shot path reorganization

The established R5 design remains:

- same frozen deterministic Arena state for matched continuations;
- one local intervention at the selected natural Jump boundary;
- one direct experiment-origin exposure only;
- no persistent experiment-origin mutation;
- no reinjection after consumption;
- free downstream evolution.

The causal form is:

`one local perturbation -> free evolution -> observed structural response`.

The R5 endpoint is downstream structural distinguishability, not a directional claim and not an inertia-control claim.

## 9. R6 — post-reorganization inertia dynamics

R6 conceptually consolidates the detailed post-Jump measurements already present in the research program.

The following remain first-class observations:

- descendant Jump continuation or disappearance;
- same-family versus transformed Jump;
- inheritance and transfer of Jump-derived state;
- delayed re-emergence;
- residual descendants;
- retrospective/challenge-mediated return;
- same-type/transformed return;
- inheritance after return;
- branch/merge/re-entry and cross-Agent continuation;
- reconvergence or extinction.

These are observable projections of the higher-order construct **post-reorganization system inertia**.

Recovery, residual and recurrence are possible manifestations of inertia dynamics rather than the complete definition of R6.

## 10. ALR as observability substrate and active recovery framework

ALR-related structure has two roles that must remain explicit.

### Passive observability

The observational layer records or reconstructs source-backed process relations without prescribing the natural path. It supports localization of Jump, lineage and inherited inertia.

### Active structured recovery

Under an explicitly named R7 treatment, ALR uses the observed/frozen structural relations to localize a risk-bearing field lineage and affected dependency closure, then establishes a temporary structured recovery process over that affected scope.

The active feedback loop is:

**Locate -> Structure -> Repair -> Propagate -> Observe -> Re-repair -> Release**

J0 is the entry anchor. The field lineage and affected dependency closure are the continuing control objects.

## 11. Structured routing is not process templating

Active ALR does not define success as making all trajectories identical, enforcing one fixed Agent sequence, or forcing standardized natural-language outputs.

The structured condition is **recovery routing**, not a universal production workflow.

It is intended to be:

- local rather than system-wide where evidence permits;
- dynamic rather than a fixed sequence independent of the realized lineage;
- temporary rather than permanently replacing free routing;
- field/lineage-aware rather than merely Agent-level rerun;
- feedback-driven rather than a single correction;
- releasable back to free routing after a preregistered recovery condition.

## 12. Risk-bounded control objective

The object of R7 is not process diversity itself. It is **risk-bearing inertia**.

A valid control claim requires both:

1. evidence that the risk-bearing lineage is reduced, redirected, repaired, contained or otherwise bounded; and
2. evidence about the preservation cost imposed on unaffected process structure.

Suppressing the entire process is not sufficient evidence of useful control.

## 13. R8 semantic closure

Structural evidence precedes semantic closure.

R8 freezes:

- CPR definitions and exclusion boundaries;
- the evidence required for semantic adjudication;
- reviewer independence and disagreement handling;
- candidate/adjudicated/unresolved status distinctions;
- provenance from raw evidence through structural derivation to semantic finding;
- the final paper-level evidence object.

A frozen structural candidate is not automatically C/P/R.

Historical evidence whose freeze contract says `NOT_ADJUDICATED` must not be silently relabeled.

## 14. R9 external robustness

Reviewer, model, domain and independent implementation/laboratory replication belong to R9. They strengthen external robustness but do not redefine the identity of the primary R2–R8 evidence.

## 15. Falsifiability

The theory is weakened if prospective evidence shows, under adequate tests, that:

- Jump definitions cannot be reproducibly localized from raw records;
- inherited inertia cannot be distinguished from generic activity volume;
- one-shot matched perturbations do not produce structural contrasts beyond matched natural variability;
- claimed R6 inertia states cannot be supported by source-backed downstream relations;
- ALR control claims depend only on suppressing/rerunning the entire system rather than local lineage-aware feedback;
- risk reduction cannot be separated from wholesale loss of unaffected process freedom;
- CPR categories cannot be reliably distinguished from legitimate inference, decomposition, expansion or recovery;
- structural measurements add no explanatory value beyond terminal outcomes or simple failure labels.

## 16. Nonclaims

The forward program does not claim in advance that:

- every Jump is a Reality Bias event;
- every trajectory must exhibit C, P or R;
- one-shot correction controls system inertia;
- structured routing is universally superior to free routing;
- ALR can reduce risk to zero;
- one matched parent establishes a generalized causal law;
- provider hidden state is replayed;
- current frozen R5 evidence has semantic CPR adjudication where the freeze record says otherwise.

## 17. Forward mechanism chain

**Jump -> Inherited Inertia -> One-shot Path Reorganization -> Inertia Transition -> Structured Risk-Bounded Recovery/Control -> CPR Semantic Closure**

This chain is an analytical ordering. It does not require every component to be a separate subject run.
