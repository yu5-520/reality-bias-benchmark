## 1. Primary contribution architecture

### C1 — Process Reality / Dynamic CPR

**Type:** theory / mechanism  
**Status:** CORE / SUPPORTED AT MECHANISM LEVEL

The first paper operationalizes changing process permission across:

- C — what may be treated as real;
- P — what may be acted on / how far scope may expand;
- R — what historical process reality may act again.

- prevalence;
- universal MAS failure theory;
- universal system-level generality.
