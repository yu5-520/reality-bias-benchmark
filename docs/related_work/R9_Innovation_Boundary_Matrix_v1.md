## Strong boundary conclusions

### A. Strongest defensible core

The paper's most defensible combined contribution is not any single primitive in isolation.

It is the **integration and empirical operationalization** of:

`dynamic process-reality permissions + functional semantic lineage + trajectory-first audit + localized lineage-addressed control`

over frozen natural, intervention and recovery trajectories.

### B. Claims that must be narrowed because of 2026 frontier work

Because F1 exists, do not claim novelty for:

- persistence vs support separation;
- dependency provenance;
- epistemic role promotion;
- source-independence logic.

Because F2 exists, do not claim novelty for:

- task-scoped execution boundaries;
- delegated authority as a runtime concept;
- transactional rollback/recovery/audit.

The paper's difference is the **observed dynamic multi-Agent semantic mechanism and its lineage-level process interpretation/control**, not ownership of those general concepts.

### C. Publication wording recommendation

Strongest safe contribution wording:

> We study multi-Agent execution as a dynamic semantic process rather than only a communication graph or terminal outcome. We operationalize information, collaboration/execution and temporal permission changes over complete trajectories, trace functional semantic lineage across transformed carriers, and demonstrate localized lineage-addressed intervention on frozen process evidence.

No `first-ever` wording is authorized by R9 v1.
