## 5. Why three matched triads

The first specificity batch freezes **three matched triads / nine branches**.

This is not a statistical-power claim and does not turn same-parent repeats into independent population samples.

Three triads are used because three conditions allow a complete cyclic order rotation with the smallest balanced repetition:

| Replicate | First | Second | Third |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | S0 | S1 | S2 |
| 2 | S1 | S2 | S0 |
| 3 | S2 | S0 | S1 |

Each condition therefore appears once in each execution position.

The role of the design is to reduce a simple order-position confound while producing repeated realizations from one fixed origin.
