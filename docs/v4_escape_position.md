# Escape Propensity Position under Reality Bias v4

Date: 2026-09-16

Escape Propensity remains a latent tendency rather than a directly observed internal variable.

Forward evidence should distinguish:

- antecedent manipulation;
- observable Jump realization;
- downstream system consequences.

Behavior-level repeated-branch estimates may approximate Jump hazard under frozen conditions, but must not be relabeled as direct neural/internal escape probability.
