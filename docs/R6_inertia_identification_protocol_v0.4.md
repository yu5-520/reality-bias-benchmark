Date: 2026-09-17  
Status: FORWARD / MAIN MECHANISM IDENTIFICATION / METHODOLOGICAL CLOSURE  
Predecessor: `docs/R6_inertia_identification_protocol_v0.3.md`

## 1. Purpose

R6 is the evidence pivot of the first paper. It identifies whether later process behavior reflects ordinary inheritance, an inertia-bearing prior commitment, natural variation, an intervention-related transition, or a frozen-target response contrast.

## 2. Ordinary inheritance baseline

Multi-Agent collaboration normally carries information forward. A read, repeated value or descendant relation is not by itself inertia.

## 3. Inertia candidate criterion

An inertia candidate requires source-backed evidence that a prior epistemic status, commitment, constraint or action tendency continues to shape later process options, transition propensity or downstream state after the originating event has passed.

Stronger evidence records persistence after a relevant challenge/downgrade/withdrawal opportunity.

## 4. Carrier chain

Canonical evidence chain:

`source -> carrier -> delivered/read -> semantic use/adoption -> inherited state/action -> propagated descendant`

Stop at the highest supported level. Do not infer missing semantic links.

## 5. Three-domain comparison

R6 preserves separate vectors for:

- structural geometry;
- information inheritance;
- epistemic authority.

A large difference in one domain does not imply a large difference in another.

## 6. R6-C intervention-related transition

The treated inertia window begins after experiment-origin exposure is consumed. Compare post-consumption behavior against natural within-condition variability.

No monotonic complexity direction is assumed.

## 7. R6-D frozen target contrast

Primary contrast remains `S2 - S1`.

First-level interpretation: `FROZEN_TARGET_RESPONSE_CONTRAST`.

Escape-derived specificity is a stronger interpretation that requires pre-outcome target comparability evidence or append-only ordinary-fact robustness controls.

## 8. Conditional effect boundary

The frozen same-parent design identifies a conditional effect under the exact historical prefix. Generalization requires later replication across prefixes/tasks/models/topologies.

## 9. Endogenous carrier rule

The experiment does not persistently mutate subject state. However, subject-generated state/messages/invocations after exposure are valid endogenous outcomes and possible inertia carriers.

## 10. R7 handoff object

R6 should hand R7:

- root/source ref;
- carrier relation evidence;
- potential closure;
- evidence-supported affected set;
- unresolved/censored regions;
- risk-condition id if separately defined.

R6 must not automatically convert potential reachability into the repair set.

## 11. Semantic boundary

R6 may nominate semantic review candidates but does not adjudicate CPR.
