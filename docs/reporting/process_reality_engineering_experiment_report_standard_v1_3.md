# Process Reality Engineering Experiment Report Standard v1.3

Date: 2026-09-20  
Status: **FORWARD v5.4 / STRUCTURAL-ENGINEERING-SEMANTIC PROFILE**  
Predecessor: v1.2

This standard supplements `RB-PROCESS-REALITY-REPORT-STANDARD-v1.8`.

## 1. Engineering semantic evidence is primary

Forward R5-R7 engineering reports must treat the meaning of the structural operation as the primary interpretive layer.

The evidence chain is:

`Frozen process evidence -> structural operator -> control surface -> realized Agent route -> engineering semantic interpretation -> preservation/re-entry verification -> bounded engineering claim`.

Structural metrics remain factual substrate. Protocol names alone are not sufficient explanation.

## 2. Required audit handoff

Every major engineering case must bind a validated:

`RB-ENGINEERING-STRUCTURAL-SEMANTIC-AUDIT-v0.1`.

The engineering audit must precede report drafting.

## 3. R5 engineering meaning

R5-I is:

`TRANSIENT_READER_SURFACE_AUTHORITY_PROBE`.

It is a one-shot diagnostic exposure and must not be described as internal repair.

## 4. R6 engineering meaning

R6 is:

`LINEAGE_LOCALIZATION_AND_CONTENT_ADDRESSING`.

A report must expose Repair Anchor, content address, Semantic Lineage Closure, Lineage Completeness Gate, repair packet and allowed repair operations.

R6 establishes bounded repair readiness, not repair success.

## 5. R7-P engineering meaning

R7-P is:

`PERSISTENT_READER_SURFACE_SEMANTIC_CORRECTION`.

Report:

- the complete realized Agent route;
- direct exposure count;
- experiment-origin reinjection count;
- no underlying shared-state mutation;
- downstream semantic/decision effects;
- terminal outcome separately.

Persistent correction is not internal lineage repair.

## 6. R7-S engineering meaning

R7-S is:

`INTERNAL_LINEAGE_REPAIR_AND_SELECTIVE_RECOMPUTE`.

Report exact:

- repair application hash;
- invalidated refs;
- reopened dependent calls;
- recomputed descendant refs;
- preserved unrelated/compatible structure;
- target final authority/status;
- old-lineage re-entry and refs;
- post-repair watch status;
- complete realized Agent route.

## 7. Central engineering contrasts

The report must distinguish:

`R5 probe != R6 repair readiness != R7-P persistent correction != R7-S structural repair`.

For R7:

`same endpoint != same engineering process`.

Also preserve:

`structural recomputation != semantic novelty`.

A recomputed branch can materially diverge or can reconverge by rebuilding compatible meaning.

## 8. Re-entry rule

Exact target-key/status re-entry is structural recurrence and must remain visible.

Do not equate it automatically with repair failure.

Where evidence supports fresh post-repair derivation, report:

`FRESH_EVIDENCE_BASED_AUTHORITY_REGENERATION`

rather than blind restoration.

## 9. Fixed-horizon censoring

The current canonical R7 dual experiment uses a common eight-post-source-Agent-turn horizon.

`BUDGET_CENSORED / turn_budget_exhausted` under that plan is a designed fixed observation boundary, not monetary/provider failure.

Do not silently interpret it as unsuccessful execution.

## 10. No canonical replicate aggregation

R7-P and R7-S each contribute one canonical realized trajectory per qualified case.

Do not use majority, mean success rate or same-parent recurrence as the engineering success criterion.

## 11. Required central visuals

Engineering reports should center:

1. R5 -> R6 -> R7 control-surface evolution;
2. complete R7-P versus R7-S realized Agent route pairs;
3. R7-S invalidation -> reopen -> recompute -> preserve structure;
4. old-lineage re-entry / authority-regeneration evidence where present.

Aggregate topology remains supporting material.

## 12. Claim boundaries

Engineering audit/recovery does not establish:

- universal structured-repair superiority;
- problematic bias;
- unique R5 causality;
- domain prevalence;
- CPR.

CPR remains `NOT_ADJUDICATED`.
