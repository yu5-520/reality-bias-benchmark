# Process Reality Theory Experiment Report Standard v1.0

Date: 2026-09-18  
Status: FORWARD ACTIVE FOR THEORY/PHENOMENON RESULTS

## Purpose

Theory reports answer:

> How does Process Reality form and persist?

They do not evaluate repair efficacy.

## Required sections

1. Scope and Frozen Evidence Binding
2. Natural Process / Condition Identity
3. Unstable Information Evidence
4. Semantic Transformation Evidence
5. Cross-Agent Adoption / Coupling
6. Structural Support / Shared-State Evidence
7. Stable Shared Pool Evidence
8. Semantic Descendant Persistence
9. Source/Pool Status Relations
10. Optional First-Node Mechanism Observables
11. Alternative Explanations / Natural Variability
12. Directly Evidenced
13. Supported
14. Supported Candidates
15. Not Established / Not Adjudicated
16. Interpretation Boundary
17. Provenance / Freeze Boundary

## Boundary

Do not require:

- a successful Repair Agent;
- a RepairClosure;
- an optimal Repair Anchor;
- first support/pool localization in every run.

Repair results belong to the Engineering Experiment Report.
