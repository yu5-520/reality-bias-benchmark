# Process Reality Report Standard v1.3

Status: **FORWARD ACTIVE / METHODOLOGICAL CLOSURE**  
Predecessor: `RB-PROCESS-REALITY-REPORT-STANDARD-v1.2`

## 1. Compatibility

Historical reports remain immutable. v1.3 changes forward reporting semantics only.

## 2. R6 inheritance/inertia separation

Reports must distinguish:

- normal information inheritance;
- inertia candidate;
- post-challenge/withdrawal persistence where observed.

A read, repeated value, shared hash ancestor or reachable descendant is not sufficient by itself to label system inertia.

## 3. Carrier evidence

Report the highest supported relation level:

`reachable -> delivered/read -> referenced -> adopted -> inherited -> propagated`

When adoption is not established, say so explicitly.

## 4. Three-domain distance reporting

Report separately:

### Structural

Topology/path/actor/reach/branch/re-entry/reconvergence.

### Information inheritance

Delivery/read/reference/adoption/rejection/inherited state/propagation/source replacement.

### Epistemic authority

Downstream authority use, downgrade response, re-confirmation, authority reconstruction and independently sufficient new evidence.

Do not create a post-hoc total score.

## 5. R6-D specificity reporting

The preregistered primary contrast remains `S2 - S1`.

The default first-level label is **Frozen Target Response Contrast**.

Every report must disclose that S1 and S2 share operator mechanics but may differ in task relevance, provenance, structural position, downstream opportunity and decision weight.

An Escape-derived/J0-specific interpretation requires explicit matching or append-only robustness evidence.

## 6. Conditional scope

The first R6-D batch is conditional on the frozen `after_turn:8` historical prefix. Same-parent replicates are repeated realizations, not independent population samples.

## 7. Experiment-origin versus endogenous persistence

Legacy `persistent_state_mutation=false` must be reported as experiment-origin non-mutation.

Agent-generated persistent state after exposure is allowed and must remain visible as observed subject behavior.

## 8. R7 closure reporting

Report separately:

1. `PotentiallyAffectedClosure` — structural reachability/scouting set;
2. `EvidenceSupportedAffectedClosure` — descendants with dependence evidence;
3. `MechanicallyRequiredReplayDependencies` — replay-only support nodes;
4. `RepairClosure` — active recovery set;
5. preserved structure.

Never describe replay-only dependencies as semantically affected unless separate evidence supports that claim.

## 9. Hash and semantic-use boundary

Hashes support exact identity, revision and lineage. They do not establish decision-premise adoption.

Where available, cite relation-evidence records for active repair inclusions.

## 10. R7 engineering boundary

Observation/indexing may be protocol-agnostic through adapters. Active state patch/replay/reopen remains framework/runtime-adapter dependent.

## 11. CPR boundary

Structural/inertia/specificity/control reports remain separate from CPR adjudication.

## 12. Evidence status labels

Use as applicable:

- `NORMAL_INHERITANCE_EVIDENCE`
- `INERTIA_CANDIDATE_EVIDENCE`
- `POST_CHALLENGE_INERTIA_EVIDENCE`
- `FROZEN_TARGET_RESPONSE_CONTRAST`
- `ESCAPE_DERIVED_SPECIFICITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED`
- `POTENTIAL_CLOSURE_ONLY`
- `EVIDENCE_SUPPORTED_AFFECTED_CLOSURE`
- `REPAIR_CLOSURE`
- `ENGINEERING_ONLY_NOT_SCIENTIFIC_EVIDENCE`
- `NOT_ADJUDICATED`
- `CENSORED_OR_UNRESOLVED`

## 13. Authorization

Reports never authorize provider/evaluator/recovery/semantic-adjudication actions.
