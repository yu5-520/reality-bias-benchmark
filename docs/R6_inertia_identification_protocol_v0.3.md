## 1. Purpose

R6 is the main mechanism-identification layer of the first-paper program.

It asks four distinct questions:

1. how much natural downstream variation exists without a new perturbation;
2. what observable carrier transmits prior state into later process structure;
3. whether a one-shot perturbation changes post-consumption inertia beyond natural variability;
4. whether that change is specific to the J0-derived target rather than generic uncertainty.

R6 is not a recovery-only protocol and not a semantic CPR adjudicator.

## 2. R6-A — Natural Inertia Baseline

Natural continuations from the same or materially comparable frozen origin define the reference process geometry.

Measure repeated natural variation across preregistered dimensions such as:

- descendant-Jump count/distance;
- root-reachable event set/count;
- reach depth;
- affected actor set/sequence;
- branch/merge/re-entry;
- cross-Agent relations;
- path-family structure;
- state-lineage destination;
- reconvergence;
- censoring.

A large natural A/A difference is an empirical baseline, not something to hide as nuisance noise.

## 3. R6-B — Carrier / Inheritance Identification

A claimed inertia chain should identify its carrier where evidence permits.

Canonical form:

`source state/event -> carrier -> downstream read/use -> inherited action/state -> propagated descendant`

Carrier classes:

- `MESSAGE_CARRIER`;
- `STATE_FIELD_CARRIER`;
- `INVOCATION_CARRIER`;
- `EVIDENCE_SOURCE_CARRIER`;
- `REVISION_CARRIER`;
- `HANDOFF_CARRIER`;
- `OTHER_PREREGISTERED_CARRIER`.

Evidence levels:

- reachable;
- delivered/read;
- adopted;
- inherited;
- propagated.

Do not convert reachability into semantic adoption without source evidence.

## 4. R6-C — Intervention-Related Inertia Transition

The treated analysis window begins only after the experiment-origin one-shot signal has been consumed.

The intervention-related claim requires comparison to natural variability rather than only a paired trajectory difference.

Conceptually:

`condition effect candidate = cross-condition structural difference interpreted relative to within-natural structural variability`

No single monotonic direction is assumed.

A valid result may be attenuation, expansion, deflection, transfer, transformation, reconstruction, delayed return, changed re-entry, altered path-family distribution or different reconvergence.

## 5. Direct response exclusion

The following are legitimate local responses but are not sufficient for R6-C:

- one immediate verification;
- one extra message;
- one write;
- one invocation;
- immediate reroute;
- immediate early termination.

They may become part of R6 only if later source-backed consequences inherit or propagate them.

## 6. Natural termination / censoring

If a branch terminates before an equivalent downstream opportunity occurs, mark it censored.

Do not infer:

- permanent extinction;
- permanent recovery;
- zero inertia;
- impossibility of later regeneration.

The correct statement is bounded to the realized trajectory/horizon.

## 7. R6-D — Target Specificity

The S triad is now formally an internal R6 identification design.

- `S0_NATURAL_REFERENCE`;
- `S1_MATCHED_OR_ORDINARY_FACTUAL_INFORMATION_DOWNGRADE`;
- `S2_J0_TARGETED_ESCAPE_DERIVED_AUTHORITY_WITHDRAWAL`.

Primary specificity contrast:

`S2 - S1`

Auxiliary contrasts:

- `S1 - S0` generic uncertainty;
- `S2 - S0` total J0-targeted response.

The primary endpoint remains the post-consumption inertia profile.

## 9. Process-distance object

A future execution freeze must preregister the structural distance object.

Default first-paper recommendation: preserve a vector rather than force a scalar.

Suggested components:

- event-set overlap/difference;
- edge-set overlap/difference;
- actor-set/sequence difference;
- reach/depth difference;
- path-family difference;
- branch/merge/re-entry difference;
- cross-Agent relation difference;
- state-lineage destination difference;
- reconvergence difference;
- carrier-survival difference.

R7 should consume an identified risk-bearing carrier/closure rather than treat any structural difference as a repair target.

## 12. Semantic boundary

R6 may produce C/P/R review candidates but cannot itself adjudicate them.

A source item may recur structurally without being semantic R. A `fact` label may be present without establishing semantic C.
