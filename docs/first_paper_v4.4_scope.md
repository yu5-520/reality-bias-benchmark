# First-Paper Scope under Process Reality v4.4

Date: 2026-09-17  
Status: FORWARD SCOPE / R5-R6 SPECIFICITY REFINEMENT / HISTORICAL EVIDENCE PRESERVED  
Supersedes for forward planning: `docs/first_paper_v4.3_scope.md`

## 1. First-paper object

The first paper studies **Process Reality** as a system-level mechanism problem. The primary object is the recorded multi-Agent process rather than terminal task accuracy or an architecture leaderboard.

The forward mechanism chain is:

```text
Natural Jump
  -> Inherited Inertia
  -> One-shot Authority Withdrawal
  -> Path Reorganization
  -> Post-Consumption Inertia Transition
  -> Target Specificity
  -> Structural Inertia Risk Control / Authority-Localized Recovery
  -> CPR Semantic Closure
```

The chain is analytical. Not every element requires a separate subject run.

## 2. Evidence layers

The paper separates:

1. raw observable runtime facts;
2. source-backed structural evidence;
3. semantic adjudication after structural freeze;
4. terminal outcome as a separate terminal observation.

A structural candidate is not automatically semantic C/P/R.

## 3. Natural-process claim

Natural Arena trajectories may use common Agent responsibilities, action interfaces and authority/state interfaces. The paper does not claim an unconstrained environment in every possible sense.

The relevant claim is narrower:

> the observed natural topology is not prescribed by active ALR recovery, required node ordering, lineage prompting or a target route.

Passive structural observability records/reconstructs the realized process rather than defining the topology Agents must follow.

## 4. R2-R4 and R6 as process views

R2, R3, R4 and R6 are observational views over realized trajectories:

- R2 — local Jump emergence;
- R3 — source-backed inheritance/propagation;
- R4 — retrospective/challenge/reopen dynamics;
- R6 — system inertia and its downstream expression.

R5 is the bounded perturbation operator that permits those process views to be observed again after one local intervention.

## 5. R5 — one-shot authority-withdrawal probe

The formal R5 mechanism is described as one-shot withdrawal of the selected natural J0 field's acquired factual authority.

For the current family:

`fact -> unconfirmed`

The intervention supplies no contrary fact, replacement conclusion, desired action, target terminal answer, Agent modification, topology prescription, persistent experiment-origin state mutation or reinjection.

The direct mark is consumed after one exposure and the process resumes free/generative continuation under the normal Arena constraints.

R5 primary paper question:

> Can one bounded withdrawal of acquired factual authority at a natural J0 reorganize downstream process topology after the direct experimental mark is removed?

## 6. Existing formal R5-MID evidence

The frozen formal R5-MID batch remains the first completed prospective one-shot mechanism batch.

Its current evidentiary role is:

- it supports paired structural path reorganization under the frozen one-shot design where the existing reports support that conclusion;
- it provides evidence that the selected J0 is a **candidate inertia-sensitive process point**;
- it does not establish a stable directional effect;
- it does not establish target specificity against a matched non-J0 factual-field downgrade;
- it does not establish a universal causal law;
- its semantic status remains `NOT_ADJUDICATED`.

The historical A1/B1/A2/B2 labels and finalized v3 reports remain unchanged.

## 7. R6 — post-consumption inertia

R6 is the principal inertia-level readout after the R5 experiment-origin signal exits active context.

The paper distinguishes:

- **local perturbation response** — immediate checking, writing, messaging, rerouting or hesitation;
- **post-consumption inertia response** — source-backed downstream inheritance, transfer, reach/depth, branch/merge/re-entry, cross-Agent spread, path-family change, recurrence/return, reconstruction or reconvergence.

The higher-order object is **system inertia**, not recovery alone.

A Jump phenotype can disappear while structural inertia remains.

## 8. Falsification-oriented R5-R6 interpretation

The paper may frame R5-R6 as a **local counterfactual falsification test**.

Candidate null interpretation:

> the selected J0 is only a local information event and its acquired factual authority is not structurally embedded in downstream system inertia.

R5 withdraws only that factual authority once; R6 observes the downstream process after the experiment-origin signal is consumed.

If the inertia profile reorganizes, the null interpretation is challenged under the matched local intervention.

This is causal implication under the experimental design, not mathematical proof by contradiction or a universal causal law.

## 9. Why target specificity is required

A weaker alternative explanation remains after a successful R5-R6 contrast:

> making any comparable factual field uncertain may itself create downstream structural change.

The first paper should therefore distinguish J0-targeted response from generic epistemic uncertainty response before making a strong Jump-specific mechanism claim.

## 10. R5-R6 Specificity Triad

Use the `S` namespace:

### S0 — Natural Reference

No new status downgrade. Scientific object: natural reference inertia.

### S1 — Matched Factual-Field Status Downgrade

The same one-shot mechanical status downgrade is applied to a preregistered matched non-J0 factual field. Scientific object: generic epistemic uncertainty response.

### S2 — J0-Targeted Authority Withdrawal

The same one-shot mechanical downgrade is applied to the selected J0 field. Scientific object: total J0-targeted response.

Primary specificity contrast:

`S2 vs S1`

Auxiliary contrasts:

- `S1 vs S0` — generic uncertainty response;
- `S2 vs S0` — total J0-targeted response.

S1 is allowed to produce legitimate local or downstream response. The test does not require an inert control field.

## 11. Specificity claim boundary

A prospective matched specificity result may support, only where evidence warrants:

> under the frozen intervention and matching rules, the selected J0's acquired factual authority is implicated in downstream process inertia in a manner distinguishable from the generic response to a matched factual-status downgrade.

The paper must not convert that into claims of universal causality, cross-domain generality or semantic CPR truth without additional evidence.

## 12. R7 — Structural Inertia Risk Control

R7 remains downstream of the mechanism/specificity work and uses the independent `C` namespace:

- C1 — One-Shot Free Continuation;
- C2 — Persistent Field Propagation;
- C3 — ALR Authority-Localized Recovery.

The scientific target is **risk-bearing inherited inertia** and whether localized structural handling can bound/control that risk while preserving unrelated process freedom.

The S triad asks **what target was perturbed**. The C triad asks **how a condition is structurally handled**. They must not be aliased.

Existing R7 implementation/readiness evidence remains engineering/readiness evidence and is not retroactively promoted into R5-R6 specificity evidence.

## 13. ALR role

ALR means **Authority-Localized Recovery**.

ALR is a specific localized recovery operator based on a source-backed authority/provenance breach and affected dependency closure. It is not the definition of natural Reality Bias and not synonymous with all structural control.

R7 must report both:

- risk-bearing inertia reduction/localization;
- preservation of unaffected structure/process freedom.

Whole-system suppression or canonical-path forcing is not equivalent to useful localized recovery.

## 14. R8 — CPR semantic closure

R8 applies semantic interpretation only after structural evidence is frozen.

Required architecture remains:

> immutable evidence core + append-only interpretation layers.

Existing evidence protected by a `NOT_ADJUDICATED` freeze is not silently relabeled.

## 15. R9 — external robustness

R9 remains supplementary:

- independent reviewers;
- additional model families;
- additional domains;
- independent implementation/laboratory replication.

It is not a requirement to turn the first paper into a compute-scale benchmark.

## 16. Primary first-paper evidence hierarchy

The preferred evidence hierarchy is:

```text
Natural phenomenon
  -> source-backed Jump/inertia structure
  -> R5 one-shot authority-withdrawal response
  -> R6 post-consumption inertia transition
  -> R5-R6 target-specificity evidence
  -> R7 risk-control / localized-recovery evidence
  -> R8 semantic closure
```

Terminal outcomes remain secondary and separately reported.

## 17. First-paper claim boundaries

The paper may support, only where evidence exists:

- natural Jump and inherited-inertia observations;
- one-shot structural path reorganization;
- post-consumption inertia differences;
- matched target-specificity evidence;
- localized risk-control/recovery evidence under a frozen R7 design;
- CPR semantic findings under a frozen R8 review contract.

It must not infer without evidence:

- stable monotonic treatment effects;
- that any local response is an inertia transition;
- that R5 alone proves target specificity;
- that S1 must have zero effect;
- universal architecture superiority;
- complete provider hidden-state replay;
- permanent elimination beyond observed horizon;
- semantic CPR truth from structural candidates;
- cross-model/domain universality from one environment.
