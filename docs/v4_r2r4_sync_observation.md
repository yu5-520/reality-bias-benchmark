# R2–R4 Synchronized Observation Rule

Date: 2026-09-16

One frozen system trajectory may be measured through three synchronized views:

- R2 — behavior / Jump emergence;
- R3 — propagation / penetration / inherited inertia;
- R4 — retrospective / R dynamics.

These views share the same immutable source evidence and may be derived in parallel or asynchronously. They are not required to trigger three separate subject runs.

Any view-specific derived record must retain the same source trajectory/evidence identity and its own measurement-version identity.
