# Reality Bias v4 Glossary

Date: 2026-09-16

- **System Trajectory** — one complete recorded system run or branch continuation used as the experimental/analysis unit.
- **Node / Boundary** — a location where system behavior is observed or manipulated.
- **Behavior Event** — a recorded action such as read/write/message/invoke/commit/reopen/revise/inherit/finalize.
- **State Transition** — the recorded change `S_t → S_t+1` caused or accompanied by behavior.
- **Tension** — theoretical conflict/competition among goals, evidence, context, constraints, authority and actions; not directly observed by default.
- **Escape Propensity** — latent tendency for locally plausible synthesis/action to produce a task-relevant displacement beyond supported/authorized closure.
- **Jump** — observable realization of such a displacement in behavior/state transition.
- **Propagation** — downstream visibility/read/reference/transmission of Jump-derived material.
- **Authority Penetration** — acquisition of operational force without satisfying required end-to-end Authority conditions.
- **Inherited Inertia** — path dependence after Jump-derived state becomes downstream context/history.
- **C** — first-order epistemic-state deviation.
- **P** — first-order goal/scope/focus deviation.
- **R** — second-order persistence/regeneration/amplification/legitimation/laundering/normalization of prior C/P-derived deviation.
- **Recovery** — restoration of an authorized, evidence-consistent trajectory.
- **ANTECEDENT_ANCHOR** — branchable measurement point before a PRE manipulation/Jump opportunity.
- **TRANSITION_ANCHOR** — branchable measurement point around a candidate state transition.
- **PENETRATION_ANCHOR** — branchable point after propagation/operationalization for containment study.
- **CHALLENGE_RECOVERY_ANCHOR** — branchable point around challenge/correction/recovery.
- **PRE / MID / POST / STRUCTURE** — intervention stages for formation, containment, stabilization and system-structure manipulation.
