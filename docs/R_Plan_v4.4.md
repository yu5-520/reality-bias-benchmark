# Reality Bias Research Plan v4.4

Date: 2026-09-17  
Status: FORWARD / R5-R6 SPECIFICITY REFINEMENT  
Predecessor: `docs/R_Plan_v4.3.md`

## 1. Version decision

v4.4 preserves all historical plans, contracts, frozen evidence, reports, manifests and reviewer records. It does not relabel the formal R5-MID batch, does not modify its `NOT_ADJUDICATED` semantic status, and authorizes no provider or evaluator call.

This revision clarifies the relation between R5 and R6 before any further R7 subject execution:

- **R5 is the bounded experimental probe axis**: withdraw the acquired factual authority of a selected natural J0 once, without supplying a replacement answer, persistent mutation or downstream instruction.
- **R6 is the principal inertia-level readout axis**: after the experiment-origin signal exits active context, measure what inherited tendency persists, disappears, transforms, transfers, returns, reconstructs or reconverges.
- A new **R5-R6 specificity layer** separates J0-targeted inertia response from a generic response to uncertainty or status downgrade.
- The R5-R6 specificity namespace is `S0/S1/S2`; the existing R7 structural-control namespace remains `C1/C2/C3` and is not renamed.

## 2. Research axis

The primary axis remains **Process Reality Fidelity** rather than terminal task correctness.

```text
Natural multi-Agent trajectory
  -> natural Jump J0
  -> inherited system inertia
  -> one-shot authority withdrawal at J0 (R5 probe)
  -> experimental signal exits
  -> free continuation
  -> post-consumption inertia response (R6 readout)
  -> target-specificity test (R5-R6 S triad)
  -> structural inertia risk control / localized recovery (R7 C triad)
  -> CPR semantic closure (R8)
  -> external robustness / replication (R9)
```

Terminal outcome remains a separate terminal observation.

## 3. R2-R6 are not five sequential subject experiments

R2, R3, R4 and R6 are process views over realized trajectories. R5 is the bounded intervention operator.

```text
Natural trajectory
  |- R2: Jump emergence
  |- R3: inheritance / propagation
  |- R4: retrospective dynamics
  `- R6: system inertia

R5: apply one bounded perturbation at a natural J0
  -> consume intervention
  -> free continuation
  -> observe R2 / R3 / R4 / R6 again downstream
```

Thus:

> **R5 supplies the perturbation; R6 supplies the principal inertia-level readout.**

The research numbers are analytical roles and views, not a mandatory linear execution order.

## 4. R5 — one-shot authority-withdrawal probe

The forward R5 intervention is not prompt steering, agent modification, topology modification, a persistent state rewrite or an opposite factual claim.

For the established R5-MID family, the intervention withdraws only the selected information field's acquired factual status once, for example:

`fact -> unconfirmed`

The experiment:

- changes only the branch-visible status representation named in the frozen intervention contract;
- supplies no replacement factual conclusion;
- supplies no desired downstream behavior;
- supplies no desired terminal answer;
- does not modify the underlying persistent Arena state;
- is delivered exactly once;
- is consumed after delivery;
- is never re-injected by the experiment;
- releases the system to free downstream evolution.

R5 primary question:

> Does withdrawing only the acquired factual authority of a natural J0 once produce a structurally distinguishable downstream response after the experimental mark is removed?

R5 establishes **perturbability / path reorganization** where supported. It does not by itself establish target specificity or persistent controllability.

## 5. R6 — inertia-level falsification readout

R6 retains the original granular observations but groups them as **post-perturbation system inertia**.

The key methodological distinction is:

`local perturbation response != inertia transition`

Immediate extra reasoning, one extra write, or one local reroute is not sufficient evidence that the perturbation reached system inertia.

R6 examines source-backed downstream structure after the experiment-origin signal has exited active context, including:

- descendant Jump continuation or disappearance;
- same-family versus transformed continuation;
- direct/indirect inheritance;
- transfer across field or Agent lineage;
- delayed re-emergence;
- retrospective return/rebound;
- post-return inheritance;
- branch / merge / re-entry / cross-Agent continuation;
- replacement, reconstruction or hybridization;
- reconvergence;
- censoring.

A Jump phenotype may disappear while structural inertia remains in lineage, routing, state or participation structure.

R6 primary question:

> After the R5 signal is consumed, does the downstream inherited process tendency differ from the natural reference inertia, and through which observable structural dimensions is that difference expressed?

## 6. Falsification-oriented interpretation

The intended logic is a **local counterfactual falsification test**, not a claim of mathematical proof by contradiction.

Candidate null interpretation:

> J0 is only a local information event; its acquired factual authority is not structurally embedded in downstream system inertia.

Test:

1. select a natural J0 using frozen evidence;
2. withdraw only its acquired factual authority once;
3. consume the experiment-origin signal;
4. permit free downstream evolution;
5. measure downstream R6 inertia.

If the post-consumption inertia is structurally reorganized, the null interpretation is challenged under the matched local intervention.

However, a further alternative remains:

> any comparable factual-status downgrade may create similar downstream uncertainty effects.

That alternative is the purpose of the specificity layer below.

## 7. R5-R6 Specificity Triad

Use a separate namespace from R7.

### S0 — Natural Reference

No new experimental status downgrade.

Scientific role: estimate the natural continuation/inertia reference for the selected parent/J0 context.

### S1 — Matched Factual-Field Status Downgrade

Apply the same mechanical status transform used in S2 to a preregistered matched factual field that is not the selected J0 target.

Scientific role: estimate the **generic epistemic uncertainty response** produced by the same kind of one-shot status downgrade.

Do not call S1 a `non-Jump field` unless semantic or structural evidence independently supports that statement. Preferred language is `matched factual field` or `matched non-J0 factual field`.

### S2 — J0-Targeted Authority Withdrawal

Apply the same one-shot factual-status withdrawal to the selected natural J0 field.

Scientific role: estimate the total J0-targeted response.

All S arms must preserve the same one-shot mechanics where applicable: no persistent experiment-origin mutation, no reinjection, no terminal answer target and free downstream continuation after consumption.

## 8. Specificity contrasts

The preregistered interpretations are:

```text
S1 - S0 = generic epistemic uncertainty response
S2 - S0 = total J0-targeted perturbation response
S2 - S1 = J0 target-specificity contrast
```

The primary specificity contrast is **S2 versus S1**.

S1 is not required to show zero response. A normal factual field becoming `unconfirmed` may legitimately cause additional checking, reasoning, messaging or local rerouting.

The target-specificity question is instead:

> Given the same intervention mechanics and materially matched target-field properties, is the downstream inertia-level response to J0 distinguishable from the response to a matched non-J0 factual field?

## 9. Matching and selection guard

The S1 target must be selected before outcome inspection under a versioned field-selection contract.

The contract should freeze:

- eligible field pool;
- source trace / parent identity;
- factual-status eligibility;
- visibility and timing eligibility;
- actor/role availability where relevant;
- state/interface family;
- pre-intervention status;
- matching features and selection rule;
- excluded targets and reasons;
- pool hash and selected-target hash.

The control field must not be chosen because it is expected to produce little or no effect.

## 10. Measurement hierarchy

The forward measurement hierarchy is:

```text
Raw observable facts
  -> structural Jump / lineage
  -> local perturbation response
  -> post-consumption inertia response
  -> target-specificity contrast
  -> R7 risk-control / recovery contrast
  -> R8 semantic adjudication
  -> terminal outcome reported separately
```

Do not collapse immediate local response and downstream inertia into one score.

Prefer a multidimensional inertia profile over a single scalar until a later preregistered mathematical summary is justified.

## 11. R7 namespace and boundary

The existing R7 conditions remain:

- `C1` — One-Shot Free Continuation;
- `C2` — Persistent Field Propagation;
- `C3` — ALR Authority-Localized Recovery.

R5-R6 `S` and R7 `C` are orthogonal experimental dimensions:

- **S asks WHAT target was touched**: natural reference, matched factual field, or J0.
- **C asks HOW a risk-bearing condition is handled**: one-shot, persistent exposure, or localized recovery.

Mechanical intervention primitives may be reused, but scientific condition identities must remain distinct.
