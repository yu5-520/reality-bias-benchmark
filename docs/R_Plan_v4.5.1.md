# Reality Bias Research Plan v4.5.1

Date: 2026-09-17  
Status: FORWARD / METHODOLOGICAL CLOSURE / NO NEW R STAGE / NO SCIENTIFIC RUN AUTHORIZATION  
Predecessor: `docs/R_Plan_v4.5.md`

## 1. Version decision

v4.5.1 does not change the R-stage geometry and does not rewrite the frozen R6-D design. It closes seven interpretation/runtime gaps before scientific S0/S1/S2 execution:

1. distinguish normal information inheritance from system inertia;
2. separate operator equivalence from target comparability in S1/S2;
3. split process distance into structural, inheritance and epistemic-authority domains;
4. state explicitly that the frozen `after_turn:8` design identifies a conditional effect under one historical prefix;
5. split R7 closure into potential, evidence-supported and repair closures;
6. separate content-addressed identity/reachability from semantic adoption evidence;
7. interpret `persistent_state_mutation=false` as experiment-origin non-mutation, not a ban on endogenous Agent state changes.

No new R stage, intervention arm or target is introduced.

## 2. Stable research geometry

`R5 perturb -> R6 identify -> R7 localize/control -> R8 adjudicate semantics`

R6 remains:

- R6-A natural variability baseline;
- R6-B carrier / inheritance identification;
- R6-C intervention-related inertia transition;
- R6-D target specificity / frozen-target contrast.

## 3. Normal inheritance versus system inertia

Shared information being read and reused is normal multi-Agent coordination.

For forward work, a carrier chain becomes an **inertia candidate** only when evidence supports that a prior epistemic status, commitment, constraint or action tendency continues to shape later process options, transition propensity or downstream state after the original event has passed.

A stronger inertia claim requires evidence that the constraint persists after a relevant challenge, downgrade or withdrawal opportunity.

Inertia is not automatically harmful. Risk-bearing status remains a separate semantic/control question.

## 4. S1/S2 interpretation boundary

S1 and S2 remain mechanically equivalent under the frozen one-shot operator, but their targets are not assumed exchangeable.

The two targets may differ in:

- task relevance;
- source provenance;
- structural position;
- downstream exposure opportunity;
- decision weight;
- causal centrality.

Therefore the frozen primary contrast `S2 - S1` is first interpreted as a **FROZEN_TARGET_RESPONSE_CONTRAST**.

A stronger claim that the difference is specifically caused by Escape-derived/J0 identity requires either:

- an explicit matching argument supported by frozen pre-outcome evidence; or
- append-only robustness controls using additional ordinary factual targets.

The original S1 result may never be replaced after outcomes are observed.

## 5. Three-domain R6 distance object

The preregistered representation remains multidimensional and is now grouped into three domains.

### Structural domain

- event-set difference;
- edge-set difference;
- actor set/sequence;
- reach/depth;
- branch/merge/re-entry;
- path family;
- reconvergence.

### Information-inheritance domain

- source read/delivery;
- explicit reference/use;
- adoption/abandonment;
- inherited state/action;
- propagation/re-entry;
- source replacement.

### Epistemic-authority domain

- authority/status used by downstream decision;
- downgrade acceptance or rejection;
- unsupported re-confirmation;
- authority transfer/reconstruction;
- later evidence that independently justifies confirmation.

No post-outcome scalar may collapse these domains unless separately preregistered and validated.

## 6. Conditional identification boundary

Repeated continuations from the exact frozen `after_turn:8` parent estimate:

> the conditional process response given this historical prefix, task, Agent registry, model binding and environment.

They are repeated realizations, not independent population samples. Claims must not be generalized to all tasks, all Jumps, all topologies or all models without later replication.

## 7. R7 closure model

R7 now distinguishes:

- `PotentiallyAffectedClosure`: descendants structurally reachable under preregistered lineage/dependency edges;
- `EvidenceSupportedAffectedClosure`: descendants with source-backed read/adopt/inherit/propagate evidence;
- `RepairClosure`: evidence-supported affected nodes plus only those mechanically required dependencies needed for the frozen local replay/reopen semantics.

Potential closure is primarily a scouting boundary. It is not automatically the repair set.

## 8. Hash versus semantic adoption

Content/revision hashes establish identity, version and traceable lineage.

They do not by themselves prove that a downstream Agent used the information as a decision premise.

Forward runtime records therefore separate:

- identity/reachability evidence;
- delivery/read evidence;
- semantic-use/adoption evidence;
- inherited state/action evidence;
- propagated descendant evidence.

Relation-level evidence should be preserved where available.

## 9. Experiment-origin versus endogenous persistence

The frozen atomic operator field `persistent_state_mutation=false` means:

> the experiment itself does not directly write a persistent state mutation.

It does **not** mean the subject system is forbidden from endogenously writing new state after observing the one-shot annotation.

Such endogenous state/message/invocation changes are scientific observations and possible carrier candidates. They must be preserved and traced.

## 10. Frozen design compatibility

The existing R6-D exact design hash, target selection, envelope hashes, branch order, horizon, budget and authorization gate remain unchanged.

v4.5.1 is an interpretation/measurement/runtime-semantic closure over the frozen design, not a post-outcome redesign.

This plan authorizes no provider call, paid evaluator call, CPR adjudication or active recovery on a scientific subject.
