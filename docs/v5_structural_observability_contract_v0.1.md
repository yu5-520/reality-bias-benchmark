# Process Reality v5 Structural Observability Contract v0.1

Date: 2026-09-18  
Status: FORWARD OFFLINE / STRUCTURAL CANDIDATE LAYER / NO SCIENTIFIC RUN AUTHORIZATION

## 1. Purpose

This contract defines machine-observable objects for Process Reality v5 without promoting structure into semantic truth.

The structural layer may nominate:

- unstable-information candidate windows;
- semantic-transformation candidate chains;
- Structural Support candidates;
- Stable Shared Information Pool entry candidates;
- direct-consumption candidates;
- Structural Exposure candidates;
- upstream trace sets;
- downstream potential/evidence-supported closures;
- candidate intervention surfaces.

These are structural/scouting objects until semantic audit binds meaning to raw evidence.

## 2. Structural Support candidate

A Structural Support candidate is emitted when recorded process structure shows that an information object or descendant has obtained reusable system-level support.

Examples of structural evidence include:

- a state write producing a new addressable state identity;
- cross-actor reads or direct references to the state;
- source-backed descendant state/action creation;
- repeated direct consumption without renewed relay from the original Agent;
- relation evidence that survives beyond the source turn.

Support does not imply correctness, legitimacy or CPR.

## 3. Stable Shared Information Pool candidate

A Stable Shared Information Pool entry candidate requires evidence of system addressability plus at least one eligible direct-consumption opportunity.

Forward structural representation separates:

- pool addressability;
- actual direct consumption;
- source epistemic status;
- pool/container epistemic status;
- transformation lineage.

A shared-state write alone may be a pool-entry candidate but is not enough to establish semantic adoption.

## 4. Structural Exposure candidate

Structural Exposure is the scouting layer.

An Exposure candidate is a bounded region in which support/reuse/dependency/constraint evidence becomes sufficiently observable for deterministic or low-cost rules to nominate a semantic-audit window.

Exposure is not semantic genesis and need not equal first support.

## 5. Scout objective

The scout should minimize semantic-audit surface:

\`All Events -> Structural Scout -> Candidate Regions -> Localized Semantic Audit\`

The scout is successful as an engineering method when it materially narrows the region requiring semantic review while preserving evidence needed for later tracing.

## 6. Upstream and downstream traces

Forward lineage must distinguish:

- \`trace_root_ref\`;
- \`structural_support_refs\`;
- \`pool_entry_refs\`;
- \`exposure_anchor_refs\`;
- \`potentially_affected_closure\`;
- \`evidence_supported_affected_closure\`;
- \`candidate_intervention_surface_refs\`;
- \`selected_intervention_surface_ref\`.

No equality between these refs is assumed.

## 7. Historical compatibility

Legacy \`structural_jump_detector_v0.1\` remains frozen for historical evidence.

Forward v5 uses a new scout interface instead of renaming or mutating old Jump records.

## 8. Semantic boundary

The structural layer must not infer:

- semantic origin;
- semantic adoption from reachability/hash alone;
- epistemic correctness;
- CPR;
- causal effect;
- repair efficacy.

## 9. Authorization

This contract authorizes no provider call, evaluator call, semantic adjudication or active repair.
