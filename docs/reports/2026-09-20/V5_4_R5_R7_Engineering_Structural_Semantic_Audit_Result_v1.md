## 3. Control-surface semantics

The audit supports a strict engineering distinction:

`R5 probe != R6 repair readiness != R7-P persistent correction != R7-S structural repair`.

### R5-I — probe, not repair

R5-I changes the **next reader's runtime view** once from `fact` to `unconfirmed`.

The stored shared state is not persistently rewritten.

Its engineering meaning is diagnostic: expose local authority dependence and observe the continuation.

### R6 — repair readiness, not repair

R6 binds the target into a machine-addressable repair object:

- Repair Anchor;
- content address;
- source / transformation / authority / pool history;
- affected descendants;
- Semantic Lineage Closure;
- Lineage Completeness Gate;
- repair packet;
- allowed repair operations.

All four stronger candidates are `COMPLETE_FOR_AUTHORIZED_REPAIR` in the bounded frozen horizon.

### R7-P — persistent correction, not internal repair

R7-P keeps corrected uncertainty visible to every downstream reader in the common horizon.

Across four cases this creates 32 direct correction exposures and 28 experiment-origin reinjections.

The underlying inherited shared-state lineage is deliberately left intact.

### R7-S — internal structural repair

R7-S operates at the exact Repair Anchor and affected closure.

It can:

`authority downgrade -> affected invalidation -> dependent reopen -> selective recompute -> preservation -> re-entry watch`.

The repaired branch is therefore not just “R7-P for longer.” It changes the internal structural continuation.
