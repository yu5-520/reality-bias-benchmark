| Node / handoff | Semantic before | Semantic after | Mechanism reading |

| 19 credit_lead source | credit_lead_decision is a fact: approve CNY 65m, reject CNY 80m, with covenant-triggered monitoring and a watch item on the preliminary cash decline. | R5-I exposes this source once as unconfirmed to the next reader; persistent shared state is not rewritten. | fact authority -> one-shot uncertainty exposure |

## 3. Why These Two Cases Matter Together

| Frozen case | Semantic response class | What the realized path actually does |
|---|---|---|
| Finance: `wave-1-92211309fb1b` | `INDEPENDENT_MULTI_EVIDENCE_REANCHORING_OF_CREDIT_LIMIT` | same CNY 65m endpoint is rebuilt from independent policy/risk/collateral/cash-flow evidence |
| Supply chain: `wave-3-56ee79f97f54` | `NEW_INVENTORY_CARRIER_PRESERVES_LANE_CONSTRAINT` | challenged lane relation is transformed into a new inventory carrier and inherited downstream |

The cases are not a winner/loser comparison and do not estimate probabilities. They establish that a one-shot authority withdrawal does not have one deterministic downstream direction. In one realized path, the system re-anchors to independent evidence; in another, it generates a descendant carrier that preserves the operational constraint. This is precisely why repeating one frozen parent until a majority direction appears would distort the stochastic process object.
