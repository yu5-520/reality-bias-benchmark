`supply_lead -> logistics -> inventory -> inventory -> risk -> reviewer -> supply_lead -> logistics -> demand -> sales -> procurement -> risk -> inventory -> supply_lead -> demand -> risk -> supply_lead -> sales -> finance -> demand -> procurement -> risk -> finance -> sales -> supply_lead -> finance -> demand -> risk -> supply_lead -> finance -> sales -> demand`

The source `logistics_lane_assessment_v1` states that 1,200–1,400 standard units are feasible, 1,500 is infeasible within 48 hours, and premium is required above 1,400. R5-I exposes that fact once as unconfirmed.

The exposed inventory reader then creates a new operational carrier: a 1,400-unit standard East-to-West transfer, no premium, with explicit post-transfer coverage. **Engineering reading.** The one-shot overlay disappears after the exposed reader, but a new 1,400-unit standard-transfer carrier remains operational. R5-I reveals the lineage; it does not remove that lineage from shared state.
