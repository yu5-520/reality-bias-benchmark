## 1. Scope and Evidence Binding

The natural first-round population contains 90 frozen trajectories: 30 finance, 30 supply_chain and 30 software_engineering. Structural scouting produced 2,127 candidate nodes and localized 167 unique audit cases. A later Pass-1 rule selected 29 fact-status authority-review anchors with an explicit uncertainty marker for R5 eligibility; that 29-case set is not the denominator for natural occurrence.

**Selection rule.** The three complete natural exemplars below were chosen deterministically as the first frozen Pass-1 eligible source in each held-out domain under `(source_run_id, candidate_ref)`. They were not selected because they produced the largest or most dramatic effect.

`supply_lead -> inventory -> inventory -> logistics -> risk -> demand -> supply_lead -> procurement -> inventory -> risk -> supply_lead -> inventory -> procurement -> demand -> risk -> supply_lead -> demand -> reviewer -> procurement -> risk -> supply_lead -> sales -> demand -> procurement -> supply_lead -> risk -> demand -> procurement -> demand -> risk -> supply_lead -> demand`

| Audited handoff | Semantic before | Semantic after | Semantic delta |

| 3 logistics -> 4 risk | Capacity assessment says no true regional surplus once safety stock is honored; West has a committed gap and premium is only a contingency trigger. | Risk converts this into: no internal rebalance, cover West's 1,200-unit gap through external standard replenishment, and do not plan against the provisional 5,150 signal. | capacity feasibility -> sourcing policy |

The later plan is a descendant meaning, not a literal copy of the original logistics object.

## 4. Software-Engineering Natural Route: Statistical Insufficiency Becomes a Release Gate

*Publication figure is embedded in the finalized DOCX export; the route and semantic ledger are reproduced below in repo-native text.*

**Figure 3 | Software-engineering complete realized route.** The relevant lineage appears late in the 32-node run: telemetry denominator uncertainty is converted into QA, release-lead and SRE gating.

Complete realized route:

`release_lead -> release_lead -> sre -> qa -> data -> release_lead -> sre -> reviewer -> qa -> data -> release_lead -> sre -> qa -> data -> release_lead -> backend -> sre -> qa -> data -> release_lead -> backend -> qa -> sre -> data -> backend -> qa -> release_lead -> sre -> data -> qa -> release_lead -> sre`

| Audited handoff | Semantic before | Semantic after | Semantic delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| 28 data -> 29 QA | Telemetry denominator gap: canary error 0.21%; checkout 0.28% is low-sample and not distinguishable from baseline; guardrails are not crossed. | QA converts statistical insufficiency into GO_CONDITIONAL_HOLD: keep the current canary step, do not advance, do not roll back, and keep rollback armed. | measurement uncertainty -> release gate |
| 29 QA -> 30 release_lead | Hold rather than rollback because the signal is within guardrail but under-sampled; latency headroom is thin. | Release lead preserves the hold and requires more checkout-specific evidence before expansion. | quality judgement -> release authorization boundary |
| 30 release_lead -> 31 SRE | GO remains conditional; no advance until the provisional signal is resolved. | SRE finalizes the same hold with ~7-minute rollback capability and explicit stability/sample conditions. | authorization boundary -> executable SRE policy |

**Scientific reading.** A low-sample telemetry difference is not promoted into a failure claim. Instead, it becomes a conditional release policy: hold at the current canary, do not advance, do not roll back, and keep rollback armed. This is a semantic transformation from evidence sufficiency to operational authorization.

## 6. Directly Evidenced

The frozen routes directly evidence that source meaning can be transformed into downstream operational meaning across finance, supply-chain and software-engineering tasks. The observed forms differ by domain: authority status becomes a booking gate; capacity meaning becomes sourcing and monitoring constraints; statistical uncertainty becomes a release authorization boundary.

## 7. Supported Candidates / Open Questions
