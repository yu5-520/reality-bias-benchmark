## What changed

`RepairReviewContext` revalidates before/after graphs and the source-bound continuation receipt, then exposes all observed records and native object/event sources. Host-owned witnesses bind exact read spans. The review catalog retains the original task and existing dynamic C/P/R/coupling/censoring rules with their hashes. It excludes planner diagnoses/meanings, field grants, completion verdicts, candidate labels and prior audit labels. The unchanged natural-audit references retain their original scope; this new interface separately permits repair-branch evidence.

Each proposed target requires assessments of the target transition, old information authority, old task permission, historical re-entry, transformed descendants and unrelated semantic progress. Before/after quotes, a reconstructed semantic path, independent versus inherited support, limitations and unresolved relations are explicit. A read/visibility association is not sufficient to support repair. Old C/P must be examined after R, and semantic descendants need not preserve wording.

The host binds the reviewer separately from its report. A repair actor cannot self-review. Mechanical intake validation checks evidence identity, coverage and window requirements; it does not certify that a semantic interpretation is true. Actual reviewer independence and model/tool provenance require a separately configured reviewer adapter, which is not supplied by this extension.

Native exit and continuation records are required before reviewer invocation. A closing, censor or failure record must have an inspected source witness. Full relevant semantic visibility and natural closure are necessary for overall support. Partial capture, unresolved relations, active censoring, failure, missing checks or a negated assessment prevent overall support. A accepted complete report is support from the bound reviewer within the observed horizon, not universal efficacy or a permission to promote the branch.
