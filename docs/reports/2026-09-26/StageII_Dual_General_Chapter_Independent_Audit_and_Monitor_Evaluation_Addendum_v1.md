# Stage-II Dual General Chapter Addendum: Independent Semantic Audit and Prospective Monitor Evaluation v1.0

Date: 2026-09-26  
Status: **FORWARD NORMATIVE ADDENDUM**  
Applies with:
- Experimental General Chapter v2
- Engineering General Chapter v1
- R_Plan v7.45

## Purpose

This addendum freezes the independence boundary between the natural experiment, online monitor, full semantic audit, monitor-performance evaluation, and engineering repair.

It supersedes any earlier sequencing language that would open active repair B after each individual G2-G5 group.

The canonical sequence is:

`84 prospective natural A`
-> `independent full semantic audit`
-> `monitor evaluation`
-> `engineering eligibility`
-> `G2/G3/G4/G5 engineering batches`.

---

# Experimental audit principles

## EXP-AU-01 — Monitor-blind semantic audit

The full semantic audit must not read monitor warnings, monitor candidate labels, monitor confidence, monitor CPR predictions, or repair-package status before the semantic reference set is sealed.

Shared raw execution evidence remains admissible only through the audit raw bundle.

## EXP-AU-02 — Audit reference seal before evaluation

Monitor performance may not be calculated until the complete semantic reference set is frozen and hashed.

## EXP-AU-03 — G1 is not the primary monitor benchmark

G1 contributes to scientific mechanism synthesis and protocol calibration.

Because the monitor design was informed by earlier evidence, primary prospective monitor-performance metrics use G2-G5 only.

## EXP-AU-04 — Semantic audit cannot repair monitor misses

If the monitor fails to identify a primary repair candidate prospectively, the full semantic audit may record that miss but may not inject a new candidate into the primary end-to-end repair pipeline.

Any audit-assisted intervention must be a separately labeled secondary experiment.

## EXP-AU-05 — Reference adjudication is within-study, not universal ground truth

The semantic audit reference set is the frozen within-study adjudication layer for the recorded evidence.

It does not claim omniscient access to hidden model reasoning or universal semantic truth.

## EXP-AU-06 — Negative and not-established cases remain in the reference set

Normal inheritance, benign review, inert residue, unavailable evidence, and unsupported causal links must remain explicit so that monitor precision is not inflated by a positive-only reference set.

# Engineering monitor-evaluation principles

## ENG-ME-01 — Monitor output is prediction evidence, not audit evidence

Monitor warnings/candidates are prospective engineering outputs.

They cannot serve as evidence that the independent semantic audit should confirm the same structure.

## ENG-ME-02 — Primary monitor version is frozen across G2-G5

The primary monitor implementation, thresholds, structural rules, and signature mappings are frozen before the first prospective benchmark group.

If the monitor changes, later evidence belongs to a separately versioned benchmark stratum.

## ENG-ME-03 — Structural signatures and CPR verdicts are distinct

The monitor should detect observable structural signatures such as scope expansion, state mismatch, historical re-entry, recursive feedback, carrier transformation, or closure divergence.

The post-hoc semantic audit determines whether and how those structures instantiate C/P/R or cross-penetration.

## ENG-ME-04 — Detector miss and evidence-surface unavailability are distinct

A monitor failure to detect an observable structure is a detector miss.

A structure whose required evidence surface was not legally exposed is an observability limitation.

These categories must not be merged.

## ENG-ME-05 — Unsupported warning and rejected warning are distinct

A warning lacking sufficient audit evidence to confirm or deny is UNSUPPORTED.

A warning contradicted by independent audit evidence is AUDIT_REJECTED.

Only the latter is a direct false-positive analogue.

## ENG-ME-06 — Hit and localization depth are distinct

Trajectory-level detection does not imply correct source, carrier, consumer, decision, or lineage localization.

These are separate metrics.

## ENG-ME-07 — Every rate exposes numerator and denominator

All monitoring rates must report their numerator and denominator.

Zero-denominator metrics are NA, never 0%.

## ENG-ME-08 — Primary claim language is within-study

Formal performance language is:

> within-study prospective monitoring performance

No monitor metric is a production SLA or universal AI-system accuracy estimate.

# Sequencing principles

## ENG-SQ-01 — All 84 natural A precede active B

No active repair B is allowed until G2-G5 all complete their natural first-attempt blocks and the 84 trajectories are sealed.

## ENG-SQ-02 — Semantic reference and monitor benchmark precede engineering eligibility freeze

The primary semantic reference set and primary monitor evaluation are frozen before the engineering eligibility population is finalized.

## ENG-SQ-03 — Engineering B proceeds by group batches

After eligibility freeze, engineering intervention proceeds in G2, G3, G4, G5 batches.

Each batch freezes its B and post-repair evidence before the next engineering batch.

## ENG-SQ-04 — Engineering fixes cannot contaminate natural A

A repair-layer bug discovered during G2 engineering may be repaired prospectively before G3 engineering only under a new engineering version boundary.

All natural A evidence remains immutable and is never regenerated.

---

# Evidence-channel invariant

The repository should preserve separate logical roots or bundles for:

1. raw execution evidence used by independent audit;
2. monitor runtime outputs;
3. semantic audit reference records;
4. monitor evaluation records;
5. engineering eligibility records;
6. repair B/post-repair evidence.

The monitor/evaluation join occurs only after both monitor outputs and semantic reference records are independently sealed.

---

# Claim boundary

This addendum does not authorize subject/provider calls, paid evaluators, or active repair.

It changes the sequencing and evaluation governance only.
