## Opening position

This report is not another experiment result report. It freezes the parent methodological interpretation contract for the Stage-II evidence programme.

Its purpose is to answer five questions before any child experiment report is read:

1. Were the studied systems and protocols genuinely present in execution?
2. Were the task trajectories naturally generated rather than manually scripted toward a desired process?
3. Did the AI coding task alter a real executable repository state rather than remain a text simulation?
4. Did the external monitor observe without entering the execution control loop?
5. Is the scientific target framework ranking or process structure?

It then records three groups of research hypotheses motivated by Stage-I and Stage-II evidence. Hypotheses are not upgraded into established causal laws merely because they are useful interpretations.

### Evidence boundary

The Stage-II natural matrix is closed at 21/21 first attempts. No natural rerun, subject call, evaluator call, or new C3/C4 contrast is authorized by this chapter.

The current R6-grade Stage-II evidence substrate contains:

- 21 natural cells;
- 482 runner-exposed route nodes;
- 1,202 passive native events;
- 840 textual protocol/observer artifacts;
- 299 frozen checkout-file entries;
- 70 source-bound target semantic nodes;
- 46 audited semantic edges.

These evidence surfaces overlap and are not one combined denominator.

The strongest Stage-II interpretation remains descriptive/mechanistic observation. Stage-I R5 supplies the separate local perturbation layer; Stage-I R6/R7 supply lineage identification and structured repair evidence. This chapter does not pool those designs into a single causal effect estimate.

# 1. Evidence hierarchy and anti-circularity rule

The reporting hierarchy is:

`General Chapter v1.0 -> child experiment reports -> source-bound route/semantic evidence -> frozen raw evidence`

The General Chapter defines the research object, terminology, methodological invariants, claim boundaries, and hypotheses. It is **not** a substitute for empirical evidence.

A child report may cite a General Chapter claim ID such as `GC-M3` or `GC-H3a`, but it must independently bind the concrete trajectory, semantic ledger, protocol artifact, repository state, or other evidence that motivates its use.

The General Chapter may later be revised into an evidence-linked synthesis after child reports are frozen. Such a revision may cite those child reports, but it may not claim that the child report proves a proposition merely because the child report previously cited the General Chapter.

Therefore:

`parent definition != child evidence`

and

`child citation != circular proof`.

# 2. Methodological foundation

## GC-M1 — Framework fidelity

**Position: DIRECTLY_EVIDENCED.**

The Stage-II study did not replace heterogeneous frameworks with one synthetic universal execution layer.

The frozen native registry binds each studied system to its own execution role:

- AutoGen retains native AgentChat scheduling/handoff and tool interaction.
- MetaGPT retains its Environment/Role scheduling and message-buffer semantics.
- A2A uses official A2A protocol/SDK boundaries for role-service communication.
- MCP uses the official tool/resource protocol boundary inside the software-engineering host rather than being misrepresented as a multi-Agent scheduler.
- RAG attaches at retrieval/context.
- MemoryBank attaches at persistent memory write/retrieve/recall.
- LongLLMLingua attaches at context compression/transformation.

Framework-specific glue supplies frozen tasks, roles, subject configuration, checkout state, and evidence attachment. It does not rewrite each system into one common internal process.

The scientific invariant is:

> **freeze scientific inputs and upstream versions; preserve heterogeneous native execution; observe externally; normalize evidence only after observation.**

This chapter therefore treats X1-X7 as heterogeneous process structures, not seven implementations of one hidden benchmark runtime.

### Boundary

“Framework fidelity” does not mean there is zero integration code. Task attachment, environment binding, protocol hosting, compatible model-provider attachment, and passive observation are necessary experimental infrastructure. The claim is narrower: the studied upstream framework/protocol semantics were not modified merely to make every X execute through one common architecture.

## GC-M2 — Process naturalness

**Position: DIRECTLY_EVIDENCED WITH INTERFACE BOUNDARY.**

T1-T3 freeze the user objective and initial project state. The experiment does not prescribe:

- which file must be read first;
- which specialist must be called;
- the role order;
- the number of reviews;
- the semantic conclusions that must be reached;
- whether scope should expand;
- the target CPR structure;
- the sequence of intermediate states;
- the required process route;
- the desired terminal explanation.

Once a natural cell begins, the experimenter does not intervene in response to the emerging trajectory.

For custom JSON-action surfaces, T2/T3 prospectively expose the minimum machine-readable action contract needed by the already-existing parser. This specifies serialization and action admission, not semantic content or the route the model should take. X1 retains its native AutoGen tool interface and does not use this helper.

The correct statement is therefore:

> **The task and executable boundary are frozen; the process trajectory is not.**

### Boundary

It is inaccurate to say that the experiment imposes no input/output contract at all. Some execution surfaces require a parsable action envelope. The methodological claim concerns the absence of experimenter-prescribed semantic outputs and process routes.

---

## GC-M3 — Repository-grounded execution

**Position: DIRECTLY_EVIDENCED.**

Stage II is repository-grounded AI coding, not a text-only role-play of software engineering.

Each cell receives an isolated copy of the frozen checkout fixture. The execution surface can perform real repository operations including:

- file enumeration;
- UTF-8 file reads;
- persistent file writes;
- unit-test execution;
- role communication/delegation;
- terminal finalization.

Writes alter the cell checkout, and the post-run repository state is frozen for audit.

Consequently, the study can distinguish:

`model-reported completion`

from

`persisted repository state`.

X4-T3 is a clear example: the terminal account semantically closes more of the legacy cleanup than the frozen checkout tree supports. The launcher repair is real; the broader claimed cleanup is not fully present in the persisted tree.

X3-T3 provides the complementary pattern: a historical file remains physically present after its functional runtime role is cut, and later review repeatedly re-reads that persisted residue.

The repository is intentionally small and controlled for experimental comparability, but the code execution semantics are real.

---

## GC-M4 — Non-interfering external observability

**Position: DIRECTLY_EVIDENCED WITH OBSERVABILITY BOUNDARY.**

The Stage-II monitor is an external/passive evidence system, not the controller of the studied process.

The monitoring architecture does not:

- choose the next Agent;
- rewrite model messages;
- alter protocol payload meaning;
- decide tool calls;
- prescribe handoff order;
- modify checkout state;
- decide task completion;
- inject a desired CPR path.

Instead it captures already-emitted execution surfaces such as framework-native events, protocol bytes, tool/resource calls, process stdout/stderr/return codes, retrieval/memory/compression carriers, and persisted checkout effects.

The evidence order is:

`system execution -> passive capture -> immutable evidence -> post-hoc semantic audit`.

This separation is strong enough to reconstruct route-level process evidence while leaving the subject execution path independent of the monitor.

### Observability boundary

The observer does not expose identical semantic surfaces for every framework.

- AutoGen, MetaGPT, A2A, and MemoryBank preserve rich target semantics in their strongest cells.
- MCP preserves exact tool/resource state but only partial internal role semantics.
- RAG preserves retrieval carriers but not every downstream host semantic.
- LongLLMLingua preserves compression before/after state but not every downstream raw response.

Missing internal semantics remain missing. They are not reconstructed into fictional hidden utterances.

The monitoring responsibility is therefore:

> **faithfully preserve observable process reality and explicitly preserve the boundary of what was not observed.**

---

## GC-M5 — Outcome-independent process analysis

**Position: METHODOLOGICAL POSITION; SUPPORTED BY FROZEN PROCESS EVIDENCE.**

Stage II is not a seven-framework performance ranking.

The systems occupy different architectural layers and solve different engineering problems. Terminal success, call count, turn count, or token count cannot be converted into a valid overall “best framework” ordering.

The primary research proposition is:

> **Outcome convergence does not imply process equivalence.**

A functional endpoint describes where a trajectory ends. It does not reconstruct:

- what information became authoritative;
- how semantic descendants formed;
- how collaboration scope expanded;
- what historical state remained active;
- which persisted artifacts disagreed with terminal language;
- why the process closed or reopened.

T2 and T3 show multiple cases in which useful or comparable functional work is achieved while the collaboration, semantic, and temporal routes differ substantially.

The inverse boundary is equally important:

> **Process difference does not automatically imply process inferiority.**

Additional review, memory, retrieval, or verification may be useful. The first scientific task is to identify and trace process structure before assigning benefit or harm.

# 3. Research hypothesis group H1 — Open-process reliability

## GC-H1a — Protocol compliance is not process reliability

**Status: SUPPORTED_CANDIDATE.**

Real communication, orchestration, tool/resource, retrieval, memory, and compression structures participate in Stage II, yet process-state divergence, scope expansion, historical residue, and information transformation remain observable.

This motivates the bounded proposition:

> **Protocol compliance can constrain valid interaction without guaranteeing process-reality equivalence.**

This is not a claim that the named protocols cause unreliability.

## GC-H1b — Reliability control and open execution may trade off

**Status: NOT_ESTABLISHED / RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS.**

A system can increase predictability by predefining routes, permitted files, agent order, and stopping rules. At the limit, however, such control approaches a conventional workflow and may reduce the adaptive value for which open Agent execution is used.

The research hypothesis is that future reliable Agent systems will need to preserve a meaningful open decision space rather than obtain reliability only by eliminating that space.

No optimal point on this trade-off is established here.

## GC-H1c — Process reliability may be better externalized as observability, QA, and repair

**Status: SUPPORTED_CANDIDATE AS AN ENGINEERING DIRECTION; SUPERIORITY NOT ESTABLISHED.**

Stage-I R6/R7 and Stage-II non-interfering observation motivate an architecture in which:

`open probabilistic execution`
-> `external monitoring`
-> `semantic / authority / lineage checks`
-> `content-addressed localization`
-> `structured local repair`
-> `continued native execution`.

The hypothesis is not that an external monitor is universally superior to protocol design. It is that process integrity can be treated as an independent observability-and-repair responsibility rather than forcing all reliability semantics into the internal execution protocol.

# 4. Research hypothesis group H2 — Communication friction and propagation

## GC-H2a — Communication efficiency and process fidelity are orthogonal

**Status: NOT_ESTABLISHED / RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS.**

Structured inter-Agent and inter-system mechanisms can improve interoperability, routing, handoff, and state exchange. The existence of those engineering benefits does not logically guarantee preservation of source authority, semantic status, or process lineage.

The hypothesis is:

> **Higher communication efficiency can coexist with process-reality divergence.**

Stage II motivates this question but does not provide a controlled efficiency treatment.

## GC-H2b — Friction may create re-examination opportunities

**Status: NOT_ESTABLISHED / RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS.**

Less structured free collaboration may require repeated explanation, recalculation, negotiation, or restatement. Those operations are costly, but may also create more opportunities for a premise to be revisited or contradicted.

The hypothesis is not that friction is beneficial. It is that communication cost and epistemic re-examination opportunity may be coupled in some systems.

## GC-H2c — Lower friction may shorten source visibility while accelerating descendant propagation

**Status: NOT_ESTABLISHED / RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS.**

The proposed mechanism separates two variables:

- **source visibility lifetime** — how long the original uncertainty/error remains directly visible in its source form;
- **descendant propagation velocity** — how quickly transformed descendants become usable downstream premises, actions, or constraints.

A high-throughput structured system may make an original issue disappear from the visible process quickly because it has already been transformed into a new carrier. This would mean that apparent disappearance of a source problem is not equivalent to disappearance of its process consequence.

This hypothesis is closely related to Stage-I semantic-descendant evidence and Stage-II state-reconciliation / protocol-carrier observations.

### Resource-accounting boundary

The current repository does not freeze a normalized R-versus-Stage-II efficiency comparison. Total token use across experiments with different trajectory populations and task structures is not a valid comparison.

Any future resource claim must normalize at an appropriate unit such as:

- token per trajectory;
- token per executed turn;
- token per handoff;
- token per semantic transition;
- or another prospectively frozen denominator.

Until then, “R uses more tokens than T” remains an observation requiring normalized accounting, not a paper-level result.

# 5. Research hypothesis group H3 — Memory, retrieval, compression, and shared operational reality

## GC-H3a — External memory is not equivalent to a Common Pool

**Status: SUPPORTED_CANDIDATE.**

Stage II separates at least three information mechanisms:

- RAG: retrieval and context exposure;
- MemoryBank: persistence, recall, and reinforcement;
- LongLLMLingua: context transformation/compression.

These mechanisms answer variants of:

> **What information can be made available again?**

A semantic Common Pool answers a different question:

> **What information currently constitutes the system's shared operational reality, with what source, status, authority, descendants, and downstream use?**

Therefore retrieval, persistence, or compression alone do not establish a shared operational Common Pool.

## GC-H3b — Memory utility can coexist with process-reality divergence

**Status: SUPPORTED_CANDIDATE.**

An information layer can support task continuity while its process carrier differs from the underlying source state.

Stage-II examples sharpen the distinction:

- X5 repeatedly retrieves a stable hit set even while the active collaboration process changes; retrieval presence is not semantic evolution.
- X6 demonstrates memory write/recall/strengthening, including a T1 path in which early malformed exchanges become dominant recalled items; memory presence is not equivalent to current authority.
- X7 demonstrates substantial lossy context transformation in T2/T3; a useful compressed carrier is not necessarily semantically identical to its source.

This does not establish that RAG, persistent memory, or compression generally harms task performance.

## GC-H3c — A Common Pool is an authority-and-lineage structure, not merely storage

**Status: SUPPORTED_CANDIDATE / THEORY BOUNDARY.**

The Stage-I Common Pool mechanism and Stage-II external information layers motivate a stronger operational definition.

A Common Pool should be studied through:

`source`
-> `entry / shared visibility`
-> `epistemic or authority status`
-> `reader/adopter`
-> `semantic transformation`
-> `descendant`
-> `shared-state writeback`
-> `downstream adoption`
-> `revision / invalidation / reactivation`.

Under this definition, a storage or retrieval system can participate in a Common Pool but is not automatically one.

A compact distinction is:

> **Memory manages information availability; a Common Pool manages shared operational reality.**

# 6. Unified research direction

The three hypothesis groups converge on one research programme.

Protocol design improves valid interaction. Memory systems improve information availability. Compression improves context tractability. None of those functions should be treated as automatically equivalent to process integrity.

The emerging architectural question is:

> **Can an AI system preserve open probabilistic execution while making shared operational reality continuously observable, lineage-addressable, auditable, and locally repairable?**

This motivates a future process-integrity stack:

`source/provenance`
-> `shared authority state`
-> `semantic lineage`
-> `collaboration/permission scope`
-> `temporal carry-forward`
-> `external quality checks`
-> `content-addressed repair`
-> `selective recomputation`
-> `re-entry watch`.

The current evidence supports this as a research direction. It does not establish universal superiority over alternative reliability architectures.

# 8. Claim boundary

## Directly evidenced / methodological foundation

- `GC-M1` framework fidelity.
- `GC-M2` process naturalness within the explicit interface boundary.
- `GC-M3` repository-grounded execution.
- `GC-M4` non-interfering external observability within the exposed surfaces.
- `GC-M5` outcome-independent process-analysis position is consistent with frozen Stage-II route evidence.

## Supported candidates

- `GC-H1a` protocol compliance is not sufficient to infer process-reality equivalence.
- `GC-H1c` external observability / QA / lineage-scoped repair is a viable engineering direction; superiority is not established.
- `GC-H3a` external memory/retrieval/compression is not automatically a Common Pool.
- `GC-H3b` information-layer utility can coexist with process-state divergence.
- `GC-H3c` Common Pool as an authority-and-lineage structure.

## Research hypotheses / not established

- `GC-H1b` an optimal openness/reliability trade-off.
- `GC-H2a` communication efficiency and fidelity as orthogonal measured variables.
- `GC-H2b` communication friction as a source of additional re-examination opportunity.
- `GC-H2c` source-visibility lifetime versus descendant-propagation velocity under lower friction.
- any normalized claim that R-series free collaboration consumes more communication resources than Stage-II structured systems.
- universal superiority of an external monitoring/repair architecture.
- universal cross-framework, cross-model, or cross-domain prevalence.
