## 7. Engineering interpretation

The strongest engineering result is not a universal repair-success statement.

The stronger supported statement is:

> A one-shot content-addressed repair can remain local and preserve unrelated structure while downstream process dynamics remain heterogeneous.

The 12/12 preserve-set result and the heterogeneous process-effect distribution establish that **locality and efficacy are different variables**.

A repair can be structurally precise yet leave temporal inertia, route persistence, or broader collaboration dynamics intact.

## 8. Why persistence matters

Persistence after a legal repair is not automatically repair failure.

It may indicate that:

- the repaired node was only one support in a larger structure;
- descendants already carried the relevant process state;
- collaboration or temporal inertia survived local target change;
- the system regenerated support from another route;
- the target changed while the full-horizon process remained open.

These alternatives require full-path semantic evidence and must not be reduced to endpoint equality.
