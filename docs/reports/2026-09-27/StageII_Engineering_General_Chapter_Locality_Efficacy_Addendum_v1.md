# Stage-II Engineering General Chapter Addendum: Locality, Efficacy, and Post-Repair Dynamics v1

Date: 2026-09-27  
Status: **FORWARD NORMATIVE ADDENDUM / EMPIRICALLY GROUNDED BY R7-G1**  
Parent: `StageII_Engineering_General_Chapter_Process_Integrity_Monitoring_and_Repair_v1.md`

## Purpose

The first completed prospective R7 paired-repair group establishes an engineering distinction that must now be explicit in every forward repair report:

> **repair locality and downstream repair efficacy are separate properties.**

R7-G1 preserved unrelated structure in 12/12 legal paired repairs while downstream process effects remained heterogeneous.

Therefore a single binary "repair success" field is no longer sufficient.

## ENG-LE-01 — Locality is measured independently

A repair is local only when the evidence shows that the authorized target/preserve boundary was respected.

Locality is measured from:

- target identity;
- actual mutation surface;
- preserve-set hashes/state;
- native-interface compliance;
- permission/scope compliance;
- repair-executor exit.

Locality does not require downstream closure.

## ENG-LE-02 — Efficacy is measured downstream

Repair efficacy is evaluated from the native continuation after the repair executor exits.

At minimum classify:

- targeted redirection;
- targeted suppression;
- persistence;
- mixed effect;
- inconclusive/not established.

A locally precise repair may have incomplete downstream efficacy.

## ENG-LE-03 — Direct target change is not global closure

A direct target may be redirected or reduced while:

- the process remains horizon-censored;
- the collaboration scope remains broad;
- temporal reach remains stable;
- descendants remain active;
- another support route appears.

Direct target effect and process closure must therefore be reported separately.

## ENG-LE-04 — Preserve-set integrity is first-class evidence

Preservation of unrelated structure is not a side note.

Every B report must record whether the unrelated preserve set remained intact at repair-executor exit and, where observable, through subsequent native continuation.

## ENG-LE-05 — Persistence is a mechanism result

Persistence after a legal one-shot repair must be preserved as observed evidence.

It must not trigger automatic second repair, resampling, or package enlargement.

The semantic audit may examine whether persistence reflects:

- surviving descendants;
- alternative support;
- temporal inertia;
- collaboration-route regeneration;
- independent re-anchoring;
- unresolved local effect.

## ENG-LE-06 — Mixed effect remains mixed

When a local target changes but broader process structure persists or reorganizes, the result is reported as mixed rather than forced into success/failure.

## ENG-LE-07 — Fail-closed boundaries are not negative repair samples

A cell that lacks a legal same-parent repair branch remains a boundary observation.

It is excluded from the paired-effect denominator unless an intervention actually occurred under the frozen repair contract.

## ENG-LE-08 — One-shot discipline remains mandatory

Heterogeneous R7-G1 results do not justify repeated repair until a preferred trajectory appears.

Per eligible pair:

- one frozen parent;
- one frozen package;
- one B;
- repair executor exits;
- watch-only continuation;
- post-hoc audit.

## ENG-LE-09 — Task-family structure is reportable, not presumed universal

R7-G1 showed different within-group patterns across T1, T2 and T3.

Future batches must preserve task-family views so cross-group recurrence can be tested.

A G1 pattern must not be promoted to a universal task law without later evidence.

## ENG-LE-10 — Cross-group engineering synthesis

After G2-G5 engineering batches are frozen, the final synthesis should compare for each X×T condition:

- recurrence of targeted effects;
- recurrence of persistence;
- route migration;
- collaboration-scope change;
- temporal-reach change;
- preserve-set stability;
- fail-closed boundary recurrence;
- CPR migration/re-entry where semantically established.

The goal is a cross-system local-intervention response topology, not a leaderboard.
