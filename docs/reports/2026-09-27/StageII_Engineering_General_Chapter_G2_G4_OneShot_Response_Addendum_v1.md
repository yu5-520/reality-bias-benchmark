# Stage-II Engineering General Chapter Addendum: G2–G4 One-Shot Response Topology v1

Date: 2026-09-27  
Status: **SEALED DESCRIPTIVE CROSS-GROUP SYNTHESIS — G2/G3/G4 ONLY**

This addendum updates the engineering evidence base after three independently frozen prospective repair groups. It does not include G5 and does not replace the per-group semantic audits.

## Frozen cross-group population

Across G2, G3 and G4:

- first-B attempts considered at group level: **36**;
- valid same-parent paired-effect samples: **34**;
- explicit execution boundaries: **2**;
- Natural-A reruns used to manufacture pairs: **0**;
- B reruns used to manufacture preferred outcomes: **0**.

Among the 34 valid same-parent pairs:

- direct target changed: **21/34**;
- clean targeted suppression/redirection: **7/34**;
- persistence: **13/34**;
- mixed effect: **11/34**;
- regeneration: **3/34**;
- unrelated structure preserved: **34/34**.

These are descriptive counts for the frozen study sample, not population prevalence estimates.

## 1. Locality and efficacy remain separate

The clearest cross-group result is not a single repair-success rate. It is the repeated separation between **where the repair acts** and **what the downstream system subsequently does**.

All 34 valid interventions preserved unrelated structure under the frozen repair boundary. Yet downstream process behavior split among targeted redirection/suppression, persistence, mixed response and regeneration.

Therefore locality cannot be used as a proxy for efficacy, and endpoint correctness cannot substitute for post-repair process observation.

## 2. Regeneration is reproduced across groups

G2, G3 and G4 each contain one regeneration case.

The important engineering point is structural: a legal local intervention can preserve its mutation boundary and still reopen, extend or reorganize later collaboration. Repair-generated process expansion is therefore an observed repeated phenomenon in this Stage-II sample.

This supports the existing Engineering General Chapter rule that the monitor must remain active after the repair executor exits.

## 3. Fail-closed boundaries are part of the result

G2 and G4 each preserve an X5-T2 no-effect execution boundary rather than creating a second stochastic repair attempt.

Those boundaries demonstrate a distinct engineering property: structural eligibility and a frozen package do not guarantee that the concrete legal mutation surface can express a non-no-op intervention at execution time.

The correct behavior is to preserve the first attempt and stop, not enlarge scope or rerun until something changes.

## 4. Task-family response topology

### T1 — persistence-dominated

Across 12 valid G2-G4 T1 pairs:

- persistence: **10**;
- mixed effect: **1**;
- regeneration: **1**.

The recurring structure is that unresolved or invalid process states often survive a one-shot local process revision.

### T2 — route redirection, regeneration, and execution boundaries

Across 12 attempted G2-G4 T2 cells:

- valid pairs: **10**;
- explicit execution boundaries: **2**;
- mixed effect: **7**;
- targeted redirection: **1**;
- regeneration: **2**.

T2 is therefore the clearest family for observing how a broad coding request can remain locally constrained at the repair surface while the subsequent collaboration path reorganizes or reopens.

### T3 — stable group-level response distribution

Each of G2, G3 and G4 contains the same group-level T3 response distribution:

- one targeted suppression;
- one targeted redirection;
- one mixed effect;
- one persistence.

The system identity occupying the middle categories is not identical across every group, so this is a recurring response topology rather than a deterministic per-framework law.

## 5. Engineering implication for the final G2–G5 synthesis

The final Engineering General Chapter should not collapse repair into a binary success field. At minimum it should preserve:

1. repair locality / preserve-set integrity;
2. direct target effect;
3. route relation;
4. collaboration-scope change;
5. temporal-reach change;
6. downstream persistence/regeneration;
7. execution/readiness boundaries;
8. terminal outcome as a separate indicator.

G5 remains independent and is not included here until its first-B evidence and paired semantic audit are frozen.

## Claim boundary

This addendum makes no framework ranking, no universal repair-success claim, no population-prevalence estimate and no repeat-run probability estimate. It summarizes the frozen G2-G4 one-shot same-parent contrasts under the Stage-II process-reality design.
