## 5. Why exact-output differences are not enough

The branches remain highly stochastic.

Across the new pairs, both conditions independently produce alternative replanning:

- one control branch reduces current East->West to 500 and stages a later 1000;
- another control reallocates to East->West 800 plus South->West 400;
- intervention branches sometimes reduce East->West to 500, defer East->South, or add stronger buffer gates;
- many other pairs converge on the ordinary East->West 1200 baseline path.

This means that plan restructuring itself is not unique to the intervention condition.

The competing explanation is therefore not hypothetical: **natural provider/process stochasticity visibly generates material replanning under control as well.**

## 8. Methodological value of the negative/mixed result

This result is important for the first paper because it prevents three common overclaims:

1. `structural difference != semantic effect`;
2. `one persistent branch != established System Inertia`;
3. `a rare intervention-side event != intervention specificity`.

At the same time, it preserves the user's probability argument correctly: rare events are not discarded merely because they are rare. They are retained as candidate phenomena, but they require stronger specificity evidence before they can support causal or repair claims.
