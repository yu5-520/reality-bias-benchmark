The central engineering result is therefore **not** "the enhanced repair always
fixes the workflow." It is:

> each stronger intervention removes a different regeneration channel, and a
> process becomes controllably repairable only when route reconstruction,
> coordinated carrier change, execution authority and closure policy are treated
> as separate layers.

The final F arm is especially important because it does not force a success
answer. When the release lead still attempted to reopen work after bounded
verification, the system terminated at an explicit escalation boundary. This is
a stronger process-integrity result than silently continuing until the native
turn limit.

These targeted contrasts do not change the historical 45-pair repair
denominator, the monitor denominators, or the natural-trajectory population.
They are mechanism discrimination experiments on a frozen case.
