# First-Paper Scope under Process Reality v4.3

Date: 2026-09-17  
Status: FORWARD SCOPE / HISTORICAL EVIDENCE PRESERVED  
Supersedes for forward planning: `docs/first_paper_v4.2_scope.md`

## 1. First-paper object

The first paper studies **Process Reality** as a system-level mechanism problem. Its primary object is the recorded multi-Agent process, not terminal task accuracy and not an architecture leaderboard.

The forward mechanism chain is:

```text
Natural Jump
  -> Inherited Inertia
  -> One-shot Path Reorganization
  -> Post-Reorganization Inertia Transition
  -> Structural Inertia Steering / Authority-Localized Recovery
  -> CPR Semantic Closure
```

The chain is analytical. Not every element must be a separate subject run, and no run is required to exhibit every phenomenon.

## 2. Evidence layers

The paper separates:

1. **raw observable facts** — recorded runtime behavior/state/message/invocation/revision evidence;
2. **derived structural evidence** — Jump, lineage, descendants, path topology, inertia and dependency closure;
3. **semantic adjudication** — CPR interpretation after structural evidence is frozen;
4. **terminal outcome** — reported separately as a terminal observation.

A structural candidate is not automatically semantic C/P/R.

## 3. Natural-process claim

The Arena natural condition may use common Agent responsibilities, action interfaces and authority/state interfaces. The paper does not claim the environment is unconstrained in every sense.

The narrower relevant claim is:

> the observed natural topology is not prescribed by active ALR recovery, lineage prompts, required node ordering or a target route.

Passive structural observability records/reconstructs the realized process; it does not define the topology that Agents must follow.

## 4. R2–R4 natural mechanism evidence

R2–R4 establish the natural structural basis:

- local Jump emergence;
- source-backed downstream inheritance/propagation;
- branching, crossing and re-entry;
- retrospective/challenge/correction dynamics;
- inherited system inertia.

CPR remains a later semantic taxonomy over frozen structural evidence rather than a template imposed on every trajectory.

## 5. R5 — one-shot path reorganization

The current formal R5-MID evidence remains the first completed prospective one-shot mechanism batch.

Its role is narrow:

> show whether one bounded local change at a frozen natural J0 can reorganize downstream process topology after the direct experimental mark is removed.

R5 establishes perturbability/path reorganization where supported. It does not establish persistent inertia control.

The frozen R5-MID batch and finalized v3 reports remain unchanged, including their semantic `NOT_ADJUDICATED` status.

## 6. R6 — inertia transition

R6 retains the original detailed post-Jump questions:

- does another Jump appear;
- is it the same family/type or transformed;
- does Jump-derived state disappear, transfer or remain inherited;
- does a retrospective/challenge boundary bring prior structure back;
- is the returned structure inherited again;
- does the lineage decay, reconverge, rebound, reconstruct or persist?

The higher-order object remains:

> **post-reorganization system inertia**.

Recovery, residual and recurrence remain important manifestations but do not exhaust R6.

A future full R6 run may need a longer continuation horizon than the existing R5-MID batch. Such a run requires a new execution freeze and explicit authorization.

## 7. R7 — structural inertia steering

R7 asks whether inherited system inertia can have its **direction, persistence, localization or recoverability changed by structural handling**.

R7 is broader than ALR.

ALR is a specific **Authority-Localized Recovery** operator used as one experimental arm to test whether source-backed inertia has locatable structural carriers and whether the affected dependency closure can be selectively revised/reopened while unrelated successful structure is preserved.

The paper should prefer wording such as **structural steering**, **localized recovery**, **observed redirection** or **recovery failure** until evidence justifies a stronger controllability claim.

## 8. R7 three-condition comparison

The preferred design uses the same frozen J0 / parent state and materially equivalent correction semantics.

### C1 — One-Shot Free Continuation

The correction signal appears once, is consumed and then leaves active experimental context.

Scientific object: **endogenous inherited inertia**.

### C2 — Persistent Field Propagation

The same correction field/signal remains continuously available downstream.

Scientific object: **experiment-maintained persistence**.

### C3 — ALR Authority-Localized Recovery

The same correction semantics are handled through:

- earliest authority-violating ancestor localization;
- dependency-closure derivation;
- affected/preserved partition;
- repair of the violated authority/provenance condition;
- revision lineage/hash;
- local reopen/re-execution of the affected subgraph only.

Scientific object: **causally localized structural recovery**.

## 9. R7 nested mechanism contrasts

### R7-A — C1 vs C2

Question:

> Is downstream persistence still present when the experiment-origin signal is removed, or does persistence depend on continuous experimental exposure?

This separates endogenous system inertia from experiment-maintained state persistence.

### R7-B — C2 vs C3

Question:

> Given materially equivalent correction information, does dependency/lineage-aware localized recovery produce a different process structure from merely keeping the same correction signal continuously visible?

The comparison must not give ALR richer task-solving semantics. The intended variable is structural handling.

## 10. ALR role in the first paper

ALR is not introduced at the start of the theory and is not used to define natural Reality Bias.

Its role comes after natural mechanism and inertia are established:

> if system inertia propagates through locatable structural relations, then a lineage/dependency-closure recovery operator should be able to target those relations more selectively than whole-system rerun or continuous field exposure.

The ALR evidence object should show:

- recovery anchor;
- authority/provenance breach;
- affected dependency closure;
- preserved unaffected structure;
- repaired revision lineage;
- reopened affected subgraph;
- downstream residual/recurrence/re-entry/reconstruction.

Terminal answer quality is secondary.

## 11. R7 primary evidence requirement

Primary process evidence should reuse the Process Reality structural language:

- Jump / descendant Jump lineage;
- affected descendant localization;
- unaffected node/field/branch preservation;
- root reach/depth;
- branch/merge/re-entry;
- cross-Agent relation;
- path-family shift/reconstruction;
- state inheritance;
- residual old inertia;
- recurrence/rebound;
- secondary Jump formation;
- structural steering direction;
- reconvergence/recovery distance;
- terminal/process decoupling.

Possible outcomes include weakening, redirection, delay, localization, transfer, partial recovery, recurrence, secondary bias/Jump, reconstruction, no measurable difference or failure to localize/recover.

## 12. R8 — CPR semantic closure and evidence freeze

R8 closes the semantic layer only after structural evidence is fixed.

It requires:

- a versioned CPR Definition Contract;
- a versioned CPR Adjudication Contract;
- bounded semantic-review packets linked to frozen structural evidence;
- append-only reviewer/adjudication records;
- explicit disagreement/unresolved states;
- paper-level evidence provenance and hashes.

The preferred architecture remains:

> **immutable evidence core + append-only interpretation layers**.

## 13. R9 — external robustness

R9 remains supplementary external robustness/replication:

- additional independent reviewers;
- additional model families;
- additional domains;
- independent implementation or laboratory replication.

R9 is not allowed to redefine the primary mechanism study as a compute-scale benchmark requirement.

## 14. First-paper claim boundaries

The paper may support, only where evidence exists:

- natural Jump/inherited-inertia observations;
- one-shot structural path reorganization;
- post-reorganization inertia differences;
- structural steering/localized recovery evidence under a frozen R7 design;
- CPR semantic findings under a frozen R8 review contract.

It must not infer without evidence:

- stable directional treatment effects;
- universal architecture superiority;
- that persistent-field exposure proves endogenous inertia;
- that ALR is synonymous with all structural control;
- complete hidden-state replay;
- permanent elimination of recurrence beyond the observed horizon;
- semantic CPR truth from structural candidates alone;
- cross-model/domain universality from one experimental environment.
