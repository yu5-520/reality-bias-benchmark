## Core claim family

The first paper is a system-level existence/mechanism study of Reality Bias with one narrow frozen R5-MID containment experiment as its confirmatory empirical core.

The broader theory path remains:

```text
controllable antecedents
→ Tension
→ Escape Propensity
→ observable Jump
→ propagation / operational reach
→ inherited inertia
→ challenge / retrospective dynamics
→ R or recovery
```

The first confirmatory intervention does **not** attempt to measure every element in that chain.

## Frozen first-paper experiment

The empirical contract is:

`RB-FIRST-PAPER-ANALYSIS-CONTRACT-v0.1`

Experiment:

```text
same frozen parent
same selected shared-state value
same task / model / branch plan / non-target state

CONTROL: selected state retains high-certainty status
INTERVENTION: selected state status is downgraded to provisional
```

This is an R5-MID containment experiment. The exact branch-start state is the measurement anchor.

Primary confirmatory structural endpoint:

`R5MID_ANCHOR_DOWNSTREAM_OPERATIONAL_CROSSING_COUNT`

Pair quantity:

```text
intervention count - control count
```

Batch estimand:

```text
mean of eligible complete-pair deltas
```

A lower intervention value is consistent with reduced structural reach under this registered status manipulation in this experimental setting. Mechanical crossing count is not semantic Authority Penetration and does not by itself establish a universal causal law.

## Secondary evidence

Frozen structural secondary outcomes include branch-anchor mechanical reach depth, downstream affected-agent count, downstream event count and visible-agent-turn count.

The key secondary semantic endpoint is Reviewer-v4 adjudicated `authority_penetration` over `BRANCH_START_ANCHOR_PROPAGATION` packets. It is deliberately separate from the primary structural endpoint.

Semantic resolution requires at least two distinct eligible independent reviewers per packet. Agreement resolves directly; disagreement remains unresolved unless an append-only adjudication record references the conflicting parent reviews. `UNCERTAIN`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, insufficient/partial evidence, missing review and unresolved disagreement are missing/non-resolution for the confirmatory binary semantic estimand and are never encoded as `NO`.

The repository now provides a separate semantic aggregation layer so reviewer additions can occur after subject evidence is frozen without changing the subject traces or structural measurements.

## Measurement position

- system trajectory is the analysis unit;
- node/boundary is a measurement location;
- behavior/state transition is the primary observable;
- branch-start state is an exact R5-MID exposure anchor;
- structural lineage and operational crossings are machine-derived;
- C/P/R, semantic adoption, decision effect and Authority Penetration remain bounded semantic judgments;
- hidden chain-of-thought is not required.

## Tension position

Tension supplies an upstream explanatory source for Escape Propensity and gives later PRE experiments manipulable antecedent variables.

The first paper may state:

`controllable antecedents → Tension → Escape Propensity → Jump`

as a theoretical mechanism, but its R5-MID confirmatory experiment only makes empirical claims about the registered status manipulation and its measured downstream system consequences. Direct Tension measurement is outside this paper's completion criteria.

## CPR position

CPR remain semantic mechanisms inside the larger trajectory theory rather than the whole theory:

- C/P describe first-order state deviation;
- propagation/operational reach describe how deviations can enter system reality;
- R describes second-order retrospective stabilization/regeneration dynamics;
- recovery is kept distinct from R.

## Evidence and reviewer position

The system records and freezes subject evidence first. Structural candidate localization, lineage, branch-anchor exposure and mechanical crossings are derived from that fixed evidence. Reviewers receive bounded packets rather than full hidden reasoning or full trajectories.

Reviewer records are append-only. Disagreement, uncertainty and missingness are preserved. Subject runs are not repeated to manufacture reviewer agreement or to repair an inconvenient outcome.

## Excluded from first-paper completion criteria

- direct Tension measurement;
- full PRE variable-response curves;
- full POST stabilization program;
- complete model × domain × topology matrix;
- hidden chain-of-thought reconstruction;
- broad industrial tool/MCP/A2A ecosystem coverage;
- universal architecture ranking;
- complete cross-domain generalization.

Any later expansion changes the research program additively; it does not silently rewrite the frozen first-paper v0.1 contract.
