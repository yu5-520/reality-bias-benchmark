# Observation vs Explanation under v4

Date: 2026-09-16

Forward work separates:

- **Observation:** what behavior/state transition occurred and where;
- **Semantic adjudication:** whether the localized event is C/P/R or Authority-relevant;
- **Mechanism explanation:** which controlled variables change its incidence or downstream effect.

This avoids using a reviewer’s narrative explanation as a substitute for controlled intervention evidence.
