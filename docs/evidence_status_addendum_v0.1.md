# Evidence Status Addendum v0.1

Date: 2026-09-17  
Status: FORWARD CLAIM-BOUNDARY INDEX / NO NEW SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE

## 1. Purpose

This addendum prevents one observed trajectory difference from being used to support multiple scientifically distinct claims at once.

Each claim family requires its own evidence.

## 2. Local perturbation response

Question:

> What happened when the subject directly received the one-shot perturbation?

Required evidence:

- exact input delta;
- one-shot delivery/consumption integrity;
- actor/turn;
- immediate action/message/write/invocation/finalization;
- target-related reference/use evidence where available.

Current status:

`OBSERVABLE_IN_FROZEN_R5MID`

Boundary:

Direct temporal adjacency alone does not establish a condition effect beyond natural variation.

## 3. System inertia / inheritance

Question:

> What influence persisted after the direct experiment-origin signal exited active context, and through what carrier?

Required evidence:

- post-consumption window;
- source-backed carrier/lineage;
- downstream delivery/read/use/inheritance/propagation as supported;
- matched horizon or censoring.

Current status:

`NATURAL_INERTIA_CANDIDATES_OBSERVABLE`

Strongest current frozen example:

A2 provides multi-turn, multi-Agent structural continuation including O -> A -> I -> O, branch/merge structure and role re-entry.

Boundary:

B2 natural early termination cannot establish permanent extinction or zero inertia.

## 4. Intervention-related inertia transition

Question:

> Is the post-consumption difference attributable to the intervention rather than ordinary natural process variation?

Required evidence:

- natural repeated-continuation baseline;
- frozen process-distance dimensions;
- post-consumption comparison;
- repeated condition structure where feasible;
- explicit censoring.

Current status:

`NOT_YET_ESTABLISHED`

Reason:

The current natural A1/A2 trajectories already exhibit large structural divergence, so a single A/B difference cannot by itself identify an intervention effect.

## 5. Target specificity

Question:

> Is the J0-targeted inertia response distinguishable from generic one-shot factual uncertainty?

Required evidence:

- S0/S1/S2 prospective design;
- outcome-blind S1 selection;
- mechanically equivalent S1/S2 intervention;
- post-consumption R6 inertia profiles;
- `S2-S1` primary contrast.

Current status:

`PROSPECTIVE_NOT_EXECUTED`

Offline exact-source audit, target selection and operator readiness exist; scientific S-arm outcomes do not.

## 6. CPR semantic identity
