## 5. Main engineering conclusion

The frozen G1 result separates two engineering properties that must remain distinct:

1. **repair locality** — whether the intervention remains inside the declared target/preserve boundary;
2. **downstream efficacy** — whether later process dynamics redirect, suppress, persist, reorganize, or remain inconclusive.

R7-G1 establishes that a one-shot content-addressed repair can remain boundary-limited even when downstream process inertia is not fully removed.

Therefore future engineering reports must not collapse `boundary compliance` and `repair effect` into one success flag.
