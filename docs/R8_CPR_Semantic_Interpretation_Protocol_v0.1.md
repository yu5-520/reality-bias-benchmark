# R8 CPR Semantic Interpretation Protocol v0.1

Date: 2026-09-18  
Status: FORWARD SEMANTIC REVIEW PROTOCOL / FROZEN-EVIDENCE ONLY

## 1. Purpose

R8 interprets frozen process evidence produced by earlier stages.

It does not rerun subjects and does not mutate raw evidence.

## 2. Unit of analysis

The primary unit is a semantic process event, not an exclusive task-level label.

Multiple C/P/R events may occur in one trajectory, may overlap in time, and may form transition chains.

Examples such as `C -> P -> C` are hypotheses to be adjudicated from evidence, not hard-coded classifications.

## 3. Candidate semantic questions

R8 may investigate:

- whether shared-pool information is verified or merely treated as premise;
- whether multiple Agent supports are independent evidence or descendants of one root lineage;
- whether logical completion is distributed across Agents;
- whether scope expansion is evidence-seeking or coherence-seeking;
- whether uncertainty is preserved at the source while certainty migrates to operational descendants;
- whether a later event is exact, semantic or structural recurrence;
- whether C/P/R events induce one another.

## 4. Evidence discipline

Every semantic event record must bind to frozen refs.

A reviewer may not invent missing lineage.

Unknown or insufficient cases remain `UNRESOLVED`.

Machine structure is evidence input; it is not itself a semantic verdict.

## 5. Collective completion

A candidate Collective Completion event requires investigation of whether:

- multiple Agents contribute local logical closures;
- those closures share a root premise/lineage;
- derived support is distinguishable from independent evidence;
- newly derived semantics become reusable shared premises.

The existence of reasonable inference alone is not sufficient for a bias verdict.

## 6. P-like expansion

Structural expansion is only a surface signal.

R8 must inspect whether expanded roles/information were required to obtain independent evidence or primarily served additional completeness/coherence pressure after an executable closure already existed.

## 7. R-like persistence/recurrence

Repeated wording is insufficient.

R8 requires a traceable carrier, adoption/re-entry relation or structure-supported recurrence.

Post-stimulus persistence should be distinguished from experiment-maintained exposure.

## 8. Probability

A trajectory without a candidate event is a non-event trajectory, not proof of zero structural risk.

Later analysis should separate incidence, transition probability, propagation depth, persistence, recurrence and impact.

## 9. Reviewer model

Semantic audit material is fixed first.

One or more reviewers may be added later without rerunning the subject.

Paid/multi-model review is optional supplementary work and is not required to preserve the evidence.
