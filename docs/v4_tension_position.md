# Tension Position under Reality Bias v4

Date: 2026-09-16

Tension is an upstream theoretical construct introduced to give Escape Propensity experimentally addressable antecedents.

The first paper does not require a direct Tension measure.

Forward empirical form:

```text
manipulable antecedent X
→ predicted change in Jump-related behavior
```

Possible antecedents include goal conflict, evidence conflict, context competition, authority mismatch, information load and alternative path availability.

A change in Jump behavior after manipulating X does not by itself prove that internal Tension was directly measured. Tension remains an explanatory construct until stronger convergent evidence is available.
