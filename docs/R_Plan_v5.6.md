## 1. Purpose

## 2. Experimental stages are observation contexts, not CPR labels

No experimental stage is permanently mapped to one CPR dimension.

- R2-R4 may expose C, P and R under natural stochastic collaboration.
- R5 may expose C/P/R response after one bounded authority challenge.
- R6 may expose C/P/R through passive carrier, reader, adoption, re-entry, recomputation and persistence structure.
- R7 may expose C/P/R under persistent correction and explicit repair/reopen/recompute conditions.

Thus:

`R stage != CPR label`.
