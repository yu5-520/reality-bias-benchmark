# First-Paper Scope under Process Reality v5.3

Date: 2026-09-19  
Status: FORWARD SCOPE / PROBABILITY-STRUCTURE SEPARATED  
Predecessor: `docs/first_paper_v5_scope.md`

## 1. Paper thesis

The first paper studies Process Reality Fidelity in stochastic multi-Agent systems.

It separates two questions:

1. **Probability:** under a frozen domain environment, how often and where do candidate phenomena naturally appear?
2. **Structure:** once a case appears, what carrier/read/adoption/persistence structure is realized?

## 2. Domain-level evidence

The held-out three-domain cohort is the probability layer.

Claims about occurrence frequency, cross-domain distribution or rarity must use natural trajectories as the denominator.

The current denominator is 30 natural trajectories per held-out domain.

## 3. Case-level evidence

Localized semantic audit, R5 and R6 operate on realized source-bound cases.

R6 may establish case-level carrier/adoption/persistence structure without requiring repeated reproduction of the same exact trajectory.

## 4. R5/R6 boundary

R5 may provide one minimal local probe.

R6 then passively reads the frozen continuation.

A new R6 subject experiment is not required.

## 5. System Inertia

The paper must distinguish:

- case-level System Inertia evidence;
- domain-level occurrence rate of inertia;
- local fixed-parent recurrence of one exact phenotype.

Only the first is an R6 structural claim.

## 6. Optional local sensitivity

Fixed-parent repetition is supplementary robustness only.

It must be reported separately and may not be used as a domain prevalence denominator.

## 7. R7

R7 is entered only when the case-level affected lineage is sufficiently complete and separately authorized.

Fixed-parent recurrence is not a repair gate.

## 8. First-paper nonclaims

Do not claim:

- that one realized structure is common merely because it exists;
- that a rare structure is unimportant merely because it is rare;
- that fixed-parent repetition estimates domain probability;
- that non-reproduction erases a recorded carrier/adoption chain;
- that R6 requires a new provider experiment;
- that cross-domain probability implies identical structural manifestation.

## 9. Reporting rule

Every major result declares its claim unit:

`DOMAIN_LEVEL_OCCURRENCE | CASE_LEVEL_STRUCTURE | SUPPLEMENTARY_LOCAL_CONDITIONAL_SENSITIVITY`.
