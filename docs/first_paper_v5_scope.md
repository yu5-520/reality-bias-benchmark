## 1. Paper thesis

The first paper studies Process Reality Fidelity in multi-Agent systems.

Its central object is how multi-source conditions create unstable information, how that information changes semantic form during transmission, how descendants obtain structural support and enter directly consumable shared state, and how such structure can later be scouted, traced and locally revised.

Terminal answer correctness is a downstream endpoint, not the definition of process reality.

## 2. Contribution layers

1. **Phenomenon** — terminally similar runs may contain materially different process reality.
2. **Formation mechanism** — unstable information may be transformed across an Escape Structure into supported descendants and shared state.
3. **Structural stabilization** — some descendants may become directly addressable by later Agents through a stable shared information pool.
4. **Identification** — structural scouting can nominate bounded regions for semantic audit and trace support/pool/exposure relations.
5. **Control** — evidence-supported affected structures may permit localized repair without whole-process suppression.
6. **Semantic closure** — CPR remains append-only semantic adjudication over frozen evidence.

## 3. Main evidence chain

\`raw process -> structural facts -> support/pool/exposure candidates -> localized semantic audit -> bounded mechanism interpretation\`

No structural candidate automatically proves semantic transformation, support, pool entry, inertia or CPR.

## 4. Historical J0

Historical J0 remains an important frozen anchor.

The paper must not call J0 the earliest semantic origin merely because it was selected as an intervention anchor.

Forward re-analysis asks whether J0 is better interpreted as:

- a Structural Support candidate;
- a later Structural Exposure anchor;
- both under different evidence scopes;
- or neither if evidence is insufficient.

## 5. R5 position

R5 contributes:

- a structural-scouting method that can reduce all-event semantic review;
- a minimal driver probe that tests whether source-status change reaches already materialized descendants.

The existing one-shot authority withdrawal is evidence about one operator. It does not define R5 and does not prove that all persistence is structure-driven.

## 6. R6 position

R6 is the central structural-identification layer for:

- support location;
- pool entry;
- exposure;
- upstream trace;
- downstream closure;
- intervention-surface alternatives.

System Inertia remains one downstream property of this structure rather than the sole R6 object.

## 7. R7 position

R7 tests local recovery over an explicit intervention surface and RepairClosure.

Scientific success requires both integrity improvement and preservation of unrelated process structure.

## 8. First-paper nonclaims

Do not claim without evidence:

- that unstable information is false;
- that Escape is a hidden Agent trait;
- that J0 is semantic origin;
- that every support point is observable in real time;
- that stable shared state is epistemically correct;
- that source withdrawal is the sufficient perturbation operator;
- that one control surface is universally optimal;
- that structural reachability proves semantic affectedness;
- that current evidence establishes universal R7 efficacy;
- that current single-environment evidence generalizes across models/domains.

## 9. Engineering implication

The paper may motivate a lightweight Process Integrity Protocol with:

\`Observe -> Scout -> Address -> Trace -> Localize -> Select Surface -> Repair if authorized -> Resume -> Verify\`

The engineering layer remains a mechanism-derived implication, not a production-safety claim.

## 10. Historical compatibility

Historical reports and frozen experiments keep their original terminology and identifiers. v5 interpretation is append-only.
