## Supplementary Note 2 — Trajectory-first semantic audit contract

The canonical audit unit is a complete **Dynamic Semantic Episode** embedded in a frozen multi-agent trajectory. Review proceeds from chronological evidence reconstruction to functional semantic lineage, goal/authorization/process/result alignment, retrospective and censor timelines, joint C/P/R adjudication and coupling analysis.

C denotes information permission, P collaboration/execution-scope permission, and R retrospective permission that regenerates process work. Structural events are evidence locators rather than semantic verdicts.

Healthy alternatives remain explicit: uncertainty-preserving continuation, independent re-anchoring, ordinary inheritance, boundary preservation and conservative pre-existing gates.

## Supplementary Note 4 — R5 one-shot authority perturbation

Canonical R5 challenges an already-recorded authority state once at the next relevant reader as fact → unconfirmed. The persistent historical state is not rewritten by the one-shot probe.

The 29-case canonical set is interpreted as bounded intervention-response evidence rather than an average-treatment-effect design. Each case has one frozen natural reference and one intervention continuation, without newly sampled matched controls or canonical replicates.

Across the 29 cases, 25 persistence outcomes are better explained by ordinary inheritance, uncertainty-preserving continuation, independent re-anchoring or pre-existing conservative gates. Four show stronger case-level persistence structures. Persistence alone is therefore not classified as System Inertia.

## Supplementary Note 5 — R6 lineage and System Inertia identification

R6 is passive over frozen evidence. It reconstructs:

source → local response → semantic carrier → downstream read → semantic adoption → decision/action dependence → post-stimulus persistence.

The analysis also records candidate affected closure and intervention surfaces. A lineage is not authorized for repair when source, transformation, authority-history, pool-state or dependent-descendant information remains unresolved.

## Supplementary Note 6 — R7 lineage-addressed repair

R7-P and R7-S operate on different control surfaces. R7-P persistently corrects downstream reads while inherited shared state remains. R7-S changes the internal lineage, invalidates affected descendants where needed, reopens dependent recipients and selectively recomputes the affected closure while preserving compatible state.

The four canonical structured-repair cases are:

- **wave-3-56ee79f97f54 — Supply Chain.** Selective recompute with parameter divergence; 1 reference invalidated, 8 calls reopened and 43 descendants recomputed. Final authority: unconfirmed. Re-entry: false.
- **wave-4-8b1731b57396 — Supply Chain.** Selective recompute with action recomposition; 1 reference invalidated, 8 calls reopened and 35 descendants recomputed. Final authority: recommendation. Re-entry: false.
- **wave-4-cf726639de1d — Supply Chain.** Reconvergence with fresh authority regeneration; 0 references invalidated, 8 calls reopened and 28 descendants recomputed. Final authority: fact. Re-entry: true.
- **wave-6-7c7e526e4d91 — Software Engineering.** Reconvergence with compatible decision reaffirmation; 1 reference invalidated, 8 calls reopened and 29 descendants recomputed. Final authority: recommendation. Re-entry: false.

Compatible or unrelated structure was preserved in 4/4 canonical repair cases. Two materially diverged and two reconverged after recomputation. This does not establish universal superiority of R7-S over persistent correction.

## Supplementary Note 7 — Censoring and negative comparators

Trajectories are distinguished as natural complete, control-boundary complete, externally censored active process or failure terminated. Positive prefixes before censoring remain usable evidence. Unobserved tails do not support absence claims.

Negative comparators are part of the mechanism definition: continuation does not imply C; additional collaboration alone does not imply P; reopening alone does not imply R; persistence alone does not imply System Inertia.

## Supplementary Note 8 — External-validity and generalization boundary

The formal E-commerce collection, the 90 held-out Finance/Supply Chain/Software Engineering trajectories and canonical active R5/R7 extensions use the same frozen DeepSeek subject configuration and common Arena family. Domain variation is therefore broader than subject-model variation.

A historical blind reviewer comparison used a different reviewer model on the same frozen evidence and informs measurement reliability, not cross-model subject replication. The study does not establish model-family invariance, provider invariance, topology invariance or universal inter-system generalization.
