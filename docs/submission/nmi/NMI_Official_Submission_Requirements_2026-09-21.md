# Nature Machine Intelligence — Official Initial-Submission Requirements Snapshot

Verified: 2026-09-21  
Status: **LIVE PRE-SUBMISSION REVALIDATION / SUBMISSION-ENGINEERING REFERENCE ONLY**

This snapshot was revalidated immediately before P8 packaging against the current official Nature Machine Intelligence pages. It is not scientific evidence and must be re-checked again if submission is delayed materially.

## Article format

Current Nature Machine Intelligence Article format:

- main text: up to **3,500 words**, excluding abstract, Methods, references and figure legends;
- abstract: up to **150 words**, unreferenced;
- display items: up to **6** figures and/or tables;
- structure:
  - Introduction without heading;
  - Results;
  - Discussion;
  - Methods;
- Results and Methods should use topical subheadings;
- Discussion should not contain subheadings;
- references: typically recommended up to **50**;
- supplementary information may accompany the Article.

Official source:
- https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/content

## Initial-submission file format

The journal currently states that initial submission does not require special formatting as long as the work is suitable for editorial assessment and peer review.

Accepted initial formats include:

- PDF;
- Word;
- TeX/LaTeX with compiled PDF.

Official source:
- https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/submission-guidelines/initial-formatting

## Required submission materials

The current preparing-submission page lists:

- manuscript file, including Methods and figures/Extended Data if applicable;
- cover letter;
- Supplementary Information if applicable.

The manuscript file must contain author names and affiliations unless the authors choose double-anonymized peer review. If double-anonymized peer review is chosen, author identity and contact information belong in the cover letter instead.

LLMs do not satisfy Nature authorship criteria. Use of an LLM must be documented in the Methods section or another suitable part if Methods is unavailable.

Official source:
- https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/submission-guidelines/preparing-your-submission

## Cover-letter requirements

The cover letter should explain:

- the importance of the work;
- why it is appropriate for the diverse Nature Machine Intelligence readership.

It should also disclose:

1. related manuscripts under consideration or in press elsewhere;
2. prior discussions with a Nature Machine Intelligence editor;
3. if double-anonymized peer review is chosen, affiliation and contact information for all authors.

Reviewer recommendations/exclusions are optional.

Official source:
- https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/submission-guidelines/preparing-your-submission

## Double-anonymized peer review

Double-anonymized peer review is optional. Authors choosing it are responsible for properly anonymizing the manuscript and selecting the option in the manuscript tracking system.

Official source:
- https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/submission-guidelines/dapr

## Competing interests

Authors must disclose competing interests during submission. Nature Machine Intelligence also requires a competing-interest statement in the published article. Under double-anonymized peer review, the submission system receives a minimal anonymous statement while the full disclosure belongs in the cover letter.

Official source:
- https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/editorial-policies/competing-interests

## Editorial screening

After completeness checks, editors decide whether to send a manuscript to peer review based on:

- degree to which it advances understanding of the field;
- soundness of conclusions;
- extent to which evidence/data/analyses support the conclusions;
- wide relevance to the journal readership.

Official source:
- https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/submission-guidelines/editorial-process

## Writing and accessibility

The journal asks authors to communicate clearly to non-specialists, explain unavoidable technical terminology, minimize abbreviations and make the background, rationale and conclusions clear.

Official source:
- https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/submission-guidelines/writing-and-language
