# NMI-P4 Reviewer Attack 05 — R5 Causal-Boundary Attack

Date: 2026-09-21  
Status: **CAUSAL CLAIM BOUNDED / MECHANISM EVIDENCE RETAINED / NO NEW SUBJECT RUN**

## Attack question

> Canonical R5 uses only one one-shot perturbation continuation per frozen case. Why should any downstream difference be interpreted causally rather than as ordinary stochastic variation?

This is a valid attack.

The correct answer is not that one stochastic branch proves a treatment effect.

It does not.

The scientific task is to identify exactly what R5 controls, what it observes, what later stages add, and where causal language must stop.

## 1. Canonical R5 geometry

The frozen canonical protocol is:

`N0 frozen natural continuation + one R5-I intervention continuation`.

Per case:

- existing natural reference: 1;
- newly sampled natural control: 0;
- new intervention continuation: 1;
- canonical replicate count: 1;
- same-parent matched synthetic control: 0.

The current operator is:

`fact -> unconfirmed`

shown once in the copied runtime view of the first resumed Agent.

The operator:

- does not rewrite persistent Arena/shared state;
- is not automatically re-injected;
- is localized to the selected reader surface.

The canonical mechanism set contains:

- 29 frozen R5-I trajectories;
- 29 corresponding original natural histories;
- 29 complete-route R5/R6 semantic audits.

## 2. What is directly controlled

The strongest directly causal statement is about the intervention surface itself:

> **The selected next reader was experimentally exposed to a reduced authority status once.**

This is not inferred from outcome differences.

It is part of the execution operator.

Therefore the experiment directly establishes:

`selected source authority in runtime view: fact -> unconfirmed`.

It does **not** directly establish:

`R5 uniquely caused every later semantic or terminal difference`.

## 3. What is directly observed after intervention

R5 directly records the realized continuation after the localized authority withdrawal.

Observed response classes include, for example:

- independent multi-evidence re-anchoring of the same decision;
- creation of a new descendant semantic carrier;
- reinterpretation into a different plan carrier;
- continued operationalization of a constraint through transformed descendants.

These are:

> **responses observed under the intervention**

not:

> average treatment effects.

The distinction is essential.

## 4. Why N0 is a reference, not a randomized counterfactual

The natural continuation N0 is historically frozen and is not regenerated.

This protects the original stochastic realization from outcome-conditioned rerunning.

However, N0 and R5-I are not a randomized matched-control pair.

Consequently:

`N0 != exact counterfactual world for R5-I`.

A difference between N0 and R5-I can be consistent with an intervention effect, but stochastic continuation remains a compatible contributor.

The study therefore does not estimate:

- an average treatment effect;
- an individual causal effect under a fixed random seed;
- a probability that the perturbation changes the endpoint;
- a universal direction of response.

## 5. What the heterogeneous response paths add

The same one-shot operator is followed by different realized semantic families.

### Finance comparator

A challenged credit-lead decision is independently rebuilt from:

- policy threshold;
- downside stress;
- collateral;
- cash-flow serviceability.

The endpoint reconverges, but source authority is not required as the continuing carrier.

### Supply-chain stronger candidate

A challenged logistics fact is followed by a new inventory carrier that preserves the lane constraint downstream.

These opposite response families matter because they reject a simplistic directional law such as:

> “making a fact unconfirmed always weakens the final decision.”

The operator is better interpreted as a **diagnostic perturbation** exposing how the process reconstructs or carries meaning.

## 6. R6 does not magically create unique causality

R6 is passive.

It consumes:

`N0 + R5-I`

and reconstructs:

- source;
- exposed reader;
- new carrier;
- downstream reads;
- semantic adoption;
- decision/action dependence;
- persistence;
- independent re-anchoring;
- affected closure.

Its key contribution is ancestry discrimination.

Across 29 canonical cases:

- semantic adoption / downstream dependence / persistence is observed in 29/29;
- 25/29 are better explained by ordinary inheritance, independent re-anchoring, boundary preservation or pre-existing gates;
- 4/29 show stronger descendant-carrier structures consistent with System Inertia candidates;
- unique R5 causal attribution remains 0/29 established.

Thus R6 strengthens mechanism localization while preserving the causal boundary.

## 7. What R7 contributes

R7 acts on the lineage identified after R5/R6.

In the four canonical repair-ready cases, structured repair:

- reopens 8 calls per case;
- recomputes bounded descendant closures;
- produces material divergence in 2/4;
- produces compatible reconvergence in 2/4;
- preserves compatible/unrelated structure in 4/4.

This creates a second intervention surface at the lineage level.

It supports **intervention coherence**:

> a lineage localized by the R5/R6 stack can later be selectively modified and downstream computation can be rebuilt from that modification.

But R7 still does not retroactively convert R5 into a randomized treatment-effect experiment.

The evidence chain is therefore:

`natural occurrence`
-> `localized authority perturbation`
-> `realized response`
-> `semantic lineage localization`
-> `lineage-level repair/recomputation`.

This is mechanism triangulation, not unique causal identification.

## 8. Allowed causal vocabulary

### Allowed

- localized perturbation;
- controlled authority-status exposure;
- realized response under intervention;
- intervention-response evidence;
- local dependence probe;
- mechanism-compatible response;
- lineage localization;
- intervention coherence across probe and repair stages.

### Not allowed

- R5 proves the terminal outcome was caused by the status change;
- unique R5 causality;
- average treatment effect;
- treatment probability;
- universal effect direction;
- 29 cases as causal prevalence;
- identical rerun reproducibility as a requirement for mechanism validity.

## 9. Why not repeatedly rerun until a majority appears?

In a stochastic multi-Agent process, repeated same-parent reruns generate new realizations.

If reruns are continued after observing the initial outcome until a preferred majority or recurring phenotype appears, the research object changes from:

> the realized natural/intervention path

to:

> an outcome-conditioned resampling distribution chosen after inspection.

The canonical design deliberately avoids that move.

Earlier repeated-parent development evidence remains supplementary sensitivity material but is not promoted into the canonical R5 geometry.

## 10. Falsification boundary

The R5 mechanism interpretation would be weakened if:

- the supposedly one-shot operator had silently modified persistent state;
- the intervention were repeatedly re-injected downstream;
- later semantic carriers could not be tied to readable evidence;
- healthy independent re-anchoring were systematically mislabeled as source-specific dependence;
- R6/R7 lineage localization failed to bind the purported affected descendants.

The frozen protocol/report stack explicitly audits these boundaries.

## Verdict

Attack:

**“One stochastic one-shot branch cannot establish causality.”**

Verdict:

**SUPPORTED AGAINST STRONG CAUSAL LANGUAGE; DOES NOT INVALIDATE THE BOUNDED MECHANISM ROLE OF R5.**

Canonical interpretation:

> **R5 is a localized intervention-response probe, not a treatment-effect estimator.**

The paper may use R5 to expose a realized dependence surface and to motivate R6 lineage analysis.

It may not use R5 alone to claim unique causality, a universal effect direction or a treatment probability.
