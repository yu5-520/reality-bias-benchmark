# Cover Letter — Nature Machine Intelligence

Dear Editors,

We submit the Article **“Process reality in multi-agent AI systems”** for consideration in *Nature Machine Intelligence*.

Multi-agent AI systems are commonly evaluated by answer quality or task success. Our study asks a complementary reliability question: what representations does an interacting system become permitted to treat as real, actionable or still operative while it works? In a natural planning trajectory, an inventory estimate remained numerically unchanged yet moved from preliminary and unreconciled to a reconciled fact without new independent warehouse confirmation. We use this observation to define **process reality** and then trace information, execution-scope and historical-process permission through complete frozen trajectories.

The study links natural observations to functional semantic lineage, a bounded one-reader authority perturbation, passive ancestry analysis and content-addressed lineage repair. This mechanism chain distinguishes ordinary propagation and healthy re-anchoring from stronger permission drift, and shows that a disputed semantic lineage can be selectively reopened and recomputed while compatible state is preserved.

We deliberately bound the claims. The combined evidence inventory is heterogeneous and is not used as a prevalence denominator; the formal subject-model family is fixed; the final semantic protocol is retrospective; and inter-system generalization remains a future test. These boundaries are stated directly in the manuscript and Supplementary Information.

We believe the work is relevant to machine-intelligence reliability because endpoint success can leave the process that authorized intermediate reality unqualified. The study makes that process observable, auditable and locally repairable without requiring the entire stochastic run to become the repair unit.
