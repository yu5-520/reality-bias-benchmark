## 2. Submission thesis

The paper is not positioned as:

- a new multi-Agent failure taxonomy;
- a large-scale benchmark;
- a prevalence study;
- a compute-heavy cross-model comparison.

The primary position is:

> **A mechanism-first study of Process Reality in multi-Agent AI systems.**

The paper asks:

> **What process reality does an interacting AI system construct while operating, and can that reality be traced and locally repaired when semantic, epistemic, authorization or temporal conditions drift?**

Multi-Agent interaction is the controlled experimental realization.

The broader outlook is:

> **Inter-System Process Reality / Semantic Process Integrity.**

## 3. Four claim levels

### Level 1 — Experimental fact

Frozen multi-Agent trajectories show that terminally plausible or stable outcomes can coexist with different internal semantic and permission trajectories.

### Level 2 — Mechanism

Dynamic C/P/R and Functional Semantic Lineage describe how:

- information authority;
- collaboration / execution scope;
- historical process permission

change through complete trajectories and can couple directionally.

### Level 3 — Control

Content-addressed lineage closure, affected closure and semantic repair packets allow local intervention and selective recomputation without treating the whole stochastic run as the repair unit.

### Level 4 — Outlook

The same structural objects suggest a broader future problem:

> how process reality crosses interacting system boundaries while preserving or losing semantic meaning, epistemic status, authority scope and historical validity.

Level 4 is an outlook abstraction, not a completed universal generalization.

## 7. NMI editorial objective

The desk-review problem is not:

> “Can a reviewer understand every R-stage?”

It is:

> **Can an editor quickly see a sound, evidence-supported advance with wide relevance?**

Therefore the first page and Figure 1 must make clear that:

- the paper is mechanism-first;
- endpoint metrics miss the target object;
- multi-Agent is the experimental scene rather than the theoretical ceiling;
- the study contains observation, intervention and control;
- system-level generalization is clearly labeled as outlook.

## 8. First-submission freeze rule

Before submission, new work is admitted to the main first-paper evidence only if it repairs a concrete defect in one of:

- validity of a main claim;
- evidence binding;
- experimental integrity;
- reproducibility;
- journal compliance.

New ideas that merely make the theory broader or more complete do not reopen the experimental programme.

This rule exists specifically to prevent supplementary completeness goals from becoming a new blocking dependency.
