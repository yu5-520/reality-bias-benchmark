## 5. Opening prose guard

The opening must preserve the exact conceptual distinction:

`value correctness`
!=
`epistemic status`
!=
`operational permission`.

Do not write:

> “The system hallucinated the 1,520 value.”

The evidence does not establish that.

Prefer:

> **The same value moved from preliminary/unreconciled to fact/reconciled without new independent warehouse reconciliation, increasing the permission attached to its downstream use.**

This is the opening evidence surface.
