## Numerator / denominator discipline

### Allowed

- formal ecommerce Batch001 may report its own 3-case denominator;
- held-out natural population may report 90 when referring to that frozen cohort;
- R5 canonical mechanism set may report 29 when referring to that selected set;
- R7 engineering closure may report 4 complete repair cases;
- R8 may report supported mechanism counts inside the 150 reviewed inventory.

### Not allowed

- C/P/R prevalence over the combined 150;
- merging discovery/development/mechanism/intervention trajectories into one population rate;
- cross-version rate comparisons without matched population conditions;
- converting active-censored tails into negatives.

## Manuscript prose guard

Every sentence containing one of the following must have an evidence route or explicit outlook label:

- `shows`;
- `demonstrates`;
- `establishes`;
- `supports`;
- `causes`;
- `generates`;
- `generalizes`;
- `prevents`;
- `improves`;
- `reduces`.

The submission manuscript must not promote a Tier D idea into a Tier A empirical claim.
