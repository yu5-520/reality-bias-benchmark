# Cover Letter Draft v0.1 — Nature Machine Intelligence

Dear Editors,

We submit the Article **“Process reality in multi-agent AI systems.”**

Multi-agent AI systems are commonly evaluated by answer quality or task success. Our study asks a complementary reliability question: what representations does an interacting system become permitted to treat as real, actionable or still operative while it works? In a natural planning trajectory, an inventory estimate remained numerically unchanged yet moved from preliminary and unreconciled to a reconciled fact without new independent warehouse confirmation. We use this observation to define **process reality** and then trace information, execution-scope and historical-process permission through complete frozen trajectories.

The paper links natural observations to functional semantic lineage, a bounded one-reader authority perturbation, passive ancestry analysis and content-addressed lineage repair. This mechanism chain lets us distinguish ordinary propagation and healthy re-anchoring from stronger permission drift, and shows that a disputed semantic lineage can be selectively reopened and recomputed while compatible state is preserved. The claim is deliberately narrower than universal prevalence or cross-model invariance: the combined evidence inventory is heterogeneous, the formal subject-model family is fixed, and the final semantic protocol is retrospective. We present those limitations directly and keep inter-system generalization as an explicit future test.

We believe the work is relevant to machine-intelligence reliability because endpoint success can leave the process that authorized intermediate reality unqualified. The study turns that hidden process into an observable, auditable and locally repairable object without requiring the entire stochastic run to become the repair unit.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.

Sincerely,  
The author
