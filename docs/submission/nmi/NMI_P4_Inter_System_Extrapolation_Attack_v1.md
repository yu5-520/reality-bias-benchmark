# NMI-P4 Reviewer Attack 07 — Inter-System Extrapolation Boundary

Date: 2026-09-21  
Status: **OVERREACH ATTACK HARDENED / OUTLOOK BOUNDARY EXPLICIT / NO NEW EXECUTION**

## Attack question

> The experiments are all inside one multi-agent runtime. Why is the Discussion allowed to generalize toward Human-AI, database-workflow, service-service or other system boundaries?

This attack is valid.

The first paper does **not** empirically establish universal Inter-System Process Reality.

The correct defence is to separate what was observed, what is abstracted, and what remains a future hypothesis.

## 1. What the experiments actually test

The empirical programme includes:

- e-commerce discovery/mechanism trajectories;
- 90 held-out natural trajectories across Finance, Supply Chain and Software Engineering;
- 29 canonical R5 intervention-response cases;
- R6 semantic-lineage/inertia analysis;
- R7 persistent-correction and lineage-repair trajectories;
- R8 trajectory-first semantic audit.

All of these remain inside an interacting multi-agent experimental architecture.

The three held-out domains provide **domain variation**.

They do not provide:

- independently governed source/target systems;
- database-to-workflow replication;
- Human-to-AI replication;
- service-to-service replication;
- organizational boundary replication;
- model-native protocol replication.

Therefore:

> **cross-domain != cross-system validation**.

## 2. What the data do support at a more abstract level

The experiments support a narrower structural observation:

> **representation content and the permission attached to that content are separable variables.**

Inside the studied trajectories, the same or functionally equivalent content can move while:

- epistemic status changes;
- operational authority changes;
- collaboration/execution scope changes;
- historical process state re-enters;
- semantic descendants preserve or transform the source function.

This motivates a boundary question that is broader than Agent-Agent communication.

It does not answer that broader question.

## 3. Allowed scale shift

The paper may say:

> Multi-agent interaction provides a tractable experimental setting in which semantic content, authority, scope and historical re-entry can be frozen and inspected.

It may then propose:

> Similar integrity questions may arise when representations cross other independently governed state/authority boundaries.

This is a **mechanism-induced research hypothesis**.

It is not:

> evidence that arbitrary interacting systems exhibit Dynamic C/P/R.

## 4. Inter-system statements: status by claim type

### Empirical

Allowed only for the studied multi-agent setting:

- Dynamic C/P/R findings;
- functional semantic lineage;
- local authority perturbation;
- System Inertia candidates;
- lineage-addressed repair;
- selective recomputation/preservation.

### Mechanism abstraction

Allowed with careful wording:

- content transfer and authority transfer are conceptually separable;
- value/schema preservation may be insufficient to encode epistemic/authorization conditions;
- explicit source, status, scope, validity and re-entry metadata are plausible interface variables.

### Outlook/design hypothesis

Must remain explicitly non-empirical:

- Human <-> AI Process Reality;
- Agent <-> RAG / Database;
- Workflow <-> ERP/CRM;
- Service <-> Service;
- giant-model <-> giant-model protocol;
- Character <-> World Process Reality;
- universal Semantic-Authority Interface Contracts.

## 5. Canonical invariants are design hypotheses outside the studied runtime

The following are useful system-design invariants:

> **semantic transfer != authority transfer**

> **retrieval != re-entry authority**

> **cross-system output != target-system commit authority**

Within the paper's multi-agent evidence, closely related distinctions are observed directly.

At arbitrary external system boundaries, however, these statements remain:

> **design hypotheses / protocol principles**

rather than universally validated empirical laws.

## 6. What a real inter-system validation would require

A future experiment should include at least:

1. independently governed source and target state;
2. explicit source/receiver authority boundaries;
3. a transfer object whose value/schema can remain stable while semantic-authority metadata varies;
4. observable target commit/action rules;
5. provenance and validity tracking;
6. an explicit re-entry/history mechanism;
7. controls distinguishing ordinary data-transfer failure from authority-transfer failure;
8. target-side outcome and process-level auditing;
9. where repair is tested, a bounded cross-system repair contract.

A useful experimental contrast would be:

`value/schema preserved`

versus:

`value/schema + semantic authority conditions preserved`.

Only such evidence could move Inter-System Process Reality from outlook toward supported generalization.

## 7. Why the outlook is still scientifically useful

Removing the inter-system outlook entirely would understate the mechanism.

The experimental object is not tied to one business field or one literal message string.

It concerns:

- what a representation is allowed to count as;
- what it may authorize;
- how long that permission persists;
- how descendants inherit it.

Those variables naturally raise interface questions.

The scientifically defensible move is therefore neither:

> “multi-agent evidence proves all system boundaries”

nor:

> “the result has no relevance outside this exact runtime.”

The correct position is:

> **the first paper establishes the mechanism in multi-agent trajectories and defines inter-system protocol integrity as a testable next research direction.**

## 8. Manuscript consequence

The Discussion should:

- explicitly state that Finance/Supply Chain/Software Engineering are cross-domain, not cross-system replication;
- replace universal-sounding inter-system language with conditional/outlook language;
- label semantic-authority contracts as future engineering hypotheses;
- remove any implication that Character/World, Human-AI or database/workflow examples are evidence;
- specify what future inter-system validation would need.

The Methods should record this generalization boundary so it survives later prose compression.

## Verdict

Attack:

**“Inter-System Process Reality is an overreach from multi-agent evidence.”**

Verdict:

**SUPPORTED IF PRESENTED AS AN EMPIRICAL GENERALIZATION; NOT SUPPORTED IF KEPT AS A CLEARLY BOUNDED, TESTABLE OUTLOOK.**

Canonical first-paper boundary:

> **Multi-Agent is the empirical setting. Inter-System Process Reality is the next-level hypothesis.**

No cross-system prevalence, universality or validated protocol-performance claim is licensed by the present evidence.
