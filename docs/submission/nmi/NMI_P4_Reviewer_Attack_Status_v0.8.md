## Reviewer Attack 07 — Inter-System overreach

Question:

> The experiments are Multi-Agent. Why generalize to arbitrary system boundaries?

### Finding

The attack is valid against any claim that the current experiments empirically establish universal Inter-System Process Reality.

They do not.

The held-out Finance, Supply Chain and Software Engineering cohort establishes domain variation inside the same broad experimental architecture.

Therefore:

> **cross-domain != cross-system validation**

### Three evidence levels

1. **Empirical:** Dynamic C/P/R, functional lineage, perturbation and repair inside the studied multi-agent trajectories.
2. **Mechanism abstraction:** content, authority, scope and temporal permission are separable variables that may remain useful outside the exact domains.
3. **Outlook:** Human-AI, database/workflow, service/service, ERP/CRM, giant-model or Character/World boundaries.

### Canonical first-paper boundary

> **Multi-Agent is the empirical setting. Inter-System Process Reality is the next-level hypothesis.**

The paper may propose future semantic-authority contracts.

It may not claim that they are already validated or generally necessary across all system classes.

## Future validation requirement

A real inter-system experiment should independently enforce source and target state/authority boundaries and compare ordinary value/schema preservation against preservation of semantic-authority conditions such as provenance, epistemic status, scope, validity and re-entry rules.
