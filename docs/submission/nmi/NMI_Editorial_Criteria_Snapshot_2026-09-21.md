## Scope

Nature Machine Intelligence explicitly covers AI, machine learning, robotics and multi-agent systems, and describes interest in the wider effects of machine intelligence across science, society and industry.

## First editorial decision criteria

The official editorial-process page says editors consider:

- the degree to which a manuscript advances understanding of the field;
- soundness of conclusions;
- whether evidence, data and analyses support those conclusions;
- wide relevance to the journal readership.
