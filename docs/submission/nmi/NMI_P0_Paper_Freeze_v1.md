## 3. Frozen main claims

### MC1 — Process / endpoint separation

A stable or plausible endpoint does not establish process-reality integrity.

### MC2 — Dynamic CPR

Information, collaboration/execution and historical-process permissions can change dynamically during multi-Agent execution.

### MC3 — Functional Semantic Lineage

Semantic influence can persist across transformed carriers after source wording disappears.

### MC4 — Local authority challenge exposes downstream dependence

A bounded authority perturbation can reveal which semantic carriers/readers/decisions continue to depend on the challenged lineage.

### MC5 — Persistence is not automatically pathology

Normal inheritance, uncertainty-preserving continuation and independent re-anchoring must be separated from stronger process-reality drift.

### MC6 — Lineage-level repair is feasible in the frozen engineering cases

A semantic object can be content-addressed, its affected closure bounded, and its dependent continuation selectively recomputed while compatible unrelated state is preserved.

### MC7 — Engineering control surfaces are not equivalent

One-shot probe, persistent reader-surface correction and internal lineage repair operate on different process surfaces.
