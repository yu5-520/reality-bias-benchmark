## 1. Purpose

R6 identifies the realized mechanism structure of a qualified case.

R6 is passive by default and consumes frozen natural/R5 evidence.

Entering R6 does not require an additional provider experiment.

## 2. Required map

Where evidence permits:

`source -> transformation -> carrier/support -> pool/exposure -> downstream read -> adoption -> decision/action effect -> post-stimulus persistence`

R6 binds every relation to raw evidence refs.

## 3. Case-level System Inertia

A case-level System Inertia claim may be supported when downstream semantic relations, constraints or action tendencies continue after the relevant direct stimulus/challenge has ceased.

This is a case-level structural claim, not an occurrence-rate claim.

## 4. Domain probability boundary

How often such inertia appears belongs to the natural domain trajectory population.

R6 does not estimate this by repeating one fixed parent.

## 5. Optional local sensitivity

Fixed-parent repetition may measure local conditional recurrence.

Its failure to reproduce one exact phenotype does not invalidate an observed:

- carrier;
- read;
- adoption;
- post-stimulus persistence chain.

## 6. Handoff to R7

R6 hands R7:

- evidence-supported affected lineage;
- selected intervention surface;
- lineage completeness state;
- preserved compatible structure;
- unresolved semantic risks.

R7 entry depends on completeness/repairability, not fixed-parent recurrence.

## 7. Authorization

No provider/evaluator/recovery action is authorized.
